## Company background
X Square Robot (自变量机器人, "Zibianliang" = "independent variable") is a Shenzhen-based embodied-AI startup founded in December 2023 by Wang Qian. Its core asset is the WALL-A / Wall-X family of end-to-end embodied foundation models (10B+ parameters) — a unified perception-planning-control policy — with robot hardware built primarily as an embodiment for the models. The company is among China's best-funded embodied-AI startups: after billion-yuan-level Pre-A/Pre-A+ rounds in late 2024 and a Series A with Meituan as exclusive investor, it raised a ~$100M A+ round led by Alibaba Cloud (2025), then a ~RMB 1bn (~$140M) A++ round led by ByteDance and Sequoia China (HSG) in January 2026, followed by a further round led by SAIC Capital Group (with Meituan Longzhu and Sequoia participating) in February 2026; additional B/C-stage rounds and a multi-billion-RMB valuation were reported by mid-2026 (all third-party, amounts partly conflicting across reports). [S2][S3]

The Quanta X2 wheeled humanoid is its flagship embodiment: a 172 cm dual-arm platform with 20-DoF dexterous hands, marketed as a "robot butler" for homes, hotels, labs and logistics and priced around USD 80k (third-party listing). [S1]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A model-first competitor: strong AI stack + Alibaba Cloud backing, wheeled-humanoid embodiment aimed at services rather than industry. Currently prototype/early stage; threat grows if WALL-A generalisation claims hold and hardware matures. Trajectory: scaling up on the AI side; hardware maturity unproven.
