## Company background
SEER was founded in Shanghai in 2020 by a team of three-time RoboCup world champions and built its business not on robots but on the layer beneath them: standardized robot controllers ("robot brains") and software that let integrators and OEMs build AMRs, forklifts and composite robots without in-house control expertise. It claims 50,000+ controller/brain units installed, 2,100+ customers in 70+ countries across 20+ industries (automotive, semiconductor, pharma), 400+ compatible components, and 500,000+ hours of accumulated real-world robot operating data [S1][S5]. It holds "national specialized & sophisticated little giant" status and has subsidiaries in Hong Kong, Germany and Japan [S1]. In June 2026 SEER listed H-shares on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, branded by Chinese media as the "robot brain first stock" [S2][S3].

Its embodied-AI strategy extends this platform play upward. At CIIF 2024 it released SRC-5000, promoted as the world's first integrated embodied-AI controller (heterogeneous AI chips + coprocessors, whole-body "brain + cerebellum" control breaking the hand-eye-foot coordination bottleneck), and demonstrated a wheeled humanoid built on it that has since been delivered to customer sites for trials [S6]. The X1 wheeled humanoid (working demos at WRC 2025) was upgraded to X1-PRO at CeMAT ASIA 2025, and at WAIC 2026 SEER exhibited X1-PRO alongside the C1-D universal humanoid chassis in two venues; X1-PRO won a 2026 IEEE Robotics & Automation Product Innovation Award [S5][S7][S8].

Go-to-market mirrors the controller business: sell the platform (SRC-5000 brain, C1-D chassis, M4 fleet software, dev toolchains) to any humanoid builder, while offering its own X1-PRO as reference product for logistics/industrial scenarios.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
SEER is strategically the most interesting Tier-2 Chinese player for a would-be EU entrant not because of X1-PRO itself but because it is industrializing the SUPPLIER LAYER of wheeled humanoids: an off-the-shelf certified-grade omni chassis (C1-D: 650×650 mm, 150 kg payload, 800 mm-aisle capable) plus an embodied controller ecosystem. This lowers the entry barrier for every new wheeled-humanoid brand — SEER profits whichever robot wins — and its 50k-unit controller install base plus HKEX capital give it distribution and staying power. Trajectory: scaling up aggressively from AMR infrastructure into embodied infrastructure.
