## Company background
PNDbotics is a Beijing humanoid startup known for the Adam full-size bipedal humanoid, which drew attention for lifelike human-like motion (synced-dance demos, MWC/CES appearances) built on fully modular in-house quasi-direct-drive (QDD) force-controlled actuators [S2]. Adam-U is the company's upper-body derivative: the biped's torso, arms and head on an adjustable lifting pedestal, sold as a stationary data-collection and algorithm-development platform. Partnerships: Noitom (PNLink mocap suit for teleop/retargeting) and Inspire Robotics (RH56-series dexterous hands) form the data-collection ecosystem [S3]. Third-party resellers list in-stock Adam-U units at $45,000 [S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
PNDbotics addresses the research/data-collection segment (vs ARX, Galaxea, AgileX): Adam-U's pitch is human-morphology fidelity — 13-DoF arms with QDD force control plus a 3-DoF waist — for imitation-learning data that transfers to full humanoids. Not a commercial deployment threat, but a notable supplier into the embodied-AI training pipeline and a channel for its actuator technology.
