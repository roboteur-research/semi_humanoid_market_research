## Company background
Richtech is a Las Vegas service-robotics vendor that grew out of Wayne Huang's work on AI control of robotic arms and early COVID-era labor shortages in hospitality ("38% of small businesses reported unfilled job openings"); it validated food-delivery robots with 80+ customers around 2020-21 and now claims more than 450 robot deployments nationwide across hotels, restaurants, casinos, stadiums and offices [S2, vendor-claimed via interview]. Its strategic pillars are Industrial, Commercial and Data; the business model is anchored in RaaS with direct sales where preferred [S2][S5]. Flagship semi-humanoid ADAM (shipping since ~2022) is a dual-armed beverage worker (barista/bartender/boba) deployed at One Kitchen ghost kitchens inside Walmart stores (first installs 2024: Dawsonville GA 06/2024, Rockford IL 08/2024), the botbar boba café (Brooklyn), a Las Vegas Clouffee & Tea café (16,000+ drinks by 06/2025), Golden Knights' arena, Texas Rangers' Globe Life Field and Kennedy Space Center [S5][S6]. Monetization mixes $180k list-price sales, event rentals (from ~$15k/day) and RaaS — a 25-unit, 60-month, $5.25M agreement equating to $3,500/robot/month [S5]. A Jan-2024 non-binding LOI with Ghost Kitchens International targeted 240 ADAM systems; by Oct-2024 this became a binding LOI under which Richtech itself would franchise-operate 20 Walmart-located restaurants in AZ/CO/TX — i.e., Richtech operating restaurants to showcase its own robots [S6].

In October 2025 Richtech unveiled Dex, its first mobile industrial humanoid (Titan AMR base + ADAM-line dual arms + NVIDIA Jetson Thor, Isaac Sim Sim2Real pipeline), shown at NVIDIA GTC DC and CES 2026; as of the December 2025 Robot Report interview, "the industry is still waiting for the first Dex customer announcement" [S2][S3]. Richtech also launched an "American robotics data initiative" intending to license US-collected manipulation data [S3]. Financial reality check: FY2025 revenue was just $5.05M against a ~$322M market cap ("billion-dollar company with $3M in sales" critique at peak), with governance flags — a late 10-Q (05/2026), restatement (06/2026) and a Nasdaq compliance deadline of 2026-11-24 [S1]. A $4M+ deal with Beijing Tongchuang covers China distribution [S1]. Claimed ARCO/ampm convenience-store contract remains unverified [S6, flag].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Richtech matters as the West's most visible hospitality/F&B semi-humanoid vendor and one of the few pure-play public comparables — its filings expose real category economics (FY2024 ADAM leasing revenue $857K; $3,500/mo manipulating-robot RaaS vs $300-800/mo tray-delivery RaaS). Trajectory: scaling from static beverage cells toward mobile industrial manipulation (Dex) on NVIDIA's stack, backed by 450+ deployment sites as a distribution channel. For an EU entrant, Richtech defines the Western price/served-market envelope in F&B ($180k list / $3,500 RaaS) but is financially fragile — high burn, tiny revenue, restatement risk — meaning the segment could be contested by a better-capitalized European player, especially in CE-regulated venues where Richtech has no presence.
