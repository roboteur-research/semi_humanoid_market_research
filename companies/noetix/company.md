## Company background
Noetix Robotics (Beijing Noetix Technology Co., Ltd.; Chinese brand 松延动力, founder Jiang Zheyuan) is a Beijing startup known for its low-cost N2 biped — a podium finisher in China's 2025 humanoid running/combat competitions — and for hyper-realistic "bionic" robot heads with platinum-silicone skin. Its Hobbs line packages a 32-active-DoF expressive bionic head (98% speech-recognition accuracy claimed, <150 ms expression-imitation latency) onto various bodies; the fourth-generation Hobbs W1, launched 16 December 2025, is a wheeled service robot marketed as "China's first bionic service robot with a high-DoF humanoid head". [S1][S2][S3]

Go-to-market is direct plus e-commerce: W1 pre-sales reportedly booked 500+ units on JD.com within days at ~US$73.5k. [S3]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Differentiated on human-likeness and affective interaction rather than manipulation payload — relevant for reception, museums, elder-care companionship rather than industrial tasks. Moderate competitive relevance for an EU industrial entrant, but demonstrates China's speed at commercialising niche service humanoids. Trajectory: scaling up.
