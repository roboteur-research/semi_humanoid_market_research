# HERB (Home Exploring Robot Butler) — CMU

> HERB was CMU's wheeled dual-arm research butler (2006-2017): two 7-DoF Barrett WAM arms with 4-DoF BarrettHands on a Segway RMP 200 base. The workhorse for a decade of manipulation-planning research; retired.

HERB began as "Busboy" (Segway RMP 200 + one Barrett WAM) in 2006, became HERB 1.0 in 2008 and was fully rebuilt as dual-arm HERB 2.0 in 2010 with custom electronics and ~6 h untethered runtime [S1]. Specs (HERB 2.0): 140 cm tall, 127 kg, 65.5×62 cm footprint, 5.4 km/h; 27 DoF (2×7 WAM arms, 2×4 BarrettHands, 2-DoF head, 3-DoF base); sensors included a custom spinning 3D laser, RGB-D and gigabit-Ethernet cameras, tactile pads, force-torque sensors and planar base lasers; compute was three onboard Dell Precision M6500 workstations [S1]. Its cable-driven WAM arms were compliant and backdrivable, making it an early safe-manipulation-around-humans testbed; signature demos included microwaving meals and separating Oreo cookies. Research impact (CHOMP, grasp planning, human-robot handovers) far outlasted the hardware; the Personal Robotics Lab moved to UW ~2017 and HERB was retired. Funding came from Intel and the NSF Quality of Life Technology center [S1][S2].
