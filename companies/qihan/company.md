## Company background
Shenzhen-based Qihan Technology came out of video surveillance and launched the Sanbot cloud-connected service-robot line: Sanbot Elf (IFA Berlin 2016, 90 cm reception robot with gesturing arms), Sanbot King Kong — marketed in the West as Sanbot Max — (WRC 2017/CES 2018, 1.46 m dual-bionic-arm mecanum-wheel service robot), and Sanbot Nano (2017 home robot) [S1]. The pitch was "Robot-as-a-Service" via the Q-Link cloud platform with Western tech partners (IBM Watson AI, Nuance voice, Sony imaging) [S1]. Deployments included Shenzhen Bao'an airport and the Gongbei border checkpoint, plus overseas distribution [S1]. Robotics activity faded around 2019; the Sanbot line is considered dormant/discontinued (discovery-file assessment; no new products or news since) [S2].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Qihan is a pre-boom cautionary datapoint: it internationalized early (IFA/CES, Watson integration) with interaction-first wheeled humanoids, but without real manipulation value the category stalled and the company exited. The Sanbot Max's mecanum base and towing capability foreshadowed today's logistics-oriented semi-humanoids without their AI. Zero current competitive threat; historical reference only.
