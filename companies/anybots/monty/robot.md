# Monty — Anybots

> Monty (2007) was Anybots' human-scale, two-wheel self-balancing teleoperated manipulation robot — a decade-early ancestor of the teleop wheeled humanoid concept (1X EVE, Reflex). One prototype; program shelved 2010 for the QB telepresence pivot.

Monty stood 5'7" (170 cm), weighed 160 lb (72.6 kg), and balanced dynamically on two wheels — a deliberately slim, human-footprint base for office environments [S1][S2]. It carried two arms; the right was a 7-DoF arm ending in a fully articulated 18-DoF human-like hand, teleoperated through VR goggles and a wearable sensor glove, with which operators grasped delicate everyday objects (glassware, dishes) [S2]. The custom self-balancing wheel system later became the core of Anybots' QB telepresence product when the manipulation program was put on hold in 2010 [S2]. Monty is an instructive very-early datapoint: it demonstrated the exact morphology and teleop-first operating model that resurfaced commercially 15 years later, but at 2007 component costs and without learning-based autonomy there was no viable market, and the company retreated to telepresence.
