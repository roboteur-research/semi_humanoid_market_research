## Company background
Robotics X is Tencent's frontier robotics research lab (est. 2018, Shenzhen) led by computer-vision pioneer Zhang Zhengyou. Its lineage runs through legged and wheel-balancing research platforms (Max, Ollie) to the fifth-generation "human-habitat" robot The Five (小五, XiaoWu), revealed September 2024 [S1][S2]. Unusually among Chinese humanoid efforts, the lab explicitly targets eldercare and home assistance rather than factory work, emphasizing safe physical human-robot interaction: large-area tactile skin, force-controlled support of a person's weight, and demos such as therapeutic massage [S2][S3]. A newer demonstration humanoid, Xiao Liu, followed at WAIC July 2026 [S3]. No commercialization has been announced for any of these platforms.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
BORDERLINE inclusion: The Five is a wheel-leg hybrid (four straight telescoping legs ending in wheels), not a classic wheeled-base semi-humanoid. It matters as a signal of where hyperscaler research is heading — eldercare-grade safe physical interaction, whole-body tactile sensing — capabilities commercial semi-humanoids will eventually need. No near-term product threat.
