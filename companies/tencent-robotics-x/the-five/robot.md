# The Five / XiaoWu (小五) — Tencent Robotics X

> BORDERLINE CASE — wheel-leg hybrid, not a classic wheeled semi-humanoid: Tencent Robotics X's fifth-generation "human-habitat" research robot moves on four straight telescoping legs that end in wheels, and is aimed at eldercare/home assistance research. Included for completeness because it is bimanual, non-biped and mostly wheel-borne; it is a lab prototype with no commercialization announced.

The Five (Chinese name 小五, "XiaoWu") is the fifth-generation robot from Tencent's Robotics X lab, revealed September 2024 [S1]. Its signature is the mobility compromise: four straight legs ending in wheels roll efficiently on flat floors and extend/step to clear thresholds and uneven ground — a different formula from both bipeds and torso-on-AGV semi-humanoids [S1][S2]. The design brief is eldercare: variable height, large-area tactile skin for safe physical contact (including supporting a person), and fine force control demonstrated in massage tasks [S2][S3]. It remains a research vehicle — no pricing, availability or deployment beyond lab and demo settings — and Tencent has since shown a newer demonstration humanoid (Xiao Liu, WAIC July 2026) [S3]. For this study The Five is explicitly BORDERLINE: it is tracked as a technology signal (tactile skin, safe pHRI) rather than as a competing product.
