# Vyommitra (व्योममित्र) — ISRO

> A **legless, seated half-humanoid** (head + torso + two arms) built by ISRO's Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre to fly in the Gaganyaan crew capsule on uncrewed test flights: it operates switch panels, monitors/executes life-support (ECLSS) procedures, speaks Hindi and English, recognizes astronauts' faces and reports cabin conditions — replacing animal test subjects. Unveiled January 2020; pre-flight integration began April 2026; its ride, uncrewed **Gaganyaan-1 (G1), is currently targeted Q3 2027** (slipped from the H2 2026 plan; possible further slip to 2028). Not a commercial product — included as India's flagship state semi-humanoid.

## Design & morphology
**Half-humanoid: head, torso and two arms; no legs** — designed to remain fixed in an astronaut seat and lean/reach to console panels [S1][S2]. Female-presenting android face with expressive features. Structure primarily **aluminum alloy** to withstand launch vibration and pressure loads [S1]. Height/weight/DoF: n/a (not officially disclosed; seated form sized to a crew seat).

## Locomotion
None (seated, strapped into crew seat; legless by design — legs deemed unnecessary mass for console operation) [S1][S2].

## Upper body & manipulation
Two arms with hands capable of **switch-panel operations** in the crew module (flipping switches, operating controls per procedure) [S1]. Arm DoF/payload n/a. No tool use beyond console interaction reported.

## Sensing
Vision system with **human face recognition** (identifies crew members); microphones/speech interface; monitors cabin parameters and issues **environmental-pressure warnings** via ECLSS interfaces [S1] (third-party summarizing ISRO statements).

## Actuation & power
n/a (not disclosed); powered from capsule systems for flight.

## Compute & software
Conversational AI in **Hindi and English**; responds to queries, mimics human activity, executes ECLSS and switch procedures; telemetry of physiological-analog and environmental data to ground [S1][S2]. Stack details classified/undisclosed.

## Safety & compliance
Space-qualification testing (vibration, thermal, pressure) in progress since April 2026 as part of G1 integration [S3]. No commercial safety standards applicable.

## Deployment evidence & traction
- Unveiled 22 Jan 2020; multiple public demos (speaking, gestures) at symposia [S1].
- **G1 flight assignment confirmed**; integration/critical-systems tests underway from 28 Apr 2026 [S3].
- Schedule history: Dec 2020 → Dec 2024 → Feb 2025 → Q4 2025 → H2 2026 → **Q3 2027 current target, 2028 risk** (ISRO chairman V. Narayanan) [S3][S4]. This supersedes the discovery note's "H2 2026".

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* Vyommitra is competitively irrelevant as a product but noteworthy on three counts: it validates the minimal "console-operator" semi-humanoid morphology (seated, legless, two arms — maximum capability per kg in a fixed workstation); it showcases India's state-level systems-integration talent pool (VSSC/Kerala) from which commercial ventures recruit; and its repeated schedule slips illustrate how hard human-rated reliability is even for a single robot unit. No threat to EU entrants; possible long-term signal that space agencies (ESA included) may procure semi-humanoid test occupants — a tiny but prestige-heavy niche.
