## Company background
ISRO is India's national space agency. Within the **Gaganyaan human-spaceflight program** (India's first crewed orbital capsule, ~₹20,000 crore), ISRO chose to fly a **half-humanoid robot, Vyommitra** ("space friend": vyoma = space, mitra = friend in Sanskrit), on uncrewed precursor flights instead of animal test subjects — its explicitly stated ethical/technical position [S1]. Vyommitra was developed at the Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC), Thiruvananthapuram, with its Inertial Systems Unit, and unveiled on 22 January 2020 at the Human Spaceflight and Exploration Symposium in Bengaluru [S1].

The robot is a **legless, seated torso+head+two-arm android** (female presentation, aluminum-alloy structure for launch loads) designed to occupy a crew seat, operate **switch panels and ECLSS (environment control & life support) procedures**, speak Hindi and English, recognize crew faces and answer queries — generating flight data on vibration, radiation and cabin environment ahead of human flights [S1][S2]. Schedule: after slips from Dec 2020 → 2024 → 2025 → H2 2026, the uncrewed **Gaganyaan-1 (G1) carrying Vyommitra is now targeted Q3 2027** (possible slip to 2028; crewed flight 2028+). Vyommitra's pre-flight integration and critical-systems testing began 28 April 2026 [S3][S4].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Zero commercial overlap — Vyommitra is a one-off state R&D artifact, not a product. Its market relevance is contextual: (1) it is the highest-profile Indian semi-humanoid and a talent/PR anchor for India's robotics ecosystem (VSSC sits in the same Kerala ecosystem as Genrobotics); (2) it demonstrates the seated, legless half-humanoid form factor for control-console operation — a niche (cockpit/console retrofit automation) occasionally cited in industrial contexts; (3) any EU vendor selling into Indian government/space programs will encounter ISRO's build-domestic preference.
