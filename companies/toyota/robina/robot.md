# Robina (ロビーナ / TPR-Robina) — Toyota Partner Robot

> Historical wheeled guide humanoid (2007–2012+): a 1.2 m, 60 kg "female-styled" Partner Robot that gave guided tours at the Toyota Kaikan Exhibition Hall from 27 Aug 2007 — one of the earliest humanoids in continuous public-facing service. Two drive wheels plus four maneuvering wheels hidden under a skirt; jointed five-finger hands dexterous enough to sign autographs. Ancestor position in the Toyota lineage that led to HSR (2012) and the dual-arm ELEY successor unveiled Dec 2025.

Robina ("Robot as Intelligent Assistant"-styled guide, publicly TPR-Robina) was announced in August 2007 as Toyota's first Partner Robot in real service, escorting visitors around the Toyota Kaikan Exhibition Hall displays from 27 August 2007 [S1][S3]. Against the gesture-only communication robots of its era, Robina demonstrated genuinely articulated hands (autograph signing) and personalized interaction via name-tag reading, while navigating crowds autonomously [S2]. One unit guided tours 2007–2009, returned to the lab, and was retired in 2012 — later donated to the UK Science Museum Group; its twin continued at the Kaikan [S2]. Robina never approached commercialization but seeded Toyota's service-robot line: the "Robina" family concept evolved into the single-arm Human Support Robot (2012, 40+ research institutions, borderline for this study), and in December 2025 Toyota unveiled HSR's dual-arm successor ELEY at WRS 2025 Aichi — returning the lineage squarely to semi-humanoid manipulation [S4]. TRI's Punyo research platform is covered in the US batch.
