## Company background
Lanxin Robotics (杭州蓝芯科技, Hangzhou Lanxin Technology) is a Hangzhou machine-vision and mobile-robotics company whose core asset is the proprietary LX-MRDVS 3D vision-sensing stack ("Mobile Robot Depth Vision System") used for AI-driven navigation, obstacle avoidance, positioning and grasping. Its historical business is vision-guided AMRs and full-factory logistics automation, with named customers including Huawei, BYD and LG across electronics, automotive, battery and packaging industries. [S1, vendor-claimed]

Around 2025 it extended into "humanoid" platforms: the VersaBot line (VB1, VB1-I, VB2) mounts compact dual 6–7-joint arms on its AMR bases — VB1/VB1-I as a dual-arm manipulator on a mast over a wheeled chassis with 0.2–2 m operating height, VB2 as a taller anthropomorphic torso on a wheeled base — all navigated by 3D laser SLAM and the panoramic RGB-D vision system, and tied into Lanxin's MCS/RCS fleet software. Positioning is "software ecosystem + in-house technology" for factory intralogistics and machine-tending rather than standalone hardware sales. [S1][S2, vendor-claimed]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A vision/AMR incumbent moving up into dual-arm mobile manipulation — the most direct Chinese analogue to an EU intralogistics-focused semi-humanoid entrant. Its strength is deployed factory infrastructure (fleet software, 3D vision, blue-chip customers), not humanoid dexterity: current VersaBots carry only 2–5 kg per arm with 2–6 h runtime. Trajectory: incremental industrial scaling; watch its ability to convert existing AMR accounts into dual-arm deployments.
