Zoomlion started its embodied-AI robot program in 2024 and had three humanoid models by August 2025 — two wheeled, one biped — expanding to eight prototypes across four categories by end-2025 [S1][S3]. The wheeled units (torso + dual arms on a wheeled chassis, per official imagery) are validated inside the group's Changsha Smart City complex on machining, internal logistics, loading/unloading, pre-assembly and vision-based quality inspection tasks [S1][S2]. The company built a ~100-workstation humanoid training ground for data collection and runs models on its ZValley cloud subsidiary's platform [S1]. No specs, names or prices for the wheeled models have been published, and there is no indication of external sales; the traction claim ("dozens" in factories, excavator lines) is vendor-reported and amplified by Western press [S2]. Treat as a captive-automation program with productization optionality.
