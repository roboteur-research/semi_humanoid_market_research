## Company background
Zoomlion is one of China's largest construction-machinery groups. It began developing embodied-AI humanoid robots in 2024 and by end-2025 had eight prototype robots across four categories, including two wheeled humanoid models and one biped [S1][S2]. The robots are developed for and validated in Zoomlion's own "Smart City" manufacturing complex in Changsha (12 smart factories, 300+ lines), performing machining support, intra-factory logistics, loading/unloading, pre-assembly and visual quality inspection [S1][S3]. Supporting infrastructure includes a humanoid training ground with nearly 100 workstations for data collection and the "Zhongke Yungu" (中科云谷/ZValley) embodied-intelligence cloud platform for end-to-end models and self-evolution loops [S1].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Zoomlion typifies the Chinese industrial-giant pattern: humanoids as a captive automation program feeding its own factories first (dozens of units deployed), with the option to productize later — the biped Z01 has already appeared at trade fairs (KOMATEK 2026) [S2]. For an EU entrant it is less a competitor for external customers than evidence that large Chinese manufacturers may self-supply wheeled humanoids rather than buy them.
