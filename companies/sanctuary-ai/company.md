## Company background
Sanctuary AI was founded in Vancouver in 2018 by Geordie Rose, Suzanne Gildert and Olivia Norton — the same circle that created quantum-computing pioneer D-Wave and teleoperated-picking company Kindred. From the outset the thesis was cognitive: build "human-like intelligence in general-purpose robots", with the hardware (Phoenix) primarily serving as a high-fidelity human-behaviour data-capture device for its Carbon AI control system. The company iterated unusually fast: Gen 5 was deployed commercially in a Canadian retail back-of-store pilot in January 2023, Gen 6 (2023) made TIME's Best Inventions list, Gen 7 arrived April 2024 (~11 months later), and Gen 8 followed in December 2024, less than 8 months after Gen 7 [S1][S2].

Sanctuary's signature technology is its hydraulic dexterous hand: 21 DoF per hand, miniaturized hydraulic valves, and tactile sensors the company says can detect forces down to ~5 mN — widely regarded as among the most dexterous robot hands built anywhere [S7]. In 2024-2026 the company published a stream of manipulation results (RL control of hydraulic hands, zero-shot in-hand manipulation, NVIDIA Isaac Lab sim-to-real, new touch sensors) that reinforced this positioning [S6].

2024-2026 brought heavy turbulence: co-founder/CTO Suzanne Gildert left in May 2024 (to found consciousness-AI startup Nirvanic), Geordie Rose was removed as CEO in mid-2024, ~30 staff were laid off, and long-time executive James Wells served as interim CEO before leaving in 2026. In June 2026 board chairman Daniel Friedmann (ex-MDA, ex-Carbon Engineering) took over as CEO, explicitly to drive a strategy shift "from the laboratory to the factory floor": commercializing Sanctuary's Physical AI (teleoperation + dexterous-manipulation software) on industrial robotics rather than perfecting its own humanoid — demonstrated June 17 2026 with a "production-ready" automated wire/connector-plugging task for a Tier 1 automotive supplier [S4][S8].

Manufacturing and go-to-market run through strategic partners: Magna International (investor since 2022, second investment plus strategic deployment/contract-manufacturing relationship announced October 2024; Phoenix trials in Magna plants mastering new assembly tasks in <24 h reported late 2025), Microsoft (AI/cloud collaboration), Accenture (Ventures investment + Project Spotlight client access), and Zeon (specialized materials for dexterous robotics) [S5][S6][S9].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Sanctuary is the canonical "dropped the legs" datapoint: after customer feedback that legs were "too frail to support a strong torso", Gen 8 (Dec 2024) put the humanoid torso on a small wheeled base optimized for teleop data capture [S1]. Its hydraulic 21-DoF hands remain the dexterity benchmark competitors are measured against. However, the company is now pivoting from selling humanoids to selling Physical AI software/teleoperation for industrial automation, making it less a direct hardware competitor and more an AI-stack rival (and a cautionary tale about hardware capital intensity) for any new EU entrant. Its deep automotive channel via Magna is the asset to watch.
