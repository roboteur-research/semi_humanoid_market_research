## Company background
Beyond Imagination is Harry Kloor's and Ray Kurzweil's humanoid venture, best known for the Beomni 1.0 VR-teleoperated humanoid shown at CES 2022 (telemedicine demos, "world's first general-purpose humanoid" marketing) and an August 2022 LOI/order commitment for at least 1,000 robots over five years from SELF Labs for automated off-grid agricultural "grow boxes" [S1][S4]. After several quiet years, it re-emerged in May 2025 with a striking claim: a $100M Series B at a $500M valuation from a single investor, Gauntlet Ventures (Dallas) — covered by Fast Company/Yahoo but with little diligence detail public [S2][S3]. In 2026 it announced a manufacturing partnership with Dreamtech (Korean electronics manufacturer) focused first on "perioperative cobots" — robots for operating-room support — with deployment targeted about two years after funding close, per its Wefunder updates (May 2026) [S5].

**Status (2026): active**, pivoted toward medical/OR and pharma/semiconductor precision markets, with Aura OS as a software play; but there is no evidence of fleet deployments, and the 1,000-robot agriculture LOI has produced no visible follow-through.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Beomni's architecture (dexterous 12-DoF-per-arm torso mountable on wheeled bases or workstations, teleop-first with an "AI Brain" learning layer) is squarely semi-humanoid, and the company's valuation claim makes headlines. Treat it as a high-variance, celebrity-backed outlier: minimal verified commercial traction since 2022, unusual single-investor financing, and repeated market pivots (telemedicine → agriculture → OR/pharma). Threat to an EU entrant: low, but its OR/perioperative niche is one wheeled semi-humanoids could credibly serve.
