# Beomni (1.0) — Beyond Imagination

> Beomni is a VR-teleoperated humanoid torso — up to ~1.8 m tall with 12-DoF arms and dexterous hands — that mounts on wheeled mobile bases or fixed workstations, driven by the "Beomni AI Brain" that learns skills from its human pilots. Unveiled at CES 2022 with telemedicine demos, it drew a 1,000-robot agriculture LOI (SELF Labs, 2022) and resurfaced in 2025-26 with a $100M Series B at a $500M valuation (sole investor Gauntlet Ventures) and a Dreamtech manufacturing deal targeting operating-room "perioperative cobots". Competitive significance is mostly cautionary: maximal claims, minimal verified deployment.

Form factor: humanoid upper body with two 12-DoF arms and dexterous hands, torso mountable on wheeled mobile bases (CES demo configuration) or fixed workstations; height up to ~1.8 m [S3]. Operation blends VR teleoperation by remote human "pilots" with the multimodal "Beomni AI Brain", which integrates sensory input and learns from repeated human operation toward increasing autonomy — Kurzweil frames it as letting people "multiply their own body n-fold" [S2][S3]. Aura, a companion "universal OS", targets human-robot-machine orchestration in smart factories [S3]. Specs beyond the above (DoF total, payload, battery, sensors, compute) were never published; all capability claims are vendor-claimed. Traction: 2022 SELF Labs commitment for ≥1,000 robots over five years for automated agriculture (no visible follow-through), 2025 $100M single-investor Series B, 2026 Dreamtech (KR) manufacturing partnership for perioperative cobots [S1][S4][S5]. No independent deployment evidence exists as of Aug 2026 — a striking gap for a company claiming a $500M valuation.
