## Company background
Zerith (零次方, "zeroth power") is a 2025-vintage embodied-AI startup founded by a post-2000s team from Tsinghua's AI & Robot Intelligence Lab, with big-tech alumni (ByteDance, Baidu). Rather than chase general-purpose humanoids, it picked one brutal service loop — hotel housekeeping — and built the H1 wheeled humanoid plus the Zerith-V0 specialized-scenario foundation model around it: cleaning showers/toilets/sinks, vacuuming, and restocking amenities in real hotel rooms, with claims of 5-hour error-free multi-task runs ("China's first ultra-long-sequence multi-task coherent operation in specialized scenarios") [third-party/vendor-claimed].

Commercially it moved unusually fast for its vintage: >¥100M raised across rapid angel extensions, tens of millions of yuan in orders (intention orders near ¥100M), 20+ venue deployments, claimed production ramp toward 100 units/month and a 500-unit multi-model delivery target for year-end [third-party — treat ramp targets as vendor-claimed]. Marketing claims ~50% hotel-cleaning cost reduction [vendor-claimed, discovery notes].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Zerith is the strongest Chinese example of the "one vertical, closed loop" strategy in hospitality — the same beachhead (hotel housekeeping) that several Western startups target. Its ROS 2-based H1 with height-adjust 0-2.2 m workspace and tactile sensing is purpose-shaped for bathrooms and beds, not showpieces. If its unit economics hold, it will set the reference price/perfomance for hotel semi-humanoids across Asia and pressure EU entrants in hospitality RaaS.
