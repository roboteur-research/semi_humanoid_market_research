## Company background
Invento Robotics built India's best-known indigenous service humanoid, **Mitra** ("friend" in Hindi/Sanskrit) — a 1.5 m, ~50 kg fiberglass wheeled robot with a chest touchscreen, face recognition and multilingual speech (English, Hindi, Kannada), fully designed and assembled in Bengaluru [S1][S2]. Its breakout moment came at the **Global Entrepreneurship Summit, Hyderabad, November 2017**, when Mitra greeted PM Narendra Modi and Ivanka Trump on stage — a viral moment that made it the face of Indian robotics [S1]. Follow-on models **Mitra 2** (hospitality/workplace) and **Mitra 3** added better interaction and CRM integration.

Deployments 2017-2021 spanned banks (HDFC/Canara pilots), hotels, malls, cinema halls, airports and hospitals; during COVID-19 Mitra variants connected isolated patients with families (e.g. Yatharth Super Speciality Hospital, Noida, 2020, widely covered) and screened visitors [S1][S3]. The company stayed small — total funding only ~$1.3M — and struggled with scale, unit economics and even logistics (a 2020 US shipment stuck at Bengaluru airport prompted public complaints to the government [S4]). By late 2024 Tracxn recorded 3 employees and flags the company as no longer active in hardware; Viswanathan's public activity has shifted to AI/ML training bootcamps and AGI-flavored software ventures [S3][S5]. Rental of existing Mitra units for events continues via mitraai.com.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Historically important as proof that a low-cost (sub-$15K class) service semi-humanoid could be built and deployed in India at small scale, and as a national symbol (the Modi-Ivanka moment). Commercially it is a cautionary tale: gesture-class arms without manipulation, thin capital and event/reception use cases did not sustain a business. Current activity: residual/rental; effectively exited hardware. Threat level: none — relevance is market-history and India-market-entry insight only.
