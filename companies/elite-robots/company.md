## Company background
Elite Robots is one of China's established collaborative-robot manufacturers: founded 2016 by Beihang robotics PhDs, with nearly 10,000 cobots deployed across 50+ countries through the EC and CS (up to 30 kg) series [S2][S3]. It sells globally through distributors and has a decade of industrial controls, ±0.02–0.03 mm-class arm repeatability engineering, and safety-certified cobot practice — the classic incumbent asset base.

In May 2026 it entered the wheeled-humanoid category: Centaur-G1 (unveiled 21 May 2026, Hangzhou) puts a dual-7-DoF-arm humanoid torso on a mecanum omnidirectional base, driven by the simultaneously upgraded "Yuanqi Primo" embodied-AI foundation platform (multimodal perception, world modeling, reinforcement learning, end-to-end decisioning) under a stated "one brain, multiple forms" strategy — the same brain across cobots, composites and humanoids [S4].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Elite Robots is the archetype of the cobot-incumbent-enters-humanoids pattern (parallel to JAKA, Dobot, Rokae): it brings proven arm supply chains, industrial-grade repeatability (Centaur-G1 claims ±0.1 mm dual-arm), existing factory customers, and international distribution — advantages pure-play humanoid startups lack. Its humanoid is still in development (2026 target launch per third-party trackers), so threat is prospective rather than proven; but for industrial precision tasks (inspection, machine tending, assembly), an incumbent with 10k arms in the field converting its installed base is a serious mid-term competitor, including in Europe where Elite already sells cobots.
