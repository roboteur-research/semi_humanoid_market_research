# KIKI (КИКИ) — Alfa Robotics

> KIKI was a Russian wheeled promo/service semi-humanoid from Alfa Robotics (an AlfaLED division): 17 DoF, rotating neck and torso, expressive face and gestures, touch-sensitive body zones, self-learning conversational neural network, able to take orders and accept payments. Marketed for retail promotion and events in the late 2010s-2020; the company appears defunct or dormant post-2022. Competitively irrelevant today; included as a regional market-history datapoint (the surviving Russian player is Promobot).

Design (all third-party [S1][S2]): mobile wheeled base (concealed, skirt-style), humanoid torso with two gesturing arms, expressive head; 17 DoF total; neck and body rotation; touch-sensitive zones on the body; conversational AI described as a "self-learning neural network"; functions included placing orders, taking payments and conversing with clients — "Kiki's main task is to attract attention". No dimensions, payload, battery, sensor or compute details were ever published; the arms were gestural, not for manipulation (estimated from promo imagery). Deployment evidence: promotional deployments at Russian events/retail claimed [S2]; no unit counts or named customers found. Post-2022, alfarobotics.ru no longer mentions robots at all [S3].
