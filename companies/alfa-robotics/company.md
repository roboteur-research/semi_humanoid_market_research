## Company background
Alfa Robotics was a division of Russian company AlfaLED focused on production, sale and leasing of commercial promo/service robots, with the interactive mobile promoter robot KIKI as its core product [S1][S2]. KIKI is a wheeled, touchscreen-equipped humanoid-torso robot with 17 DoF, expressive face/gestures and conversational AI, aimed at retail promotion, order-taking and payments [S2].

Post-2022 status: effectively defunct or dormant. As of 2026 the alfarobotics.ru domain no longer presents a robotics catalog — it serves an unrelated editorial blog ("NextJob") about AI and the labor market, with no mention of KIKI or any robot products [S3]. No KIKI news, sales or deployments could be found after ~2021. Confidence: estimated (absence of evidence plus repurposed domain).

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Historical/regional datapoint only: a pre-war Russian commercial attempt at a gesturing service semi-humanoid, now apparently gone — in contrast to compatriot Promobot, which survived and exports. No competitive relevance to an EU entrant.
