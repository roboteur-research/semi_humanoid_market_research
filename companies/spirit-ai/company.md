## Company background
Spirit AI (千寻智能, "Qianxun") pairs a hardware veteran (Han Fengtao, who built ROKAE into one of China's main cobot makers) with a leading embodied-AI academic (Gao Yang, Tsinghua/Berkeley) — a "brain + body" founding combination the Chinese press treats as a template for the sector. Mission slogan: "create the next generation of intelligent workforce"; stated vision of putting robots in the hands of 10% of the world's population within 10 years [S1].

The company moved extremely fast: founded January 2024, first commercial-grade robot Moz1 released June 2025 with a WAIC 2025 debut, and by February 2026 cumulative funding exceeded RMB 4.5 billion across ~4 rounds in three months at a valuation approaching RMB 20 billion. The Pre-A round (RMB 528M) is notable for including Huawei's Hubble investment arm — Hubble's first-ever humanoid robotics bet (1.4323% stake), widely read as Huawei signalling into embodied AI [S2][S3].

Technology strategy is full-stack: proprietary high-power-density integrated force-control joints (claimed "world's highest power density"), whole-body-control (WBC) algorithms with zero-latency whole-body teleoperation for data collection, and the Spirit v1 VLA trained via a three-stage pyramid (10,000+ h pre-training data → imitation-learning fine-tuning (~24 h/task) → RL post-training to >95% task success, plus chain-of-thought task reasoning) [S4][S5].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Spirit AI holds one of the strongest deployment references in the entire Chinese field: its "Xiaomo" (小墨) robots work on a CATL battery-pack line (Zhongzhou base) doing end-of-line connector insertion for EOL/DCR testing at ~99% insertion success — promoted as the world's first scaled humanoid-robot deployment on a production line [S6]. Combined with Huawei-affiliated capital, force-control hardware IP, and a credible VLA program, it is a fast-rising tier-1 competitor. Trajectory: scaling up aggressively from office/demo pilots into EV-battery manufacturing.
