## Company background
Franka Emika's Panda (2017) became the world's most widely used research cobot arm thanks
to joint torque sensing in all 7 joints, a 1 kHz real-time control interface (FCI) and
accessible pricing; thousands of labs use Panda/FR3 arms, and derivative platforms
(including PRIN Lab's dual-Panda mobile manipulator in Japan and countless bimanual rigs)
build on them. After financial difficulties and insolvency proceedings in 2023 the company
was acquired by Agile Robots SE and renamed Franka Robotics; the research product line
continued. [S1][S2]

In 2025 Franka moved up the integration chain from arms to reference platforms: the
stationary **FR3 Duo** (dual-arm torso mount) and the **Mobile FR3 Duo** (dual FR3 on the
omnidirectional "Tactile Mobile Robot" base), positioned as ready-made embodied-AI /
mobile-manipulation research platforms — a German counterpart to Chinese data-collection
rigs (ARX, AgileX, Galaxea). [S3]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Franka supplies the arms for a large share of the world's bimanual research; its Duo
reference platforms lower friction for European labs building semi-humanoid systems, and
its Agile Robots ownership ties it into a larger Munich robotics group with its own
humanoid ambitions. For a German entrant, Franka is both a potential component supplier
(arms, FCI software stack) and a channel into the research segment.
