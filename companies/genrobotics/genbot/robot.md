# GenBot — Genrobotics

> A rare **tracked** semi-humanoid: torso with dual arms and sensor head (depth camera + 3D lidar beacon) mounted on a rubberized quad-track base with adaptive multi-terrain suspension, targeting industrial logistics, autonomous security patrols and inspection in harsh off-road environments. Vendor targets: 18 DoF, up to 200 kg payload, torso extension to 2.5 m working height, 8 h battery, 3 km/h off-road, 5G/satellite connectivity. Status: pilot production of the core mobility platform with field testing; dual-arm manipulation and non-GPS AI navigation are Phase-3 roadmap items, not yet demonstrated. Competitively interesting as the outdoor/rough-terrain counterpoint to indoor wheeled semi-humanoids.

## Design & morphology
Semi-humanoid torso (yellow industrial styling) with two arms and an articulated sensor head, on a **tracked chassis with four rubberized track units** and adaptive suspension (renders show independently articulating track bogies) [S1]. **18 DoF total** (breakdown not disclosed). **Torso lift: extends working height up to 2.5 m** via articulated/linkage torso (renders show a scissor-type raised torso on boom) [S1]. Height/weight n/a (not disclosed).

## Locomotion
**Rubberized tracks, 3 km/h off-road**; adaptive multi-terrain suspension for uneven ground; high-speed autonomous navigation with real-time 3-D mapping claimed [S1] (vendor-claimed; field-testing stage).

## Upper body & manipulation
Dual arms with heavy grippers (renders show hydraulic-look cylinders and 2/3-finger industrial claws). **Payload up to 200 kg** (platform-level; per-arm rating n/a) [S1]. "Dual-arm manipulation ensures precise handling in unstructured environments" is a **Phase-3 roadmap capability** — treat as planned, not demonstrated [S1]. Modular hot-swappable components / tool-payload integration emphasized ("rapid deployment and effortless integration of diverse tools and payloads") [S1].

## Sensing
**3D lidar + depth cameras** for perception and real-time 3-D mapping; non-GPS operation planned (Phase 3) [S1]. Additional sensors n/a.

## Actuation & power
**8 h battery under standard payload**; battery-monitoring system highlighted; actuator technology n/a (renders suggest electro-hydraulic/linear elements in arms — estimated from imagery) [S1].

## Compute & software
AI-driven navigation with smart path planning (Phase 3), real-time 3-D mapping; **5G/satellite-ready** connectivity for remote operations [S1]. Compute hardware, SDK, autonomy level n/a.

## Deployment evidence & traction
None commercial. Vendor states **"Pilot Production of Core Mobility Platform and Integrated Field Testing"** as current stage [S1] (vendor-claimed; no third-party sightings found). Credibility rests on Genrobotics' Bandicoot track record: sewer robots deployed in 22 Indian states + 4 countries [S2].

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* GenBot addresses a genuinely underserved slot — heavy-payload (200 kg-class) semi-humanoid manipulation on rough terrain — where it would compete with tracked EOD robots and teleoperated demolition machines more than with indoor wheeled humanoids. Strengths: harsh-environment DNA from sewer robotics, Indian government procurement channels, differentiated tracked morphology. Weaknesses: sub-$10M funding for a very hard platform, manipulation still on the roadmap, no third-party validation of any spec, and the 18 DoF / 200 kg / 2.5 m combination suggests slow hydraulic-style motion rather than dexterous work. Threat to a new EU entrant: low in core indoor segments; worth monitoring if the EU entrant targets construction/outdoor logistics, where GenBot's price point from India could undercut.
