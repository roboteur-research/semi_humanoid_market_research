## Company background
Enchanted Tools was founded in Paris in October 2021 by Jérôme Monceaux, the co-founder of Aldebaran Robotics who co-created Nao and Pepper, together with Samuel Benveniste (healthcare-robotics researcher, ex-Broca Living Lab/CEN STIMCO). The company raised a €15M seed round at founding in February 2022 — reported as the largest early-stage robotics investment in French history — and unveiled its first robot character, Miroki, on 29 November 2022, followed by the female character Miroka at VivaTech in June 2023 [S3][S4][S7]. The founding thesis fuses animation-film character design (a Gaumont partnership produced an animated launch film) with service robotics: robots as "characters" people accept emotionally, built on a technically unusual ball-balancing (ballbot) base [S4][S5].

Strategy and manufacturing: robots are designed and assembled in Paris. An urban factory in the 11th arrondissement was inaugurated at the end of 2024, an industrial production line was set up in early 2026, and the commercial "V1" Mirokai launched on 16 June 2026 at VivaTech [S6]. Production was running "in the dozens" as of March 2026, with a stated ramp to hundreds in 2026 and thousands in 2027 [S8]; the long-term ambition stated since 2023 is 100,000 robots in 10 years [S5]. Enchanted Tools works with an ecosystem of French partners — Pollen Robotics (robot arm development) and Spoon (character design) — and mutualizes R&D/factory investment to grow the French robotics industry [S1].

Go-to-market is direct plus local deployment partners in the USA, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Peru and Saudi Arabia, with a five-phase deployment methodology (needs assessment → multi-site extension) [S2]. Pricing is hybrid: ~€50k initial purchase plus a monthly software/service subscription of several hundred euros, with a stated evolution toward full RaaS [S8]. In Japan, Kanematsu Corporation signed a domestic sales agreement on 8 September 2025 (with Nagakura Mfg. Co. as official distributor and maintenance partner), targeting customer service, retail, offices, medical care, nursing care and education; the Japanese debut was at iREX 2025 (3-6 Dec, Tokyo Big Sight) [S9]. US expansion began in 2025 (CES 2025 announcement), with pilots at Mather Institute (Evanston, IL) and Live Oak Adult Day Services (San Jose, CA) [S2][S10].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Enchanted Tools is Europe's most visible social/service semi-humanoid company and the only market player shipping a ballbot-based humanoid. It occupies a differentiated niche — emotional engagement, hospitality and care — rather than industrial logistics, validated by real paid pilots (AP-HP hospitals, Institut du Cancer de Montpellier, Lyon-Saint Exupéry Airport with VINCI Airports, an 11-robot APREH deal, US senior-care pilots) and distribution in Japan. Trajectory is scaling up: commercial V1 (June 2026), Paris production line, 2026 target of hundreds of units. Risks: heavy losses (€10.1M in 2024) against a disclosed funding base of only ~€15-33M, an unproven RaaS economics model at ~€50k+subscription, and a deliberately non-industrial positioning that caps payload-driven use cases [S6][S7][S8].
