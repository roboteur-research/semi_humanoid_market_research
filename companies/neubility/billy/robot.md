# Billy (빌리) — Neubility

> Billy is Neubility's announced "half-human, half-vehicle" semi-humanoid: a dual-armed upper body with a human-like head and two five-fingered hands mounted on a four-wheeled cargo-vehicle base with modular shelved compartments. It targets delivery, logistics, manufacturing and facility operations, extending Neubility's proven Neubie delivery-robot business into manipulation. Competitively it matters as one of three early-2026 Korean launches (with T-Robotics TR Works and I.L ILBOT) that signal a deliberate Korean industry pivot to wheeled semi-humanoids over bipeds.

Design: humanoid upper body (head + two arms with five-fingered hands) whose torso and arms rotate 360°, on a four-wheeled vehicle lower body with a rear cargo compartment and modular opening/closing shelves; intended to press door buttons, interact with existing shelves/facilities without infrastructure modification, and pick-and-load items into its own cargo bay, indoors and outdoors [S1][S2] (all vendor-claimed via press). No DoF, payload, battery, compute or sensing specs have been disclosed. VP Kang Ki-hyuk: "Billy was designed with a vehicle base to ensure superior mobility... On flat surfaces such as factory floors or roads, wheels enable faster and longer-distance travel" [S2]. Given Neubility's Neubie heritage, camera/V-SLAM-based navigation is plausible but unconfirmed (estimated). No deployment evidence exists yet; nearest traction proxy is Neubility's existing RaaS fleet and corporate partners (SK Telecom, Samsung Welstory, 7-Eleven) [S3].
