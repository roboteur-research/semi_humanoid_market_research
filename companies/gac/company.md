## Company background
GAC is one of China's big state-backed automakers. Its embodied-AI robot program sits in the GAC R&D Center and reached its third generation in December 2024 with GoMate, a variable wheel-leg humanoid that reuses group assets: the autonomous-driving perception/decision stack and GAC's all-solid-state battery technology [S1][S2]. The roadmap announced at launch: pilot demonstrations in select industries during 2025, small-volume production from 2026, then scaling — initially for inspection on GAC's own production lines, later security, healthcare, auto aftermarket (e.g. charging/service robots), logistics and education [S1][S2].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
GAC exemplifies automaker entry into humanoids with a distinctive variable-height wheel-leg architecture (4-wheel stable mode / 2-wheel tall balancing mode) rather than a torso-on-AGV. BORDERLINE for this study's wheeled-semi-humanoid scope. Like Zoomlion, first customer is its own factories; external productization is aspirational. Its solid-state-battery and AD-stack reuse could make it cost-competitive if it ever ships at volume.
