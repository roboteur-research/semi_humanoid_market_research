GoMate, unveiled 26 December 2024, is GAC's third-generation embodied robot and its first with the variable wheel-leg concept: it drops onto four wheels for stability and low energy consumption, or rises onto two wheels to reach human working height [S1]. GAC claims the design cuts energy use by over 80% versus comparable humanoids, and fits the robot with the group's all-solid-state batteries for ~6 h runtime [S1]. Perception and navigation reuse GAC's automotive AD stack [S2]. The announced roadmap — 2025 industry pilots, 2026 small-volume production starting with inspection duties in GAC's own plants — makes it primarily a captive-automation play for now; no pricing or external customers are public. Included as BORDERLINE: its two-wheel mode is a balancing wheel-leg architecture rather than a statically stable wheeled base.
