## Company background
Galaxea AI was founded in September 2023 by Gao Jiyang, a computer-vision PhD (USC) with autonomous-driving pedigree at Waymo and Momenta, together with Tsinghua professor Zhao Xing. The company's thesis is "one brain, multiple forms" (一脑多形): a full-stack embodied-AI platform spanning self-developed hardware (arms, grippers, steering-wheel chassis modules), end-to-end VLA models, and deployment tooling [S1][S2]. Its R1 family of three-wheeled omnidirectional dual-arm robots (R1, R1 Pro, R1 Lite) shipped from 2024 primarily into the research and data-collection market — the "ALOHA-class" segment upgraded to an integrated full-size platform [S3][S4].

Funding escalated rapidly through 2025-26: Ant Group led the A round (~RMB 300M), Meituan and Today Capital led later A-series tranches (> $100M), and B/B+ rounds (~RMB 1B and ~RMB 2B) reportedly took the valuation to ~RMB 20B, making Galaxea one of the top-valued Chinese embodied-AI startups [S2] (figures third-party press, treat with caution). The company sells internationally — US reseller Robotopian lists R1 Pro at $69,999 [S4] — and publishes unusually complete public hardware documentation (docs.galaxea-ai.com), lowering integration friction for research buyers.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Galaxea owns the research/data-collection beachhead of the wheeled semi-humanoid market: transparent docs, SDK, VR teleop, and force-controlled grippers make it the default "buy a platform, collect demonstrations, train VLAs" vendor from China, competing directly with ARX/AgileX below it and AgiBot above it. Every lab that standardizes on R1 hardware becomes a future channel for its commercial robots. For an EU entrant targeting research/university sales, Galaxea is the most direct Chinese competitor on price, documentation quality and availability. Trajectory: scaling up aggressively, moving from research into commercial operations.
