## Company background
AGIRobots is a Nagoya deep-tech startup that grew out of a well-followed Japanese technical blog on AGI×robotics started in 2019, incorporating as AGIRobots株式会社 on 17 October 2024 — the "education/blog community turned maker" path. Its pitch is a "pure Japanese-made, fully in-house" (純国産・完全内製) semi-humanoid stack: hardware, QDD actuators, and AI models all domestic, tuned to Japanese manufacturing-site standards. [S1][S2]

On 2 March 2026 it formally unveiled the AGIRobots Worker, a wheeled semi-humanoid (車輪型セミヒューマノイド) with a rising/lowering torso for factory material handling, alongside a "data collection factory" concept: robots deployed for PoCs double as continuous real-world data harvesters for Physical AI model training. Teleoperation (smartphone/VR) feeds imitation learning from skilled workers. Factory PoCs are scheduled for FY2026 with partner recruitment underway, in-house QDD motor production targeted FY2026, and a live demo booked for Robot Technology Japan 2026. [S1][S3]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
AGIRobots is notable as the company that pushed the word セミヒューマノイド ("semi-humanoid") into Japanese trade-press vocabulary and as the first purely domestic startup building a Chinese-style wheeled worker robot (QDD, swerve drive, teleop-to-imitation-learning) for Japanese factories. It is tiny and unproven — no funding disclosures, no deployments, 4kg arm payload — but it is a leading indicator of Japanese domestic entry into the category and a possible acquisition/partnership target for larger Japanese industrials wanting a fast entry.
