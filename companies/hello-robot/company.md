## Background
Hello Robot's Stretch is the deliberate minimalist counterpoint to humanoids: one
telescoping arm on a lift column over a compact diff-drive base, ~$25k, hundreds of
units in research labs and pioneering home-assistance use by people with disabilities
(notably quadriplegic user Henry Evans). Founders' lineage runs through MIT Domo, Meka
Robotics and Google. It is included in this study as the archetype of the
"minimum-viable mobile manipulator" design philosophy that semi-humanoid makers compete
against on cost. [S1][S2]
