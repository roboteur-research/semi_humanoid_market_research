## Company background
Elu.AI — operating the robotics brand INFIFORCE (原力无限, "unlimited force") under the ELU.AI Technology Group — is a Hangzhou embodied-AI company whose stated philosophy is "One Brain, Multiple Bodies, Multiple Scenarios": a proprietary Hyper-VLA multimodal vision-language-action model intended to drive multiple robot embodiments across industrial and commercial scenarios. [S2, third-party]

Its first showcased embodiment, the AstroDroid AD-01 wheeled humanoid, was unveiled on 24 September 2025 at Alibaba's APSARA (云栖) Conference in Hangzhou: a 170 cm dual-arm robot with 11-DoF dexterous hands on a wheeled AMR base with an articulated pitch linkage, targeting ~200 ms end-to-end response through cloud-edge collaborative computing. The company positions pilots in manufacturing, logistics and retail; status remains lab/demo with pricing on inquiry (~$100k per third-party listing). [S1][S2]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A model-first Hangzhou startup in the Alibaba-ecosystem orbit (APSARA debut), competing on VLA latency and cloud-edge architecture rather than hardware payload. Early-stage and opaque — no funding or customer disclosures — so near-term threat is low, but it adds to the density of Chinese wheeled-humanoid entrants converging on the $100k industrial/retail bracket. Trajectory: too early to call.
