## Company background
Zhongke Huiling was founded in Beijing in August 2023 by Zhang Zhengtao, a Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) Institute of Automation researcher — the "CAS" in CASBOT reflects this lineage. The founding team claims 20+ years in intelligent robotics, precision assembly, visual servoing and robot skill learning, including CAS flagship projects such as micro-target precision assembly and the C919 airliner high-lock-nut compliant assembly robot [S1][S2]. The consumer-facing brand 灵宝CASBOT launched in October 2024; the first product, the full-size bipedal CASBOT 01, followed on 13 Nov 2024 with small-batch production planned for 2025 [S2][S3].

Lenovo is the anchor backer: Lenovo Capital led the March 2024 angel round, CASBOT is a first-batch partner in Lenovo's "Fire Seed Plan", and CASBOT robots perform dual-arm/dual-hand force-position hybrid assembly tasks on a Lenovo notebook production line [S1][S4]. A February 2025 angel+ round took cumulative early funding above RMB 100m [S4][S5].

The product strategy is a broad modular family on a shared technology base ("skeleton/shell decoupled" construction that cuts assembly time and cost, hierarchical end-to-end "Embodied Brain" models, real-virtual hybrid training): bipeds for interaction/commercial service (01, 02, 02 Lite, Mini) and wheeled dual-arm machines for industrial work (W1 at WAIC 2025, W2 in 2026) [S3][S6][S7]. Go-to-market is unusually consumer-visible for the sector: CASBOT runs a JD.com self-operated flagship store, where CASBOT 02 listed at ¥328,800 in June 2025 and took its first orders within 48 hours [S6][S8].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
IMPORTANT CORRECTION to the discovery sweep: CASBOT 02 is a BIPED, not a wheeled robot — the company's actual semi-humanoids (wheeled dual-arm) are CASBOT W1/W2, which pair a multi-stage lifting chassis with humanoid 7-DoF arms and sub-millimeter manipulation for retail, sorting/logistics, packaging QC and factory handling. CASBOT matters as a CAS-pedigree, Lenovo-backed player that already has robots doing paid assembly work on a real electronics line and is one of the first to retail full-size humanoids through mainstream e-commerce — a signal of how fast Chinese vendors are commoditizing distribution. Trajectory: scaling up, with an aggressive multi-form-factor roadmap.
