## Company background
TOSY Robotics JSC was Vietnam's robotics pioneer of the late 2000s, best known for TOPIO, a bipedal table-tennis-playing humanoid shown at international fairs, and for consumer toys. TOPIO Dio, initiated November 2008 and unveiled at AUTOMATICA 2010 (Munich), was its wheeled service spin-off — "Vietnam's first service robot" — a 125 cm, 45 kg, 28-DoF dual-arm waiter/bartender on a 3-wheel omnidirectional platform [S1][S2]. After the early-2010s publicity wave TOSY shifted to toys (mRobo, promoted with Justin Bieber at CES 2012) and its humanoid line went quiet; no TOPIO Dio commercialization or deployments are documented. The robotics business is effectively defunct today.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Historical curiosity only: an early (2010) low-cost hospitality semi-humanoid from an emerging-market player that never reached market. Useful as evidence that the waiter/bartender use case for wheeled humanoids was attempted — and failed commercially — 15 years before the current wave.
