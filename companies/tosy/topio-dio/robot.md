# TOPIO Dio — TOSY

> TOPIO Dio was TOSY's 2010 wheeled hospitality semi-humanoid — "Vietnam's first service robot": 125 cm, 45 kg, 28 DoF, dual arms, 3-wheel omnidirectional platform, designed to work as a restaurant waiter and cocktail bartender, operated remotely over Wi-Fi. Unveiled at AUTOMATICA 2010, it never shipped and TOSY's robotics business faded; it is a defunct early datapoint showing the hospitality semi-humanoid concept predates the current wave by 15 years.

Specs (third-party [S1][S2]): height 125 cm, weight 45 kg, 28 DoF total (per-limb breakdown not published); 3-wheel omnidirectional mobile platform; 48 V 20 Ah battery (~0.96 kWh); servo-motor actuation; two cameras for 3D vision, ultrasonic obstacle detection, speaker/microphone; remote operation over wireless internet (teleoperated rather than autonomous). Deployment evidence: trade-show demos only (AUTOMATICA 2010); no customers, no production run. TOSY subsequently pivoted to toys and the humanoid program ended (estimated from absence of any post-2012 activity).
