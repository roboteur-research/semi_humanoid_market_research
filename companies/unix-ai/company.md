## Company background
UniX AI is the highest-profile "Gen-Z founder" story in Chinese embodied AI: founder Yang Fengyu started the company while a Yale PhD student working on tactile perception, and assembled a young Yale/SJTU research team in Suzhou. The company's original technical calling card is Unitouch, claimed as the world's first visual-tactile multimodal large model, which lets its robots discriminate fabrics, handle tofu without crushing it, and insert charging cables — tactile-heavy demos that went viral on CCTV in August 2024 [S2].

Its strategy is unusual among Chinese humanoid startups: consumer-first (C-end). The first robot Wanda (万达/"旺达") was unveiled August 2024 doing laundry, dishwashing and burger-making; Wanda 2.0 opened pre-sales on 25 December 2024 from RMB 88,000 — marketed as the industry's first "ten-thousand-yuan-class" full-size humanoid — with mass-production deliveries from spring 2025 (reported variously as February and 8 May 2025) [S3][S4]. A third-generation robot, Panther, launched globally on 8 April 2026 (160 cm, ~80 kg, 34 DoF, dual 8-DoF arms, 12 kg per arm, 80 cm torso lift, up to 2,070 TOPS), continuing the household + commercial dual-track [S5].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
UniX AI is the price-aggression benchmark in wheeled humanoids: a full-size, 33-DoF dual-8-DoF-arm platform from RMB 88k (~EUR 11k) undercuts Western BOM assumptions by an order of magnitude. Its tactile-model IP and home-scenario demos also preview where consumer semi-humanoids are heading. Risks: consumer robotics is unforgiving on reliability/support, funding depth is thinner than tier-1 peers (AgiBot, Spirit AI), and real-world household autonomy remains demo-grade. Trajectory: scaling, with fast generational cadence (three platforms in 20 months).
