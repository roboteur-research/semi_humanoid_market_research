## Company background
Holiday Robotics is a Seoul startup founded in April 2024 by Song Ki-young, who previously built SUALAB — a deep-learning industrial-vision company sold to Cognex (2019, ~$195M) — giving the team rare credibility in factory AI deployment. Its wheeled humanoid Friday pairs 7-DoF arms and in-house 20-DoF tactile hands (63 DoF total incl. a 5-DoF torso) with a "Vision-Language-Skill" AI architecture, explicitly prioritising dexterous manipulation over legs for manufacturing work. [S1][S2]

The company raised a large Series A (~₩155bn, reported ~$110–155M) in July 2026 and targets commercialisation at manufacturing sites in H2 2026, with planned output of ~100 units/year and a mass-production price goal around ₩100M (~$72k); current listing price is ~$200k/inquire. [S1][S2]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
One of Korea's most serious wheeled-humanoid plays and a direct competitive template for an EU industrial entrant: vision-AI pedigree, tactile-hand differentiation, factory-first go-to-market and credible funding. Watch for its H2 2026 commercialisation and whether tactile bin-picking/assembly demos convert to POs at Korean manufacturers (obvious channels: electronics/automotive suppliers). Trajectory: scaling up.
