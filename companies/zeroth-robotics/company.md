## Company background
Zeroth (ZeroTh) Robotics is a young, unusually well-capitalised Chinese embodied-AI startup registered in Jiangsu that claims three angel rounds totalling ~$70M at a ~$351M valuation within its first year (as of October 2025). Its public line-up spans consumer and research embodiments: M1 ("the companion at home", an embodied-intelligence home robot), W1 ("the explorer in the wild", an outdoor robot with vision-fusion terrain navigation) and Jupiter ("the wanderer among stars", a compact humanoid). [S1, vendor-claimed]

The wheeled dual-arm platform "Sean" appears on third-party directories (listed March 2026) but not on the current vendor site — branding on the robot itself ("zeroth" logo on base and torso) confirms it is the company's hardware; it is likely a research/developer platform or an early model superseded by the current product naming. [S2][S3]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Notable mostly as a capital signal: $70M angel money for a a sub-2-year-old company shows how aggressively Chinese VCs are seeding embodied-AI hardware. Sean itself is a modest 16-DoF, 3 kg-payload prototype; the company's centre of gravity is home/consumer robots, not industrial manipulation. Threat to an EU industrial entrant: low; monitor Jupiter and whether the company pivots toward commercial service deployments.
