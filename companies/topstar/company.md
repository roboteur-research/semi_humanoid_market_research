## Company background
Topstar is a Dongguan-listed industrial automation group built on injection-molding machinery and factory automation for 3C/plastics manufacturing — a large installed base of machines and integration projects across South China. Listed on ChiNext in 2017 (300607), it entered robotics via SCARA/six-axis arms and automation cells, and in 2025 launched an embodied-intelligence line, the 拓星纪 (Tuoxingji) series: the TWH020 "小拓" wheeled humanoid, TDM020 stationary dual-arm humanoid, and TM010 humanoid single arm, plus the 星仔 industrial quadruped [vendor site]. Positioning is explicitly industrial: material handling, sorting and inspection inside the injection-molding and 3C plants Topstar already serves; press coverage calls TWH020 "China's first humanoid applied in injection-molding scenarios" [third-party].

The TWH020 datasheet is unusually complete for the category (full joint tables, EtherCAT internals, ±0.05 mm repeatability), reflecting machine-builder DNA rather than AI-startup DNA. No pricing published; go-to-market via Topstar's existing direct/integrator channels.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Topstar exemplifies the Chinese industrial-incumbent route into semi-humanoids: bolt a precise dual-arm torso onto a lift column and omni base, and sell it into your own captive customer base with real uptime obligations. Its ±0.05 mm arm claim and 20 kg dual-arm load target genuine factory tasks (injection-molding tending), not demos. Threat to an EU entrant is concentrated in Asian 3C/plastics manufacturing; brand and channels outside China are limited to its machinery export network.
