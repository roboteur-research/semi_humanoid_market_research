## Company background
GigaAI (极佳科技) is a Beijing "world-model-first" AI company that aims to become "the OpenAI of the physical world": its research lineage runs from the DriveDreamer autonomous-driving world models to the GigaWorld generative world-model family (GigaWorld-1 targets robot policy evaluation in simulation) and the GigaBrain end-to-end embodied brain combining world models with VLA and reinforcement learning [third-party/vendor-claimed]. CEO Huang Guan predicts 95% success on 90% of one hundred common tasks as the practical Physical-AGI bar [third-party].

In November 2025 it unveiled its first hardware, the Maker H01 wheeled humanoid — a "Physical AGI Native Platform" whose purpose is explicitly to train and validate the world-model stack at scale rather than to chase a service vertical [third-party]. Funding accelerated in parallel: a >¥100M Series A1 led by Huawei Hubble amid three rounds inside two months, with cumulative reporting around ¥1B (a "$518M" English headline circulates but is inconsistent with Chinese reporting — flagged) [third-party, conflicting].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
GigaAI represents the "world-model-native" faction of the Chinese wave: hardware exists to feed and validate simulation/world models, mirroring (and racing) Western bets like Genesis AI. Huawei Hubble's backing signals strategic compute/ecosystem alignment. If world-model training loops genuinely derisk deployment, GigaAI's Maker line could improve faster per unit fielded than hardware-first rivals. For an EU entrant, GigaAI matters mainly as an AI-capability benchmark and potential model licensor/competitor rather than a near-term hardware rival in Europe.
