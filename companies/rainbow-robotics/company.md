## Company background
Rainbow Robotics is the KAIST Humanoid Robot Research Center spin-off founded in 2011 by Prof. Oh Jun-ho, creator of Korea's first bipedal humanoid HUBO (2004). The team's DRC-HUBO won the 2015 DARPA Robotics Challenge, giving the company the deepest humanoid pedigree in Korea. Revenue historically came from collaborative robot arms (RB series), quadrupeds (RBQ) and research humanoids; the RB-Y1 wheeled dual-arm platform (March 2024) is its flagship entry into the semi-humanoid segment. [S1][S2]

Samsung Electronics bought 14.7% in January 2023 (KRW 86.8B) and exercised its call option in December 2024, raising its stake to 35% for ~KRW 267B (~$181M), making Rainbow Robotics a consolidated Samsung subsidiary (regulatory approval completed early 2025). Samsung simultaneously created a "Future Robotics Office" (미래로봇추진단) reporting to the CEO, headed by Oh Jun-ho, to drive humanoid development — effectively making Rainbow the humanoid arm of the Samsung group. [S3][S4] A Korean prosecution probe into alleged insider trading around the Samsung share purchases was reported in 2026 (does not affect product operations to date). [S4]

Go-to-market is direct sales plus a US subsidiary (Schaumburg/Chicago, 2023) for cobot and RB-Y1 sales/support. Partnerships include an MoU with Schaeffler Group and KETI (April 2024) to co-develop mobile manipulators in Korea, and ongoing talks with CJ Logistics; Coupang is trialling the RB-Y1 in a fulfillment center (June 2026). [S2][S5]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
The RB-Y1 is the flagship non-Chinese wheeled semi-humanoid: shipping since October 2024, 130+ cumulative units sold by late 2025 (research-platform led), production capacity ~20/month, and Samsung's balance sheet behind it. Rainbow is a core member of Korea's K-Humanoid Alliance ecosystem and is transitioning the RB-Y1 from university AI labs (MIT, UC Berkeley, UW, Georgia Tech) into commercial logistics pilots. Trajectory: scaling up aggressively; the most credible Korean threat to EU entrants. [S5][S6]
