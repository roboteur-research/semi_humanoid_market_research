# QTrobot — LuxAI (T3 compact)

> QTrobot is a 64 cm, 5 kg expressive pedestal social robot with a screen face and 3-DoF gesture arms, commercially shipping since ~2018 for autism therapy, special-needs education and HRI research. At €2-10k with content subscriptions it proves the sustainable low end of the semi-humanoid market — social value without mobility or manipulation.

Hardware (third-party, IEEE Robots Guide): 64 x 35 x 21 cm, 5 kg, ABS/aluminum; 12 DoF total — 2-DoF head, two 3-DoF arms, 2-DoF unactuated hands — driven by eight HerkuleX smart servos with position/speed/overload/temperature feedback [S1]. Screen face displays animated expressions. Sensors: Intel RealSense D435 depth camera, ReSpeaker v2.0 far-field microphone array; emotion and pose recognition onboard [S1]. Compute: Intel NUC i7, 16 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD, Ubuntu + ROS, with Blockly visual programming, Python/C++/Java APIs — a genuinely open research platform [S1]. Power: 19 V DC adapter or external battery pack (stationary device, no base) [S1]. Product tiers: Research, Schools (session reporting/dashboard), and Home (300+ curriculum units on emotional, social, cognitive skills) [S2][S3]. Deployment evidence: shipping globally since ~2018 to therapy centers, schools and universities (e.g. University of Birmingham partnership, CRC Rome); WEF Smart Toy Award 2021, CES Innovation Award 2019 [S2][S3]. LuxAI is among the very few European semi-humanoid firms with years of continuous commercial shipments — by keeping scope minimal (no locomotion, no grasping).
