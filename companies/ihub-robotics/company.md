## Company background
iHub Robotics is a Kochi (Kerala) startup founded in 2021 and registered with the
Kerala Startup Mission. It brands itself "India's Leading Humanoid Robot Company"
and develops the TARA GEN1, a wheeled service semi-humanoid for customer-facing
roles (reception, airports, banks, patient support). The company raised a ~$520k
pre-seed round from US investors and announced plans to build what it calls
"India's largest humanoid robotics manufacturing facility" in Kerala [S3][S4].

Go-to-market is direct sale of low-cost service humanoids (TARA GEN1 listed at
~USD 13,998 [S2]) with early export traction claimed in the Gulf region: units
reportedly shipped to the UAE and Saudi Arabia [S2][S4]. The product emphasis is
conversational AI (100+ languages claimed, face/emotion recognition, LLM
integration) rather than manipulation — TARA's arms are low-DoF and largely
gestural [S2].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Low direct relevance to industrial dual-arm manipulation: TARA GEN1 is a
reception/service android in the Pepper tradition, with negligible payload (2 kg)
and minimal arm DoF. Relevance is regional and price-based — a sub-$15k Indian
service humanoid with Gulf-state export channels signals emerging low-cost
competition in customer-facing segments, not a threat in intralogistics or
manufacturing. Trajectory: scaling manufacturing ambition greatly exceeds current
funding (~$520k), so claims warrant skepticism.
