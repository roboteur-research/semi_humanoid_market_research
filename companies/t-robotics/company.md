## Company background
T-Robotics is an established (founded 2004, KRX-listed 2018) Korean industrial-robot maker whose core business is medium and large vacuum transfer robots for display and semiconductor fabs — it self-describes as a global leader in that niche — plus AMR logistics robots and medical rehabilitation robots [S1][S2]. It holds patents on modular body devices and finger modules ("core robot hand technology") that it cites as the basis for its humanoid program [S2].

In March 2026 it unveiled TR Works, an industrial semi-humanoid that mounts a humanoid dual-arm upper body directly on one of its AMR bases ("a humanoid riding a robot vacuum cleaner", per Seoul Economic Daily). The company explicitly states this wheeled hybrid is the final design — it will commercialize without ever developing bipedal legs [S3]. First public showing was at Automation World 2026 (COEX, Seoul, early March 2026) [S4].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
T-Robotics is the "incumbent industrial automation firm pivots to semi-humanoid" archetype: unlike startups it has 20+ years of precision-motion pedigree, existing fab/factory customers, a listed-company balance sheet, and an in-house AMR base to build on. Its public commitment to wheels-as-final-form is a strong terminology/strategy datapoint for the Korean market's semi-humanoid consensus. Product is at unveiling stage; no deployments or specs disclosed yet.
