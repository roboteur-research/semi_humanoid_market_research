# TR Works (TR웍스) — T-Robotics

> TR Works is T-Robotics' industrial semi-humanoid: a humanoid dual-arm upper body mounted on the company's own AMR base, unveiled in March 2026 at Automation World 2026 in Seoul. It is aimed at immediate deployment in manufacturing sites, leveraging two decades of vacuum-transfer-robot precision-control expertise. Competitively notable because a KRX-listed incumbent declared the wheeled hybrid its FINAL form factor — it will never build bipedal legs — reinforcing the Korean industry consensus behind semi-humanoids.

Design: an autonomous mobile robot (AMR) is attached beneath a humanoid torso with two arms — Seoul Economic Daily likened it to "a humanoid riding a robot vacuum cleaner" [S1]. T-Robotics describes TR Works (also written TR-WORKS) as an industrial humanoid "developed based on precision control technology accumulated from large vacuum robot and AMR businesses, designed for immediate deployment in manufacturing sites", and cites its patented finger-module / modular-body robot-hand technology as a building block [S4] (vendor-claimed). No DoF, payload, dimensions, sensing, compute or price figures have been published. The company plans to launch without developing bipedal legs, treating the wheeled hybrid as the final design [S1]. No deployment evidence yet; traction proxy is its existing fab/AMR customer base. The Robot Report confirms T-Robotics showed its humanoid publicly for the first time in Korea at AW 2026, an event where wheeled semi-humanoids outnumbered legged humanoids [S3].
