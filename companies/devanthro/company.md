## Company background
Devanthro grew out of **Roboy**, the musculoskeletal humanoid research project started 2012 at the University of Zurich AI Lab and continued by a 50+ member student team at TU Munich. Roboy 3.0 — a tendon-driven, antagonistic "muscle unit" series-elastic humanoid — was developed under Devanthro GmbH and remains one of Europe's best-known bio-inspired research robots. Around 2021 the company pivoted from musculoskeletal research to a pragmatic product: **Robody**, a wheeled, VR-teleoperated human avatar for care, under the tagline "robotic helpers AND human avatars" [S4][S6]. (Historical note only — Roboy is not a commercial product.)

The business model is avatar-enabled care service rather than robot sales: **Robody Cares** aims to deliver 24/7 in-home elderly-care support where remote (potentially offshore or off-site professional) caregivers "teleport" into the home via Oculus Quest VR, seeing through Robody's stereo RGB-D eyes, speaking with their voice, and performing bimanual household tasks; AI increasingly automates routines, with every teleoperation session generating training data ("hybrid intelligence") [S1]. Devanthro collaborates with large German care providers and works on validated care-insurance reimbursement pathways — its stated moat is that Robody is "the first and so far only humanoid robot that can be legally used in domestic settings" in Germany (vendor claim) [S1][S2].

Key validation came from the 3-year BMBF-funded **Teleskoop** project (closed Jan 2025; €690k funding) with FZI Karlsruhe and Charité Berlin, where Devanthro led the consortium: Robody was permanently installed in private households of ambulatory-care clients (Diakoniezentrum Pirmasens, Aiutanda, Augustinum), with 23 days of real-world deployment showing precise execution of physical everyday assistance via VR + haptic controllers [S3]. Robody has been deployed in real homes since early 2024 (3+ hardware generations); in January 2026 a Robody became "the world's first humanoid robot to care for a person with dementia in their home" (vendor-claimed) [S1][S2].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Devanthro occupies the care/avatar niche of the semi-humanoid market: teleoperation-first, service-revenue-first, and regulatory/reimbursement-first — the opposite of the autonomy-led industrial players. Its German care-provider relationships and BMBF-project pedigree make it the reference for "Pflegeroboter" avatars in the DACH region. Threat level to industrial entrants is low, but its home-legality and reimbursement groundwork could become valuable strategic assets if home care scales; conversely, thin capitalization is a survival risk.
