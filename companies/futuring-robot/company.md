## Company background
Futuring Robot ("未来不远" = "the future is not far") is a Shanghai consumer
robotics startup founded September 2022 by edtech entrepreneur Zhang Yi (the
site is even served off the Zhangmen/zmlearn CDN, consistent with the edtech
lineage). It positions itself as China's first consumer humanoid company focused
on real household deployment: dual-arm wheeled robots that tidy clutter, operate
refrigerators and washing machines, receive deliveries, and serve tea/water.
The company claims 30,000+ service hours across 300+ Chinese households — an
unusually concrete home-deployment claim for 2026 [S2][S3].

Its technical pitch is data-centric: a proprietary end-to-end "AVLA" model
architecture trained on large volumes of real household data collected in-home,
plus a "Self-Evolving WAM" world-action-model system. Funding momentum is
strong: RMB 200M angel round led by ZhenFund (Jan 2026) followed within months
by a reported ~RMB 1B round including internet majors [S2][S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
One of the most credible consumer/home wheeled-humanoid plays in China, with
actual in-home fleet data as its moat. Not an industrial competitor today, but
the household data engine, aggressive pricing (guide lists $5,000) and Chinese
capital scale make it a fast-moving reference point for low-cost dual-arm
platforms. Note: the humanoid.guide listing (and our discovery notes) mislabel
country — vendor identity is unambiguously Chinese (未来不远机器人).
