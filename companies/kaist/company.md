## Company background
KAIST's Hubo Lab under Prof. Jun-Ho Oh is Korea's foundational humanoid research group: it built the HUBO biped series from 2002 and won the 2015 DARPA Robotics Challenge with DRC-HUBO+ (which itself used wheels-on-knees convertible locomotion). Around 2018-19 the lab built M-Hubo, a wheeled dual-arm "robotic butler" that reuses the DRC-HUBO+ manipulator design on an omnidirectional base — an early, well-documented academic argument for the semi-humanoid form factor in service tasks [S1][S2].

The lab's commercial lineage matters more than the lab itself: Rainbow Robotics (Daejeon, founded 2011 as the Hubo Lab spinoff, now Samsung-controlled) commercialized the same design philosophy as the RB-Y1 wheeled dual-arm robot (2024), today the flagship non-Chinese semi-humanoid. M-Hubo is best read as the research ancestor of RB-Y1.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Not a market participant itself, but a key technology source and talent pipeline for Korea's semi-humanoid ecosystem (Rainbow Robotics, K-Humanoid Alliance). M-Hubo provides peer-reviewed benchmark data (IROS 2019) on wheeled-humanoid fetch-and-serve performance.
