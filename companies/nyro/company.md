## Company background
Nyro is a one-person hobbyist build by Japanese maker Ryota Kobayashi that "challenges the feasibility of individual-built humanoid robots". It is an upper-body robot with dual 7-DoF arms whose signature engineering choice is differential-gear joints, actuated by SteadyWin quasi-direct-drive motors on ODrive controllers (12x ODrive Micro + 2x ODrive S1) over dual CAN lines, with open-source "Amazing Hand" five-finger hands (Feetech servos) bridged to the ODrive CAN protocol by a custom STM32G4 board. Compute is a LattePanda 3 Delta; the torso is mounted on a camera tripod (no mobile base yet). Teleoperation uses a DYNAMIXEL-based leader-arm device, with imitation learning planned [S1].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Included chiefly as a terminology and capability datapoint: Kobayashi self-labels the build a "semi-humanoid", showing the term has propagated to the hobbyist grassroots by 2026, and the build demonstrates that QDD actuators, dexterous open-source hands and leader-arm teleop — the component stack of commercial semi-humanoids — are now individually accessible. Modest traction (436 views, 5 likes at time of review); no commercial intent evident.
