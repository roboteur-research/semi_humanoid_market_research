## Company background
BamBot is Tim Qian's ~$300 open-source "low-cost AI robot": a small wheeled dual-arm robot built from two SO-ARM100-derived arms, Feetech STS3215 bus servos and 3D-printed parts on a wheeled base (variant B0 / B0-base) [S1][S2]. Its signature contribution is feetech.js — controlling the servos directly from a web browser — and bambot.org, where each variant can be simulated and teleoperated in-browser. It is LeRobot-compatible and Apache-2.0 licensed. Traction: ~950+ GitHub stars, Discord/WeChat communities [S2]. XLeRobot credits BamBot as one of its foundations.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
With XLeRobot and InMoov it defines the hobbyist tier: sub-$500 wheeled dual-arm platforms that normalize the semi-humanoid form factor for education and embodied-AI tinkering, with browser-first UX lowering the entry barrier further. Ecosystem signal, not competition.
