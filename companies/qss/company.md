## Company background
QSS AI & Robots is a Riyadh-based AI/robotics firm best known for building the first Saudi-made humanoid persona robots: Sara (female, abaya, unveiled early 2023), Mohamad/Muhammad ("first Saudi robot in the form of a man", red keffiyeh, shown at DeepFest 2024) and Saud [S1][S2]. The robots run a proprietary in-house Arabic LLM — explicitly not ChatGPT-based — tuned to Saudi dialects and programmed to observe cultural norms (no politics, no sex talk), which became their global press hook [S2][S3]. They are staples of Saudi tech events (LEAP, DeepFest, AI for Good, Arabs Got Talent).

QSS positions itself as a customization/integration house across service, delivery, security and cleaning robots, and has announced a partnership with UK-based startup Humanoid plus an ambition to deploy up to 10,000 humanoid robots in Saudi Arabia over five years [S1] (vendor-claimed, aspirational).

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Represents the Gulf sovereign-tech-branding segment: legless/concealed-base persona robots whose value is Arabic-language interaction and national identity, not manipulation. The 10,000-robot ambition and Humanoid (UK) partnership make QSS a potential channel partner/customer for hardware makers targeting Saudi Vision-2030 spending, rather than a hardware competitor.
