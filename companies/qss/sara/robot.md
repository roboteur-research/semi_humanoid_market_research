# Sara (سارة) — QSS AI & Robots

> Sara is the first Saudi-made humanoid: a 1.62 m female-presenting persona robot in an abaya, with a legless/concealed wheeled-pedestal lower body, gesturing arms, and a proprietary in-house Arabic LLM tuned to Saudi dialects and programmed to respect cultural norms (no politics, no sex). Unveiled in early 2023, she works reception/engagement roles at national events (LEAP, DeepFest, AI for Good). Competitively she is an Arabic-HRI/branding play, not a manipulation platform — but QSS's stated 10,000-robot Saudi deployment ambition makes the company a notable regional channel.

Design: humanoid upper body with expressive face and gesturing arms; persona is a 25-year-old Saudi woman wearing an abaya; height 1.62 m [S2][S3]. Lower body is concealed/legless (no bipedal locomotion; discovery sweep notes a concealed base — estimated wheeled pedestal). The differentiator is software: a proprietary LLM developed internally by QSS ("not dependent on external programs like ChatGPT"), handling Arabic (multiple Saudi dialects) and English, with guardrails matching societal norms — CEO Elie Metri: "she should be nice, not talking politics, not talking sex" [S2][S4]. Siblings: Mohamad/Muhammad (male, red keffiyeh, DeepFest 2024 — drew viral controversy over an on-stage gesture incident) and Saud [S1]. No DoF, payload, battery or sensing specs are published; arms are gestural. Deployment evidence: recurring appearances at LEAP, DeepFest, AI for Good Global Summit, Arabs Got Talent; QSS claims a plan for up to 10,000 humanoids in five years with UK partner Humanoid (vendor-claimed, unverified) [S1].
