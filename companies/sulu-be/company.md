## Company background
Sulu.be is the creative robotics studio of Belgian designer Jan De Coster
(Mechelen) — not a robotics manufacturer in the industrial sense. The studio
builds expressive character robots for entertainment, hospitality, events and
brand campaigns; its best-known creation Steve (named after Steve McQueen,
conceived 2013) is a 210 cm social humanoid deployed as a character host in
Belgian movie theaters (Brightfish media collaboration), corporate receptions,
cultural festivals and brand activations [S1][S2]. Design philosophy is
character-driven — "engage people emotionally" with an "expressive yet
minimalist form" rather than functional human mimicry [S1].

Technically the robots are artisanal: Arduino-based control, DC motors with
custom aluminum gears, WiFi; Steve is listed at $60k, "in production" (i.e.
built to order) since ~2024 [S1][S2].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Marginal. Sulu.be occupies the entertainment/character niche — a single-person
studio producing bespoke hosts, not scalable work robots. Included for market-
mapping completeness: it demonstrates European demand for humanoid *presence*
(hospitality/branding) at ~$60k, a segment adjacent to but distinct from
functional semi-humanoids. No competitive threat to an industrial EU entrant.
