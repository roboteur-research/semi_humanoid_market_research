## Company background
Paaila ("step" in Nepali) Technology is a Kathmandu robotics/AI startup founded in December 2016 by six engineers. Its first humanoid, Pari, was deployed at Nepal SBI Bank's "inTouch" digital branch as a greeter/information robot; customer feedback led the bank to order a second, upgraded unit within months [S1]. In 2018 Paaila opened Naulo, billed as South Asia's first fully digitized robotic restaurant (Kathmandu, King's Way), staffed with five of its own robots — three humanoid "Ginger" waiters and two "Ferry" delivery units — all designed and manufactured in Nepal [S1][S2].

The company registered a subsidiary in China with ambitions to export, and continues as Nepal's flagship robotics firm. Its robots emphasize conversational features (English + Nepali speech synthesis, jokes, IQ questions), swarm coordination and self-docking rather than manipulation.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A frontier-market datapoint: proves locally-built waiter/bank semi-humanoids are viable at very low cost outside advanced economies, in the gestural/hospitality (not manipulation) segment. IMPORTANT disambiguation: Paaila's "Ginger" is entirely distinct from CloudMinds/Dataa Robotics' "Ginger" (China) — the awesome-robot-descriptions list's Ginger URDF (Rayckey/GingerURDF) belongs to the CloudMinds robot, not Paaila's. No competitive threat to an EU entrant.
