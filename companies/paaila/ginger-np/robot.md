# Ginger (Nepal) — Paaila Technology

> Ginger is Paaila Technology's Nepali-built humanoid waiter robot: a wheeled torso robot that autonomously carries dishes from kitchen to table, avoids obstacles, self-docks to charge, coordinates with sibling robots via swarm intelligence, and banters with guests in English (Nepali in development). Three Gingers have served at Kathmandu's Naulo restaurant since 2018, alongside the earlier Pari bank robot at Nepal SBI. NOT to be confused with CloudMinds/Dataa Robotics' Chinese "Ginger" — the Ginger URDF in awesome-robot-descriptions (Rayckey/GingerURDF) is the CloudMinds robot, a common misattribution.

Design: humanoid upper body with head, chest display and gesturing arms on a concealed wheeled base; carries food trays; performs face tracking and natural gestures while speaking [S1][S2]. Software features (vendor-claimed): AI self-driving with obstacle avoidance, navigation by track line or SLAM, swarm intelligence coordinating multiple robots (collision prevention, task distribution), speech recognition/NLP, jokes and IQ-question answering in English and Nepali, full-day battery with autonomous docking [S2][S3]. No DoF, dimensions, payload or sensor specs are published; the arms are gestural/tray-carrying rather than manipulating (estimated). Deployment evidence: 3 Ginger + 2 Ferry robots in daily service at Naulo (South Asia's first robotic restaurant) since 2018; predecessor humanoid Pari (2 generations) at Nepal SBI Bank's inTouch branch as greeter [S1][S4] (third-party).
