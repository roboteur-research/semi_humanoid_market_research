## Company background
Moon Dynamics (攬月動力, "grasp-the-moon power") is a Shenzhen startup building the L1, a wheeled-bipedal hybrid for heavy-load industrial handling. Public information is thin — no functional corporate website, no disclosed founders or funding — with the product documented mainly through the humanoid.guide database, LinkedIn demo posts (20 kg uphill carries, water-tank transport, high-reach clamping) and social coverage [third-party]. Positioning is pragmatic: two powered wheel-legs (self-balancing, suspension articulation) give a small footprint, slope/obstacle tolerance and dynamic disturbance rejection, while 2-finger claws and a 25 kg lift target repetitive logistics/factory carrying rather than dexterity [third-party].

The L1 is listed "in production" at ~$60,000 [third-party DB, unverified]. Given the opacity, treat maturity claims cautiously.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
SCOPE NOTE — BORDERLINE: L1 is a wheel-LEG (wheeled-bipedal) robot, not a chassis-based wheeled semi-humanoid; it sits at the boundary of this study's scope alongside Hexagon AEON and GAC GoMate and is included as a boundary datapoint. Its relevance: it shows Shenzhen's supply chain now yields dynamic balancing wheel-leg humanoids with 25 kg lift at $60k — a price/capability point that pressures both wheeled and legged categories in industrial handling.
