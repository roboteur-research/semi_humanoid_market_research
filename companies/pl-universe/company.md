## Company background
PL-Universe Robotics Technology (Suzhou) Co., Ltd. (普罗宇宙机器人科技（苏州）有限公司) is a young Suzhou Industrial Park company (founded January 2025) building industrial-grade "embodied AI" robots for smart manufacturing. Its flagship ProWhite Robot 2.0 is a four-wheel omnidirectional dual-arm humanoid pitched at consumer-electronics/3C, automotive-parts, semiconductor manufacturing/test and biomedicine tasks — differentiating on sub-millimeter precision (±0.05 mm repeatability with adaptive dynamic accuracy compensation) and ≥8 h endurance rather than consumer interaction. [S1][S2, vendor-claimed]

A second, heavier model, Zeus 1, appears on third-party listings as a ~300 kg heavy-duty industrial humanoid (42 DoF, 25 kg grip strength, modular tooling, ~$140k) with early commercial units; PL-Universe itself does not document Zeus 1 on its English site. [S3, third-party]. The company operates seven language sites and English-language social channels (LinkedIn, X, YouTube), signalling early export intent despite its youth. Aggregated third-party reporting still classes ProWhite as a pre-commercial prototype as of 2026 with undisclosed pricing. [S4]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Directly relevant to an EU industrial entrant: an industrial-precision wheeled dual-arm play (±0.05 mm claims, VLA-based flexible automation) from China's strongest manufacturing cluster, priced (per third-party listings) in the $45–140k band. Credibility is unproven — the company is a year old with no disclosed customers — but its spec targets frame the price/precision bar Chinese entrants will market against. Trajectory: too early to call; watch for funding and first factory pilots.
