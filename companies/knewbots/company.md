## Company background
KNEWBOTS (晓悟智能) is a Hangzhou mobile-robot company established with investment from ThunderSoft (中科创达), the listed Chinese OS/edge-AI software house known for Qualcomm-platform robotics modules [S2]. Its core business is intralogistics AMRs (latent and forklift lines) with full-stack in-house control, navigation, perception and fleet scheduling; it won a 2024 "Golden Ant" logistics innovation award [S2]. On top of this AMR base it offers a 轮臂式 "wheel-arm" humanoid — a dual-7-DoF-arm torso on a wheeled chassis — positioned for retail guidance, substation inspection, line-side sorting and material replenishment [S1]. The stack pairs LLM task planning ("brain collaboration") with real-time motion control and multi-robot unified scheduling, plausibly leveraging ThunderSoft/Qualcomm compute heritage alongside NVIDIA Orin [S1][S2].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A minor vendor individually, but a telling example of the AMR-company upgrade path (like SEER, iSageBot): logistics fleet suppliers bolting dual-arm torsos onto proven chassis and selling the humanoid as one node in a scheduled fleet. ThunderSoft's backing gives it distribution into industrial software channels. Low direct threat outside China.
