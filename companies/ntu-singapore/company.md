## Company background
NTU's Institute for Media Innovation, led by virtual-human pioneer Nadia Magnenat Thalmann, commissioned Nadine in 2013 — a realistic gynoid built by Kokoro (Japan, the Geminoid/Actroid manufacturer) and modeled on Thalmann herself, then developed the perception/cognition/interaction software stack around it [S1]. Nadine became one of the most-deployed research androids: ~100,000 visitor interactions at Singapore's ArtScience Museum (2017), a customer-service stint at insurer AIA Singapore, and an eldercare study at Bright Hill Evergreen Home with light-dementia patients (late 2020-April 2021) [S1].

Since 2022 the research line has continued at Thalmann's MIRALab (University of Geneva), where Nadine was re-architected around large language models — an LLM-driven agent with human-like long-term memory and emotional appraisal (published 2024, trials continuing into 2026) [S2][S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Nadine defines the academic benchmark for socially-assistive androids: strong evidence base for reception/eldercare HRI, but a seated, non-mobile, non-manipulating platform. Relevant to an EU entrant mainly as (a) proof of demand signals in insurance/eldercare front-office and (b) an early template for LLM-driven robot cognition; no hardware competition.
