## Company background
The Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center, branded X-Humanoid, is a state-backed embodied-AI/humanoid platform company created in 2023 with Beijing municipal support and industrial shareholders (incl. UBTECH, Xiaomi). It is best known for the Tiangong (天工) biped, which won the world's first humanoid half-marathon in 2025, and for its Huisi Kaiwu (慧思开物) general embodied-intelligence software platform. [S2][S3]

Its wheeled line, Tian Yi (天轶), is a "wheel-arm robot" (轮臮式/轮臂式机器人): a humanoid torso with dual 7-DoF arms and dexterous hands mounted via an articulated pitch linkage on an AMR-style wheeled base. Tian Yi 2.0 won gold and silver medals at the August 2025 World Humanoid Robot Games (27 precise 8 mm peg insertions, fully autonomous) and went on retail sale via JD.com on 17 October 2025 alongside Tiangong 2.0. [S2]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Important benchmark competitor: state backing, strong autonomy demonstrations (competition-validated precision insertion), retail availability in China, and an open-platform strategy (Ubuntu/ROS stack) that seeds the domestic ecosystem. Trajectory: scaling up with strong government tailwind.
