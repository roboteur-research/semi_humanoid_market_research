## Company background
XYZ started in 2019 as a food-and-beverage service-robot company: its BARISBREW barista robots, Arys ice-cream robots and STORAGY delivery robots run in real Korean stores (accumulated 720,000+ orders, ~KRW 6B annual revenue). This gives it something most humanoid startups lack — years of paid, in-store operating data. CEO Hwang Sung-jae frames the company as a "Physical AI" business built on a loop of lab models → store deployment → data collection → model improvement. [S1][S2]

DEUX ("Deuce", 듀스), unveiled 27 July 2026 with a live demonstration at the LoungeX store in Seongsu-dong (Seoul), is its dual-arm semi-humanoid for retail floors: restocking and arranging shelves, inventory checks, tidying, and packing boxed goods. The product system spans the robot (DEUX), a 1:1-paired data-collection glove (Glove X: 220° ultra-wide dual camera, 1,000 Hz magnetic-encoder joint tracking with Meta Quest hand tracking, 3-channel fingertip pressure sensors) and Brain X behavior/agentic models. Pre-order pricing is public: Mobile DEUX $39,900, DEUX (fixed) $29,900, Glove X $3,900/unit — pre-orders include Brain X plus one task-modeling cycle; NVIDIA Thor compute is sold separately. [S3][S4]

The March 2026 Series B (KRW 13B, Kolon Investment, Yuanta Investment, Crit Ventures and other financial/strategic investors) was justified by "actual revenue and data accumulation through robot services in real commercial venues" and funds the humanoid program. [S2]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
XYZ attacks the retail/convenience segment with the lowest published price for a mobile dual-arm robot with dexterous 7-DoF hands (~$40k) and a consumer-grade data-glove workflow. Its store-operations track record and Seongsu retail testbed give it authentic deployment channels in Korea. Trajectory: early (July 2026 unveiling) but scaling ambitions across "stores, offices, hospitals, homes". [S3][S4]
