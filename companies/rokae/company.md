## Company background
Rokae is one of China's leading lightweight/collaborative robot makers, founded 2015 in Beijing by Tuo Hua. Its differentiation has always been high-performance force control: the xMate 7-axis cobot line (direct joint-torque sensing, high-dynamic impedance control) is positioned as a Franka-class research/industrial arm, and Rokae sells into 3C electronics, automotive parts, medical and general industry across a dozen-plus export markets (Germany, France, Japan, Korea, Russia) [vendor-claimed/third-party]. Manufacturing is centered in Zoucheng, Shandong; the company has raised well over ¥1.2B cumulatively, latterly from state industrial funds (National Manufacturing Transformation & Upgrade Fund) — a signal of policy backing [third-party].

In 2025 Rokae launched an "Embodied Intelligence Series" topped by Helios, a 42-DoF wheeled dual-arm humanoid that packages two 7-DoF xMate-heritage force-controlled arms, a 4-DoF torso and an omnidirectional base — debuting internationally at Automate 2026 (Chicago) [vendor/third-party]. The strategy mirrors JAKA/Fourier: leverage proven cobot arm and controller IP into the semi-humanoid category for industrial tasks.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Rokae brings the most credible force-control pedigree of the Chinese cobot makers entering this space — its whole-body "posture-vision-force" control claim rests on real shipped torque-controlled joints, not marketing. Helios's industrial framing (flexible assembly, sorting, machine tending) plus export channels and Automate presence make Rokae a realistic near-term competitor in precisely the industrial niches an EU semi-humanoid entrant would target, at likely aggressive Chinese pricing.
