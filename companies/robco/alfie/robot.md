# Alfie — RobCo (Munich)  ⚠ base type undisclosed

> "Autonomous Industrial Robot": bimanual humanoid-form torso with head, announced
> 15 April 2026 (after RobCo's $100M Series C), previewed at Hannover Messe. All released
> official imagery ends at the waist — but at 0:14 of the official promo video, the full
> robot appears in simulation on a development monitor with a broad floor-standing base
> platform under the waist column: a mobile/AMR-style base is the probable configuration
> (still not officially disclosed). Either way it is in scope
> (pedestal dual-arm torsos qualify); its stated intralogistics use cases (picking,
> kitting, palletizing) hint at eventual mobility. First customer deployments planned
> for late 2026 via RaaS. Not to be confused with Prosper Robotics' "Alfie" home robot
> (London).
