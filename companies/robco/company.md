## Company background
RobCo built its business on modular snap-together industrial robot kits for SME
automation, sold as RaaS — one of Germany's best-funded robotics startups (Sequoia,
Lightspeed; $100M Series C in January 2026). In April 2026 it announced **Alfie**, a
bimanual humanoid-form "Autonomous Industrial Robot", moving the company from modular
arms into the semi-humanoid class on a declared "Physical AI" roadmap (Level-4 autonomy
positioning: learn, adapt, execute with minimal intervention). [S1][S2][S3]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A directly domestic (Munich) competitor for a German entrant: strong SME channel,
existing RaaS motion, fresh capital, and Hannover Messe visibility. Its modular-axis
heritage suggests component reuse in Alfie's arms; its software suite (RobFlow/RobVision)
is the integration moat.
