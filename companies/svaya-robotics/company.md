## Company background
Svaya Robotics, founded 2018 in Hyderabad by Vijay Seelam, is one of India's few vertically integrated robot manufacturers — designing its own arms, controllers and software with a "software-defined robots" philosophy [S1][S4]. Its base business is the **SR-L collaborative robot** line (positioned as India's first homegrown cobots) for flexible factory automation. Credibility milestones came through defense R&D: with DRDO labs R&DE Pune and DEBEL Bangalore it developed **India's first indigenous quadruped robot** (25 kg payload, rough-terrain recon) **and a powered exoskeleton** as technology demonstrators (2022-23) [S2].

Its semi-humanoid entry, the **Bimanual humanoid** (2025-26 marketing push), stacks two high-precision 6-DoF SR-series arms on a humanoid torso with sensor head: 155 cm, 70 kg, built-in joint force sensing, EtherCAT backbone with industrial fieldbus options (Ethernet/Modbus TCP/Profinet), IP-rated construction, no-code programming and digital-twin tooling; ~$90K, early production/limited release, mountable on mobile bases per company positioning though sold as a stationary platform [S3][S5]. Target work: hazardous industrial tasks (welding, forging, foundry), industrial R&D and education, plus defense applications [S5].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Svaya is India's most credible industrial dual-arm player: it owns its arm supply chain (unlike integrators), has defense-grade engineering references, and prices its bimanual platform (~$90K) below Western dual-arm systems. Constraints: cobot-grade 6-DoF arms (no 7th redundancy axis), 2/3-finger grippers rather than dexterous hands, undisclosed funding, and a domestic-first market focus. For an EU entrant it is mainly relevant as (a) proof of an emerging low-cost Indian industrial semi-humanoid supply base and (b) a potential local partner/competitor in Indian defense-industrial procurement.
