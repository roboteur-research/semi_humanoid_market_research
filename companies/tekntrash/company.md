## Company background
TeknTrash is a UK company that started in AI-powered waste analytics —
extracting post-consumption product intelligence from waste streams — and has
extended into robotics with ALPHA ("Automated Litter Processing Humanoid
Assistant"), a dual-arm wheeled semi-humanoid for recycling facilities.
The pitch: human sorters sustain 30-40 picks/minute but fatigue and face
hazardous material exposure; ALPHA automates the picking cabin [S2][S3].

Hardware appears to integrate off-the-shelf Chinese components (Realman-branded
arm/motor technology, NVIDIA Orin AGX compute, RealSense-class head camera on a
lift-column torso over an AMR base), which fits a software/analytics company
assembling rather than developing actuation in-house. Status is prototype with
pilot deployments claimed with waste-management operators; target price ~$55k
[S1][S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A vertical-specialist entrant: rather than a general-purpose platform, ALPHA is
a waste-sorting appliance in semi-humanoid form. Relevant to an EU entrant as
(a) evidence that recycling/waste is an early commercial niche for dual-arm
mobile manipulators in Europe, and (b) a potential integration customer or
competitor in that niche at a $55k price point. Capability claims (20 ms
glass-to-action, hyperspectral vision) are unverified vendor marketing.
