## Company background
ARX ("方舟无限", Ark Unlimited), founded mid-2023 in Beijing, builds low-cost force-controlled robot arms and bimanual/mobile platforms for the embodied-AI research market. Its arms (L5 at ¥29,800, X5 at ~¥60k) reproduced Stanford ALOHA-style autonomous grasping at a fraction of Western hardware cost, and the company ships complete imitation-learning tooling: VR teleoperation, CAN SDKs, ACT training pipelines, and open GitHub repos (ARX_PLAY, X7s_PLAY, ROS2 play stacks, all-in-one data-collection/training/deployment manuals) [vendor GitHub / third-party press].

The product ladder climbs from single arms to the AC one leader-follower dual-arm station, the X7s dual-arm set, and the LIFT/LIFT2 — an omnidirectional mobile base with lift column and dual arms marketed as "the world's first omnidirectional lifting dual-arm platform", from ¥249,800, explicitly positioned for π0/ALOHA algorithm reproduction and mobile manipulation data collection [third-party snippets/vendor]. ARX is regarded (with AgileX and Galaxea) as one of the leading domestic suppliers of ALOHA-descendant research hardware, with broad adoption in Chinese university and startup labs [third-party, discovery compilation].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
ARX is infrastructure for the semi-humanoid wave rather than an end-market competitor: its cheap force-controlled arms and LIFT2-class mobile bimanual platforms are what hundreds of labs use to generate manipulation data and validate VLA policies. Its price points (¥29,800 arms, ¥249,800 mobile dual-arm robot) set the research-market reference costs an EU entrant's academic/data-collection offering would be judged against.
