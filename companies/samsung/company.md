## Company background
Samsung Research (the advanced R&D arm of Samsung Electronics' DX division) has shown consumer robot concepts at CES since 2019 (Bot Care, Bot Air, Bot Retail), adding **Bot Handy**, a single-arm wheeled home manipulation robot, at CES 2021 and an upgraded demo at CES 2022 [S1]. None of the Bot series manipulation concepts has been commercialized; Samsung's first shipping consumer robot is the arm-less rolling companion **Ballie**. A robot-avatar/telepresence line and the EX1/Bot Fit exercise assistant were announced in 2023 but the manipulation robots remained research demos.

Samsung's serious humanoid play runs through **Rainbow Robotics**: it bought 14.7% in January 2023 (KRW 86.8B) and exercised call options in December 2024 to reach **35% (~KRW 267B, ~$181M), making the KAIST-HUBO spinoff a consolidated Samsung subsidiary** in early 2025. Simultaneously Samsung created a CEO-reporting "Future Robotics Office" headed by HUBO creator Prof. Oh Jun-ho [S4] — see the dedicated dossier at `companies/rainbow-robotics/` (RB-Y1 wheeled dual-arm platform). In-house, Samsung Research's Robot Intelligence team continues work on manipulation AI (grasping transparent/obscured objects, task planning) that fed Bot Handy demos [S1].

Note on "Bot Handy 2": a 2026 aggregator page (originofbots.com) describes a dual-arm "Bot Handy 2" with Google Gemini integration and ~3 kg grip payload [S3]. **No Samsung primary source, newsroom entry, or CES 2026 listing corroborates this** — Samsung's CES 2026 robotics/appliance lineup featured Ballie and AI appliances, not a Bot Handy successor [S5]. Treat "Bot Handy 2" as unverified/likely fabricated aggregator content until Samsung confirms.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Samsung matters less for Bot Handy itself (a shelved concept) than as the deepest-pocketed Korean entrant: it owns the leading non-Chinese wheeled semi-humanoid vendor (Rainbow Robotics RB-Y1), chairs enormous manufacturing capacity, and has stated group-level humanoid ambitions under Oh Jun-ho. Any future Samsung consumer manipulation robot would likely merge Samsung Research's home-AI work with Rainbow's hardware. Trajectory: consumer manipulation concepts dormant; industrial/humanoid investment accelerating via Rainbow.
