# Samsung Bot Handy — Samsung Research

> CES 2021/2022 concept robot: a tall, slim, wheeled home robot with **one** extendable manipulator arm and 2-finger gripper, demonstrated loading dishwashers, pouring drinks, picking up laundry and tidying rooms. **Borderline inclusion**: with a single arm it sits outside the strict dual-arm semi-humanoid definition, but it is Samsung's only mobile-manipulation robot concept and the anchor for any future Samsung entry. Never commercialized; a rumored dual-arm "Bot Handy 2" (2026) is single-source and unverified.

## Design & morphology
Tall slim monolithic body on a low cylindrical **wheeled base**; single articulated arm mounted on a shoulder rail with **elevation capability** ("extended workspace in 3D space through mobility and elevation" — Samsung [S1]). Black front face panel with minimal eye display. Height/weight/DoF: n/a (never disclosed) — visually est. ~1.3–1.5 m (estimated from demo footage next to humans).

## Locomotion
Wheeled base (drive type undisclosed); indoor home navigation only. Speed n/a.

## Upper body & manipulation
- **One arm** (DoF undisclosed, visually ~6-7 joints incl. telescoping/elevating shoulder mount) ending in a **2-finger gripper** with compliant pads [S1][S2].
- Samsung's claimed core competencies: dexterous manipulation (arm/hand control), general object handling (AI + grasping of transparent and partially obscured objects with collision avoidance), extended 3-D workspace [S1] (vendor-claimed).
- CES 2021: single-object pick demos (wine pouring, laundry into machine, dish into dishwasher). CES 2022: multi-object "tableware handling" — clearing a table, environment analysis, dish sorting [S1].

## Sensing
Vision-based object recognition (cameras; details undisclosed). AI recognizes shape/position of varied objects incl. transparent glassware [S1]. No lidar or sensor suite disclosed.

## Actuation & power
n/a (not disclosed).

## Compute & software
Proprietary Samsung Research AI stack (object recognition, grasp planning, task sequencing). No SDK, no ROS statement, no connectivity details disclosed. "Bot Handy 2" Gemini-integration claims [S3] are unverified single-source content.

## Safety & compliance
n/a — concept stage; collision-avoidance during grasping claimed [S1].

## Deployment evidence & traction
None. Stage demos at CES 2021 and CES 2022 only; Samsung stated it "continues developing… for eventual commercial viability" [S1] but the newsroom tag has no Bot Handy entries after 2022 [S2], and Samsung's CES 2026 lineup (Ballie, AI appliances) contained no successor [S4]. The "Bot Handy 2" page claiming dual arms rated ~3 kg grip + Google Gemini [S3] has no corroborating primary source — treated as unreliable aggregator content (possibly AI-generated).

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* Bot Handy is competitively dormant — a 2021-22 marketing concept with no shipping path — so it poses no direct threat. Its significance is directional: it proves Samsung's interest in home mobile manipulation and its in-house grasping AI, which combined with Samsung's 35% control of Rainbow Robotics (RB-Y1 hardware) and massive consumer-electronics distribution could produce a formidable consumer semi-humanoid quickly if Samsung commits. EU entrants should watch Samsung/Rainbow announcements rather than Bot Handy itself; the unverified "Bot Handy 2" chatter suggests market anticipation of exactly such a move.
