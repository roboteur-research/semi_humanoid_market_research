## Company background
PsiBot (灵初智能, "Lingchu Intelligence") is a dexterous-manipulation-first embodied-AI startup founded September 2024 in Beijing, running a joint laboratory with Peking University (Prof. Yaodong Yang, a leading RL researcher). Its thesis: long-horizon dexterous manipulation via a "fast-slow" hierarchical brain — RL-trained low-level dexterity plus VLA-based reasoning. Model cadence has been rapid: Psi R0 (first end-to-end RL model, 12/2024), Psi R1 (VLA, 05/2025 — demonstrated by the famous 30-minute autonomous mahjong-playing demo built on the CoAT chain-of-action-thought framework), and Psi R2 (04/2026, claimed top of Molmospace benchmarks) [vendor-claimed/third-party].

Hardware exists to serve the models: the Psi V1 wheeled dual-arm robot (32 DoF), the PsiBot H1 16-DoF dexterous hand, and the Psi-SynEngine data-collection stack with 62+-DoF exoskeleton tactile gloves. Funding is exceptional for the vintage — roughly ¥2B cumulative by March 2026 from Hillhouse, BlueRun, strategic investor AgiBot (Zhiyuan), and state "national team" funds — and commercialization is targeted at semi-structured logistics/retail (small-scale warehouse sorting validation; Meituan last-mile robot cooperation; NVIDIA simulation partnership) [third-party].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
PsiBot is a model-first competitor whose wheeled semi-humanoid exists as an embodiment for dexterous VLA policies — closer to a Chinese Physical Intelligence with its own hardware than to a robot vendor. If Psi R-series models keep leading long-horizon dexterity benchmarks, PsiBot's platform (or licensed models) could leapfrog mechanically superior rivals. For an EU entrant the threat is on the software axis: PsiBot-class manipulation autonomy is the capability bar customers will benchmark against, and its state-backed ¥2B war chest funds aggressive scaling.
