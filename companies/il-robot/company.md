## Company background
I.L (아이엘, KOSDAQ 307180; formerly IL Science) describes itself as an "integrated future mobility platform company" and is pivoting into what it calls an industrial "physical AI platform" business [S3]. Its robotics thesis is data-first: rather than selling robots outright, it deploys them in its own group factories to accumulate real-world manipulation data, then plans to sell "integrated solutions combining process-specific training data, field application software, and operation and update services" [S1].

In February 2026 it deployed two ILBOT wheeled semi-humanoids on a live automotive-parts injection-molding line at group affiliate IL Mobility — one of the earliest documented real-production deployments among the new Korean semi-humanoid wave [S1][S2]. In June 2026 it added ILBOT L1 MAX, a wheel-leg quadruped for patrol/inspection, extending the ILBOT platform family [S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Among the three Korean companies profiled in the Seoul Economic Daily semi-humanoid article (with Neubility and T-Robotics), I.L is the only one with a robot already working in actual mass production (parts sorting after injection molding, omni-picker end effectors). Its captive-factory data-flywheel model mirrors Chinese players' strategies. Threat to an EU entrant is currently low (in-group deployment, simple repetitive tasks) but the production-data head start is a genuine asset.
