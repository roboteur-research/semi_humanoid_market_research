# ILBOT (아이엘봇) — I.L

> ILBOT is I.L's wheeled (legless) industrial semi-humanoid with swappable end effectors, deployed since February 2026 on a real automotive-parts injection-molding line at group affiliate IL Mobility. It matters competitively as the earliest of the March-2026 Korean semi-humanoid wave to reach actual mass-production work, and as the anchor of I.L's "physical AI platform" strategy of harvesting real factory manipulation data.

Design: a wheeled robot "lacking legs" with a humanoid dual-arm upper body; end effectors are interchangeable per task — five-fingered hands, Omni Pickers, or vacuum grippers [S1][S2]. For the injection-molding deployment, omni-pickers were installed for production efficiency. Two units run on IL Mobility's line doing simple repetitive sorting of molded automotive parts, after phased validation covering repetitive-task performance, work assistance, in-process mobility, and emergency response [S1] (vendor-claimed via press). I.L is using the deployment to collect physical-AI data on task repeatability, path optimization and exception handling, explicitly contrasting real-line data with simulation-based development [S1]. No DoF, payload, dimensions, sensing, compute or price figures are published. Deployment evidence: 2 units in actual mass-production operation at a group-affiliate factory (third-party reported, vendor-derived — note it is a captive in-group deployment, not an external customer).
