## Company background
PaXini is China's tactile-first embodied-AI company: its core IP is the ITPU (Intelligent Tactile Processing Unit) — a 6D Hall-array multi-dimensional tactile sensing unit that measures normal/shear forces and more, which PaXini claims to have cost-reduced from ~RMB 100,000 per fingertip (lab-grade) to RMB 199 [S3]. It commercialized the DexH series dexterous hands (DexH13: 13 DoF, 0.01 N force-control precision, 5 kg load, >100k-cycle durability) and, per Chinese press, entered Apple's supply chain as a sensing provider [S1][S3].

TORA-ONE (2023 first gen; current 2nd gen) is claimed as the industry's first humanoid with fully integrated multi-dimensional tactile skin/hands — current official spec: 2,280 ITPU units feeding 6,840 tactile channels and a VTLA (vision-tactile-language-action) model [S2]. Strategy is vertical: sell sensors and hands to the whole industry while fielding its own tactile humanoid, monetizing both layers of the stack. The March 2026 Series B (>RMB 1B at >RMB 10B valuation) made it, per press, the highest-valued company in the "embodied perception" niche [S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
PaXini matters twice over: as a competitor (TORA-ONE targets warehousing, healthcare, industrial and commercial pilots) and as a supplier — its hands/sensors appear across other vendors' humanoids, giving it AgiBot-style ecosystem leverage. For an EU entrant, PaXini defines the tactile benchmark: any manipulation story weaker than thousands-of-taxels + 0.01 N force control will read as behind Chinese state of the art. Trajectory: scaling up on sensor volume; robot itself is lower-volume flagship/showcase.
