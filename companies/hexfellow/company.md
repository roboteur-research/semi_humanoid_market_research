## Company background
HexFellow Robotics Co., Ltd. is a Guangzhou (Nansha) company that integrates R&D, production and sale of "embodied robot platforms and modules" — force-controlled robotic arms and intelligent mobile platforms [third-party summary of Chinese company registry/press]. Its catalogue is a LEGO-style modular system: buyers combine omnidirectional or differential chassis (TRIGGER-A3 3-omni-wheel 50 kg/2 m/s; MAVER-X4/L4 powered-caster 80 kg/2 m/s; ARK diff-drive; PCW-25 powered caster wheel module), vertical lifts (IOTA, IOTA-P, ZETA), 6-axis arms (ARCHER-D6Y: 2 kg payload, 608 mm reach, 4.7 kg, ±0.1 mm, 48 V CAN/Ethernet; ARCHER-L6Y larger variant; SABER 7-axis; Firefly-Y6), GP-100 grippers (30 N, 100 mm span), in-house HEXMOTOR planetary joint actuators (48 V, 3/7 Nm, CAN-FD), power modules, IMUs, and the HEXCOMPUTER-P1 compute box — "assembly with only a screwdriver" [vendor-claimed].

Go-to-market is direct online sale (Shopify storefront, prices on request) aimed at embodied-AI researchers and data-collection teams worldwide, backed by unusually open software: Apache-2.0 GitHub repos (75 public), ROS 2 (Humble/Jazzy) and Python SDKs, and the browser-based XVIEW device tool. The company positions the packaged e³ configurations against ARX, AgileX and Galaxea in the exploding market for ALOHA-style bimanual data-collection rigs.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
HexFellow sells the ingredients of a semi-humanoid — and pre-configured dual-arm mobile robots (e³-Mobile, e³-Mobile-π) that are functionally wheeled semi-humanoids for research. The "π" naming of its flagship mobile configuration signals targeting of π0-class VLA workflows (Physical Intelligence's model), though no formal partnership is documented. As a components-plus-platform vendor with open SDKs and low-cost Chinese manufacturing, it lowers the entry barrier for labs and startups building wheeled dual-arm robots — commoditization pressure relevant to any EU hardware entrant.
