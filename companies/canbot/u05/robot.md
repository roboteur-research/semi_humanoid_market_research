# 优友 U05 (UU U05) — Canbot (康力优蓝)

> Legacy Chinese wheeled service humanoid (~2016): 1.28-1.3 m, ~22 DoF, three-wheel base, interaction-first (speech, face recognition, gesturing arms). Deployed in the hundreds across banks, halls, hospitals and malls in 2016-19 — an early proof of commercial demand for wheeled humanoids in China, now technologically outdated.

The U05 was Canbot's flagship "类人型" (human-like) commercial service robot: a wheeled humanoid whose arms exist for expressive gesturing rather than manipulation, with services spanning eight scenario packages (banking, exhibitions, education, retail guidance, hotels, healthcare, office, catering) [S1][S2]. Canbot claims deployments in the hundreds of units across public venues in 2016-19, with appearances at high-profile events (70th National Day celebrations, National Games opening, World Robot Conference, CES Asia 2019) [S1][S2]. The successor U06 "小哪吒" (August 2019) upgraded to 38 DoF and a wheel-leg structure mixing wheeled travel with gait walking for steps/complex terrain [S4] but did not visibly scale. As of 2026 the product line is legacy: no modern VLA/manipulation capability, and reseller channels show stale, out-of-stock listings [S3].
