## Company background
Beijing Canbot (brand 康力优蓝, "UUROBOT") is one of China's first-generation commercial service-robot companies, founded 2011 with backing that included elevator maker Canny Elevator [S2]. Its flagship 优友 (UU) U05, launched around 2016, was an early Chinese "类人型" (human-like) wheeled service humanoid — 1.28-1.3 m tall, ~22 DoF, three-wheel base — deployed by the hundreds in banks, exhibition halls, hospitals, airports, malls and government halls, and shown at events like the National Day 70th-anniversary festivities and CES Asia [S1][S3]. The follow-on U06 "小哪吒" (Aug 2019) moved to a 38-DoF wheel-leg hybrid structure combining wheeled motion with gait walking [S4]. The company's visibility has faded relative to the post-2024 humanoid wave; it persists in education/service niches.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Canbot is legacy evidence that wheeled reception/service humanoids found real (if shallow) commercial demand in China years before the current boom — and a cautionary tale: interaction-first robots with minimal manipulation capability plateaued. Its installed base and the U06's early wheel-leg experiment are historically notable but pose no competitive threat to modern manipulation-capable semi-humanoids.
