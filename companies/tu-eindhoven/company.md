## Company background
Tech United is TU Eindhoven's RoboCup team. Its domestic service robot AMIGO ("Autonomous Mate for IntelliGent Operations", ~2010) was a wheeled semi-humanoid built around soccer-robot technology: four omni wheels, a spindle-lift torso (1.00-1.35 m height), two Philips Experimental Robotic Arms (PERA, 7 DoF + gripper, 1.5 kg lift), Kinect + Hokuyo sensing, three onboard i5 PCs, ROS — published open-hardware (CERN OHL) except the Philips arms [S1][S2]. AMIGO won the Dutch Open 2012 and took 2nd at the RoboCup@Home world championship 2014 (Brazil) [S3]. Successor SERGIO (~2014-2017) refined the concept, but the team then migrated its software to a purchased Toyota HSR named HERO — winning @Home world titles 2019 and 2022 — and AMIGO/SERGIO were retired [S3][S4].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Historical note with two useful signals: (1) open-hardware semi-humanoid designs existed in Europe by 2011 (CAD published), lowering the excuse-barrier for platform development; (2) like Bonn's NimbRo, a top European team abandoned self-built hardware for a commercial platform (Toyota HSR) as soon as one existed — evidence of academic demand for supported, purchasable semi-humanoid platforms, a market later served by PAL Robotics and (until its discontinuation) Toyota.
