# Astribot T1 — Astribot / Stardust Intelligence (星尘智能)

> The T1 is Astribot's down-market wheeled humanoid: a 1.55 m, 23-DoF cable-driven robot launched in late May 2026 from ¥89,900 (~$13-14k) — roughly a fifth of the S1's price — aimed at seeding volume adoption in research, education, commercial services and light industrial data collection. It matters competitively as one of the first credible sub-$15k wheeled humanoids from a top-tier Chinese vendor, pushing the category's entry price toward cobot territory.

## Design & morphology
1.55 m tall (one aggregator says 1.53 m — flagged), ~66 kg, 23 DoF excluding end-effectors; humanoid torso on a compact wheeled base; aluminium-alloy frame with polymer covers; signature Astribot cable/tendon-driven (绳驱) transmission carried over from the S1, giving light, compliant, human-safe arms [S1][S2][S4]. Marketing tagline: compact/portable, low-threshold development, flexible deployment, "high dexterity + human-like expressiveness + interaction safety" [S2, vendor-claimed].

## Locomotion
Wheeled base, max speed ~5 km/h (1.4 m/s) [S1, third-party].

## Upper body & manipulation
Dual cable-driven arms, 5 kg payload each; interchangeable end-effectors — simple grippers or five-finger dexterous hands (16 DoF total, 8 per hand) [S1][S4]. Reach n/a (not disclosed).

## Sensing
Head with stereo camera unit (visible in imagery); base dome camera; details n/a (not disclosed) [images, estimated].

## Actuation & power
Cable/tendon transmission with torso-mounted motors; battery 4-6 h runtime, <1 h charge time [S1][S4, third-party/vendor-claimed].

## Compute & software
Onboard compute n/a (not disclosed). Trained via human demonstration data (imitation learning), consistent with the Astribot Suite teleop+learning stack; zero-code programming and SDK expected per S1 lineage [S1, third-party/estimated].

## Safety & compliance
"Interaction safety" marketed via low-inertia cable-driven arms; no published certifications [vendor-claimed].

## Deployment evidence & traction
First unit shipped 2026-06-01; mass production at a Wuxi factory reported from 2026-07-09 [S4, third-party]. Launch coincided with Astribot's Series B (>¥1B, unicorn valuation >¥10B) and joint-stock conversion, signalling pre-IPO scaling [S3]. No named T1 customers public yet; S1-era customers (JD.com, CCTV, elder-care, Thundersoft order) indicate the channel.

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* The T1 is the price-disruption play in wheeled humanoids: S1-derived cable-drive tech at ~$13k undercuts almost every dual-arm competitor and could commoditize the research/education segment quickly. Weaknesses: thin published specs, undisclosed compute, unproven durability of tendon drives at consumer price points, and no safety certification. For an EU entrant, T1 resets buyer price expectations — competing head-on on price is futile; differentiation must come via certification, service and integration. FCC 2026 import rules restrict its US path, increasing pressure on EU as Astribot's export market.
