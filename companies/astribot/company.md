## Company background
Stardust Intelligence (星尘智能), branded Astribot, was founded in Shenzhen in December 2022 by Lai Jie, formerly of Tencent's Robotics X lab. It became internationally known in April 2024 when the un-cut S1 demo video (1-second tasks: flipping, pouring, cup stacking, calligraphy) went viral; the S1 debuted physically at WRC 2024 (2024-08-21). The company's engineering signature is a cable/rope-driven (绳驱) transmission that moves heavy motors into the torso, giving very light, fast, human-compliant arms — the basis of its headline ≥10 m/s end-effector speed and 100 m/s² acceleration claims under "Design-for-AI" hardware-software co-design [vendor-claimed/third-party].

Astribot monetizes in two tiers: the S1 as a research/commercial platform (~¥500k research edition, quote-based B2B) sold since 08/2024 with batch production from mid-2025, and the cheaper T1 (from ¥89,900, launched 05/2026) to seed volume adoption. Software: whole-body imitation learning from human motion, VR teleoperation, zero-code visual programming, simulation support; the "Astribot Suite" teleop+learning stack was published 07/2025 [vendor-claimed/third-party]. Reported customers/deployments include JD.com, CCTV, elder-care institutions and retail across six Chinese cities, plus a reported thousand-unit order from Thundersoft; volume deliveries reported from late 2025 [third-party, partially unverified].

Financially, Ant Group is the marquee backer (A/A+ 2024, with Jinqiu Fund); cumulative 2026 fundraising passed RMB 1B with valuation above RMB 10B — "unicorn+" but an order of magnitude below AgiBot/Galbot war chests [third-party].

**Update 2026-08:** Series B of >¥1B closed 2026-06-03 (Bohua Capital among leads; three rounds in three months), confirming unicorn status at ~¥10B+ (~$1.4B). The company also completed conversion to a joint-stock company (股份制) — the customary final structural step before a Chinese IPO — signalling pre-IPO intent. T1 shipping from 06/2026 with volume production at a Wuxi factory from 07/2026 (see [t1/](t1/robot.md)) [third-party].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Astribot occupies the performance-manipulation niche: fastest claimed arm dynamics in the class, human-motion-native learning, and consumer-adjacent demos (cooking, ironing, calligraphy) that map to home/eldercare ambitions — WRC 2026 showed medication sorting. It is scaling from viral-demo startup to volume vendor (T1 price point ¥89,900 signals aggressive down-market intent). For an EU entrant, Astribot is the benchmark for dynamic manipulation and teleop-to-autonomy pipelines, though its safety/certification posture for high-speed arms around humans remains an open question.
