## Background
PRIN Lab is Taisuke Kobayashi's robot-learning lab at NII Tokyo. Its flagship hardware is a
self-integrated dual-arm mobile manipulator combining commercial components: two Franka
Panda arms, qbrobotics SoftHand2 Research hands, a TriOrb omnidirectional ball-wheel base
(Japanese startup), and an original arm lifter. Representative of how research groups
assemble semi-humanoids from the component ecosystem rather than buying integrated
platforms. [S1]
