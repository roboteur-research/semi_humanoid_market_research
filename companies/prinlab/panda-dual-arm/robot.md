# Panda Dual-arm Mobile Manipulator — PRIN Lab (NII, Japan)

> Research-tier semi-humanoid assembled from commercial parts: two Franka Panda 7-DoF
> torque-controlled arms with qb SoftHand2 Research anthropomorphic soft hands, mounted
> via an original lifter on a TriOrb omnidirectional base, topped with a head camera.
> A textbook example of the component-ecosystem route to a semi-humanoid (arms: Germany,
> hands: Italy, base: Japan) — and of TriOrb's ball-drive base entering research use.
> Demo video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G9THl5UjO4s

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Institution | PRIN Lab, NII Tokyo (PI Taisuke Kobayashi) |
| Status (2026) | research (in active use) |
| Components | 2× Franka Panda (7 DoF, 3 kg, 855 mm reach); 2× qb SoftHand2 Research (19 anthropomorphic DoF, 2 motors, adaptive synergy); TriOrb omnidirectional base; original arm lifter |
| Software | ROS-based (Panda FCI ecosystem) [estimated] |

Purpose: machine-learning-based whole-body control, bimanual manipulation and HRI
research. No published specs beyond component data; per-component figures inherit from
their vendors (Panda 3 kg/arm). [S1]
