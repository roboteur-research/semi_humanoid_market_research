## Company background
Robot Era is a Tsinghua spin-off founded in August 2023 by Chen Jianyu, a Berkeley-trained roboticist who became a Tsinghua IIIS assistant professor at 28. The company claims to be the industry's only fully full-stack self-developed embodied-AI player across hardware and software ("软硬件全栈自研"), pairing its own actuators, dexterous hands (XHAND1: 12 active DoF, full direct-drive, >1,000 operations) and robots (biped L7, wheeled Q5) with the ERA-42 end-to-end VLA model built on a world-model, fast-slow layered architecture [S1][S2].

Commercially it moved unusually fast: >200 units delivered, hundreds of orders in fulfillment, cumulative orders reportedly >RMB 500M, and the vendor claims 9 of the world's top-10 tech companies by market cap as customers (unverified) [S2]. Funding tracked the momentum — ~RMB 500M Series A (Jul 2025) co-led by Haier Capital, ~RMB 1B A+ (Nov 2025) led by Geely, and a 2026 Series C [S3]. The Haier investment produced the most visible OEM deal in the wheeled-humanoid space: Haier's HIVA (海娃) home-service robot, a customized Robot Era Q5 integrated with the Haier Smart Home cloud ecosystem, jointly developed by the two companies and unveiled July 2025 [S4].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Robot Era demonstrates the "platform + OEM brand" route to scale: instead of building consumer channels, it lets Haier (world's #1 appliance brand by volume) put its Q5 into homes/retail under the HIVA brand, while it sells Q5 directly into malls, museums and hotels and L7/XHAND into research. Its 44-DoF Q5 with 11-DoF hands is among the most dexterous commercial wheeled humanoids. For an EU entrant, Robot Era is a template of the OEM threat: European appliance/retail brands could be captured as channels by Chinese platform makers before EU robot vendors reach them. Trajectory: scaling rapidly; auto-industry investors (Geely, BAIC) hint at future factory applications.
