# ibuki (イブキ) — Osaka University Ishiguro Lab / JST ERATO

> Research android, 2018–ongoing: a childlike android (modeled on a ~10-year-old, ~120 cm) mounted on a wheeled mobility unit — the mobile counterpart to the lab's static ERICA/Geminoid androids. 46–47 DoF including expressive face and hands; the base pairs drive wheels with an eccentric-wheel + ball-screw vertical oscillation mechanism so the android reproduces human center-of-mass sway ("gait") while rolling. Pure HRI research; never commercialized.

ibuki was built in the JST ERATO Ishiguro Symbiotic Human-Robot Interaction Project as the lab's answer to a core limitation of its androids: ERICA and the Geminoids could not move through the world. The wheeled unit is deliberately more than a cart — its vertical-oscillation mechanism recreates the rise and fall of a walking child's center of mass, so gestures and "gait" read as human even on wheels [S1][S2]. The android converses, gestures and forms facial expressions, and has appeared in demonstrations including hand-holding walks and (post-2020) telepresence/avatar studies within Ishiguro's Moonshot avatar-symbiosis program [S1][S3]. As a hand-built research artifact it has no price, deployment or industrialization path; its market relevance is as the extreme "appearance-first" pole of the semi-humanoid design space and a source of published data on public reaction to mobile androids [S2].
