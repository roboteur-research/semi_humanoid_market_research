## Company background
Hiroshi Ishiguro's Intelligent Robotics Laboratory at Osaka University (with ATR's Hiroshi Ishiguro Laboratories) is the world's best-known android research group — Geminoid, ERICA, Telenoid. Under the JST ERATO Ishiguro Symbiotic Human-Robot Interaction Project it built ibuki (2018), its first android with mobility: a child-size upper body on a wheeled base, intended to study how a moving, gesturing android forms relationships with people in ordinary environments [S1][S2].

The lab's outputs are research artifacts, not products; commercialization paths run indirectly (Vstone for CommU/Sota, AVITA Inc. — Ishiguro's avatar company — for CG/telepresence avatars). Android hardware itself has historically been fabricated with partners such as A-Lab/Kokoro.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
No commercial threat; relevant as the scientific reference for acceptance/HRI questions that commercial semi-humanoids face (appearance, motion naturalness, dialogue embodiment), and as a source of talent and licensable know-how in expressive upper bodies on wheeled bases.
