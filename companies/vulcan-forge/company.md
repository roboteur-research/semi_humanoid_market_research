Early-stage open-hardware project building Sourccey, a low-cost personal home robot:
open CAD (CERN-OHL-S-2.0), electrical and software repos, plus a LeRobot-compatible fork
(lerobot-vulcan) for training workflows. Public releases rolling out from Dec 2025;
hardware-kit docs "coming soon" as of mid-2026. Team/location not formally disclosed. [S1][S2]
