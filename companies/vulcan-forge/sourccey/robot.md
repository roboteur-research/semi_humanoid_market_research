# Sourccey — Vulcan Forge  (early development)

> Open-source personal home robot in early development: R2-D2-styled 3D-printed
> barrel body with stereo-camera dome head, TWO articulated arms with grippers, and a
> mecanum-wheel base (unit #005 photographed publicly). Fully open: CAD (CERN-OHL-S-2.0),
> electrical, desktop software and a LeRobot-compatible fork. The community/DIY end of
> the semi-humanoid spectrum, alongside XLeRobot/BamBot/Nyro — but with a distinct
> consumer-character design ambition.

| Field | Value |
