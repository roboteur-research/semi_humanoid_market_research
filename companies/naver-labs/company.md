## Company background
NAVER LABS is the robotics/AI research subsidiary of NAVER Corp, Korea's dominant internet company. Its strategy is distinctive: rather than selling robots, it builds a **cloud-native robot ecosystem** — robots stream sensor data over private 5G to **ARC** (AI-Robot-Cloud), which holds the building's digital twin and does the heavy computation ("brainless robots") [S3][S4]. The flagship showcase is **1784**, NAVER's second HQ in Bundang (opened 2022, named after its lot address and the Industrial Revolution year), certified as Korea's first "robot-friendly building": ~100 Rookie delivery robots share elevators and corridors with 5,000 staff, using 316+ patented technologies [S3]. NAVER has marketed this building-scale robotics package internationally (e.g. pitches to Saudi Arabia's NEOM) and in 2026 announced tooling to become "the Android of robotics" — a service-design platform for third-party robot operators [S5].

**AMBIDEX** is NAVER LABS' manipulation research platform: a cable-driven dual-7-DoF-arm upper-body robot developed with KOREATECH (Korea University of Technology and Education) since 2017, famous for arms lighter than a human's (~2.6 kg), 5G "brainless" teleoperation demos (CES 2019 with Qualcomm), haptic-device task teaching, and dish-washing/vegetable-peeling learning demos [S1][S2]. In 1784 it was given a real job: cleaning/disinfecting the Rookie fleet [S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
NAVER LABS is not selling a semi-humanoid and shows no productization path for AMBIDEX; its relevance is as (a) a technology benchmark for lightweight, intrinsically safe cable-driven arms + haptic imitation teaching, and (b) the most advanced real-world example of building-scale robot infrastructure (ARC/5G/digital twin) that any deployed semi-humanoid fleet would plug into. Trajectory: research + platform/ecosystem play, not hardware sales — a potential partner or infrastructure competitor rather than a direct hardware rival.
