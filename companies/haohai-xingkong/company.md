## Company background
Haohai Xingkong ("Vast Sea, Starry Sky"; HHS Robotics) is a Shanghai Minhang embodied-AI startup founded in May 2025 that went from incorporation to operating a public retail robot in about nine months. Its formula is the "Starry Space Cabin" (星空舱): a 24/7 unmanned retail pod staffed by the wheeled humanoid Juliet, which picks ordered goods from shelves and hands them to customers. The first cabin opened on Nanjing Road Pedestrian Street (Century Square) on 2026-02-13 with lifestyle brand Tayohya (多样屋), selling CCTV Spring-Festival-Gala IP merchandise; over the ~10-day Spring Festival run it drew 61,198 visitors, completed 2,368 orders and sold 3,082 items [S2][S3]. At WAIC 2026 the company ran an official out-of-venue experience point at Expo Gate 1 — a robot retail cabin with 1,000+ SKUs served fully autonomously [S1].

The company claims full-stack self-development: "Starry Brain" embodied model (99% semantic-parsing accuracy, <200 ms latency claims), proprietary joint modules, and the Stellar data platform running a data flywheel (real-robot data → QC at ≥95% recall → training → redeployment). It fields four form factors (biped Romeo, wheeled Juliet, dual-arm Xingchen A1, quadruped Xingyun) across education, commercial, consumer and special-purpose domains, and claims 100,000-unit-scale mass-production capability — an aggressive vendor claim for a 2025 startup [S1][S3, vendor-claimed].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Juliet is one of the clearest revenue-generating retail deployments of a wheeled humanoid anywhere: real transactions, real customers, public location, replicated at WAIC. The pod-plus-robot format (own the venue, control the scene) sidesteps the unstructured-environment problem that stalls general-purpose humanoids. For an EU entrant, this is the template to watch for retail/brand-activation go-to-market; the company itself is young, unproven at scale, and so far China-only.
