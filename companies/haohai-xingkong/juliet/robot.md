# Juliet (朱丽叶) — Haohai Xingkong / HHS Robotics

> Juliet is a wheeled composite humanoid that works as an autonomous retail clerk inside Haohai Xingkong's "Starry Space Cabin" (星空舱) pods: customers order on a touchscreen/tablet, Juliet drives to the shelf, identifies the item, grasps it and hands it over. Deployed on Shanghai's Nanjing Road since 2026-02-13 and at the WAIC 2026 official experience zone, it is a rare example of a semi-humanoid completing paid retail transactions in public — a scene-controlled go-to-market other vendors will copy.
