# KP-BOT — Asimov Robotics

> KP-BOT is a life-size, female-presenting wheeled humanoid built by Asimov Robotics with Kerala Police's Cyberdome R&D and inducted in February 2019 as India's first police "RoboCop", with the ceremonial rank of sub-inspector. It did front-office duty at Kerala Police HQ (greeting visitors, collecting details, guiding to departments, issuing visitor IDs) for ~INR 21.24 lakh (~$25.5k). Competitively it is a market-history datapoint: a PR-successful but operationally marginal reception semi-humanoid that was quietly withdrawn to training/research duty.

Design: life-size humanoid upper body styled as an Indian woman (bindi, khaki uniform) on a concealed wheeled base; gesturing arms; voice interaction with visitor-recognition features [S2][S3]. Promised (largely undelivered) roadmap features included Malayalam NLU, emotion assessment, metal detection, thermal imaging, gas sensing and oral complaint filing [S3]. No DoF/payload/sensing specs were published. Sibling products: Sayabot ("Saya"), the underlying commercial humanoid-torso-on-wheels platform (INR 20-30 lakh, banks/hospitals/hotels; customers incl. TCS, Samsung, HDFC Bank) and "Maitri", a related service/assistive humanoid [S1]. Deployment evidence: single unit at Police HQ 2019-~2021 (also shown to PM Modi in 2020), limited COVID-era use, then repurposed — The South First concluded Kerala Police "may have overestimated its practical utility" [S3] (third-party).
