## Company background
Asimov Robotics is a small Kochi-based robotics firm founded in 2012 by Jayakrishnan T. Its core service-robot product is Sayabot ("Saya"), a humanoid torso on a wheeled base sold customized at INR 20-30 lakh (~$25-38k) for hospitals, banks, hotels, airports, offices and retail; corporate customers cited include TCS, Cognizant, Accenture, Samsung, HCL, DRDO and HDFC Bank [S1]. Its highest-profile deployment is KP-BOT, India's first "RoboCop", a female-presenting wheeled humanoid inducted as a sub-inspector by Kerala Police in February 2019 (INR 21.24 lakh) [S2][S3]. The company also built "Maitri" and around 2018 stated plans to refocus on healthcare/caregiving robots [S1].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A representative small-volume Indian service-robot integrator: low price points, front-office/reception use cases, bespoke builds rather than a product line. KP-BOT's quiet withdrawal from public duty (relocated to Kerala Police Cyberdome for training/research by 2025) is a useful cautionary datapoint on reception-humanoid utility. No competitive threat to an EU industrial entrant; relevant mainly for market-history and India-market color.
