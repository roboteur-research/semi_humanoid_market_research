## Company background
Neuromeka is a KOSDAQ-listed Korean collaborative-robot maker (founded 2013, CEO Park Jong-hoon) known for its Indy cobot line and a full-stack in-house approach spanning actuators, safety-certified controllers, vision AI and imitation-learning software. In late 2025 it announced EIR (에이르), a "Physical AI humanoid platform", unveiled at CES 2026 (Jan 6-9, Las Vegas) in the HUMANOID M.AX Alliance pavilion [S1][S2]. EIR is explicitly industrial: an 18-DoF upper-body-centric dual-arm torso (7 DoF per arm + 4 DoF neck/waist) on a cart-based wheeled mobile base, with 100 µm-level precision plus compliance control, powered by Neuromeka's VisionX (vision-foundation-model perception) and MimicX (imitation learning) platforms; phased production was slated from March 2026 [S1].

By mid-2026 Neuromeka reported completed verification of assembly tasks (cable insertion, precision assembly) on electric-motor production lines, field trials with Hwasin Precision, Ajin Industries and SL under Daegu City's Megacity Project, automation supply to Kolmar Korea, and participation in a national surgical-assistance humanoid project; a July 2026 strategy update emphasized scaling EIR and shifting to recurring-revenue models [S3][S4].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Neuromeka is part of the 2026 Korean wave (Rainbow Robotics RB-Y1, Doosan, I.L, T-Robotics) of listed incumbents entering wheeled dual-arm humanoids from a cobot base — bringing safety-certified controllers, existing industrial channels and manufacturing discipline rather than startup demos. Its cart-based, precision-first EIR positioning (100 µm, compliance control) targets exactly the assembly niches an EU entrant would pursue; watch its Daegu automotive-supplier trials as a template.
