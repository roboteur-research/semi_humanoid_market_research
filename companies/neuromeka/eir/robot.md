# EIR (에이르) — Neuromeka

> EIR is Neuromeka's "Physical AI humanoid platform": an 18-DoF dual-arm upper body (7 DoF per arm, 4 DoF neck/waist) on a cart-based wheeled mobile base, unveiled at CES 2026 and built for direct deployment on real production lines rather than research demos. From a KOSDAQ-listed cobot incumbent, it competes on assembly-grade precision (100 µm claim) plus compliance control — squarely in the industrial niche EU semi-humanoid entrants target.

Summary: wheeled cart-based lower body chosen explicitly over legs for rapid, installation-free deployment across industrial sites [S1, vendor-claimed]. Upper body: 18 DoF total — dual 7-DoF arms + 4-DoF neck/waist — replicating human upper-body motion; 100-micrometer-level precision with simultaneous compliance control for narrow, contact-rich assembly work [S1]. CES photos show Tesollo Delto grippers and AIDIN F/T sensors at the wrists (Korean component ecosystem; same Tesollo hands used by Roboligent) [images, estimated]. Software: VisionX (vision-foundation-model perception), MimicX (imitation learning), Sim2Real policy models with zero-shot claims for new objects/environments; controller is Neuromeka's in-house safety-certified cobot controller from the Indy line [S1][S3]. Traction: assembly verification completed on electric-motor production lines; field trials with Hwasin Precision, Ajin Industries and SL (Daegu Megacity Project); Kolmar Korea automation; national surgical-assistance humanoid project [S3, third-party].
