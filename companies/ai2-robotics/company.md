## Company background
AI² Robotics (智平方, "Zhipingfang") was founded in April 2023 by Dr. Guo Yandong, one of the few Chinese founders combining big-tech AI research credentials (Microsoft, OPPO, XPENG autonomous driving) with hardware productization experience. The company's framing is "AGI terminal" — general-purpose robots positioned as the next universal device after smartphones and smart cars, built on a "Model × Hardware × Scenario" integration thesis [S1].

Its core asset is the AlphaBrain embodied foundation model with GOVLA (Global & Omni-body Vision-Language-Action) architecture: a dual-system design (slow System-2 reasoning incl. DeepSeek integration for task decomposition; fast System-1 for real-time whole-body motion and navigation) that drives not just the arms but torso lift and base trajectory — the company explicitly contrasts this "full-domain, full-body VLA" with "US Bay Area conventional VLA" that only outputs arm actions [S2][S5]. In June 2025 it open-sourced FiS-VLA, claiming ~30% better results than the π0 benchmark [S1].

Commercially, AI² Robotics is among the furthest-along Chinese wheeled-humanoid vendors: a self-built production line came online in September 2025 (annual capacity in the thousands), monthly deliveries of AlphaBot 2 passed 100 units in December 2025, and stated targets are 10,000-unit scale in 2026-2028 and 1 million units by 2033 [S3][S6]. Funding velocity is exceptional — roughly 12 rounds inside a year, culminating in a February 2026 Series B of over RMB 1 billion with Baidu and CRRC among investors at a >RMB 10 billion valuation [S3][S4]. **Update 07/2026:** a further ~$735M raise was reported at a ~$2.8-3B valuation, keeping AI² Robotics among the best-capitalized wheeled-humanoid vendors globally [third-party, freshness sweep 2026-08].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A top-tier Chinese threat: real (if early) recurring deployments in automotive plants, Geely-affiliated Jingneng Microelectronics semiconductor fab (loading/unloading, inter-line transport since 03/2025), and Bloomage Biotech (华熙生物) sterile-cleanroom logistics/inspection — three regulated, high-cleanliness environments that most competitors have not entered [S2][S7]. Trajectory is aggressively scaling up: own factory, triple-digit monthly shipments, state-linked capital (CRRC, SDIC), and a proprietary VLA stack. For an EU entrant, AI² Robotics is a benchmark for "model + own hardware + vertical scenario" integration speed.
