# Robovie-R3 (ロボビー・アール3) — Vstone / ATR lineage

> Long-lived research platform, still sold: a 1.08 m, 35 kg wheeled humanoid descended from ATR's Robovie HRI series, sold by Vstone since 2010 at ~¥3.8M for human-robot-interaction research and service trials in care facilities, hospitals and shops. Gesture-grade 4-DoF arms with 1-DoF grippers, 17 DoF total, differential-drive base (~2.5 km/h). Competitively irrelevant as a product, but the archetype of the Japanese academic wheeled-humanoid channel.

Robovie-R3 is the commercial embodiment of ATR's Robovie research line, the platform behind much of the foundational Japanese HRI literature (field trials in schools, shopping malls, museums through the 2000s). Vstone's version keeps the child-adjacent height and soft styling that the research showed maximizes approachability, adds a battery-powered diff-drive base sized for crowded indoor spaces, and prices it as lab equipment (~¥3.8M) [S1]. Arms are for gesture and light grasping only — there is no manipulation payload spec — so its role in this market study is contextual: it demonstrates the durable but small academic demand (universities, care-facility trials) that Japanese wheeled semi-humanoids served before commercial-grade manipulation arrived, and it remains purchasable in 2026 while nearly all its 2005-wave contemporaries are dead [S1]. Vstone lists Robovie-R4 as the newer model.
