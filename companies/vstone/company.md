## Company background
Vstone is an Osaka robotics SME that commercializes research robots originating in Japan's HRI community, above all ATR's Robovie series — the reference platform of 2000s human-robot-interaction studies. Vstone manufactures and sells the wheeled Robovie-R line (R3 since 2010, ~¥3.8M) to universities, care facilities and retail-service researchers, alongside the widely used Sota and CommU desktop social robots co-developed with ATR/Osaka University [S1][S2]. The company also runs a robot-parts webshop and education business, giving it stable revenue unusual among Japanese robot SMEs.

Vstone remains active (Robovie-R3 still listed; Robovie-R4 offered as the newer model), making it one of the few continuous suppliers of wheeled humanoid HRI platforms worldwide — though at research-tool volumes, not commercial service deployment [S1].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Niche but persistent: Vstone owns the Japanese academic HRI channel for child-size wheeled humanoids. No threat as a commercial service-robot vendor (no manipulation payload, no autonomy stack), but a bellwether for Japanese research demand and a potential partner/OEM for anyone selling into Japanese universities.
