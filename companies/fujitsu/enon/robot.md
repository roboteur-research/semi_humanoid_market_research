# enon (エノン) — Fujitsu Frontech / Fujitsu Laboratories

> Discontinued first-wave service robot (2005–~2012): a 1.3 m, ~50 kg wheeled dual-arm robot for guidance, guest escorting, object transport and security patrol in offices and commercial facilities. It carried up to 10 kg in a torso cargo compartment but only 0.5 kg per 5-DoF arm — physical-utility limits typical of its generation. Sold "on a limited basis" in Japan from September 2005; quietly withdrawn with no successor.

Enon ("exciting nova on network") was Fujitsu's entry in the 2005 Japanese service-robot wave, differentiated by multi-tasking — one platform for guidance, escorting, delivery and patrol, rather than a single-function machine [S1]. Notable engineering details: a swivel head/arm assembly so the robot faces users with its LCD even while driving the other way, and third-party safety appraisal by the NPO Safety Engineering Laboratory during launch [S1]. Deployments were pilot-scale: shopping centers, showrooms, and the Kyotaro Nishimura Museum guide role (2007) [S2]. The line disappeared from Fujitsu Frontech's portfolio around 2010–2012 with unit volumes never disclosed (last documented public activity 2009–2010) [S3]. Commercially it shares the wakamaru failure pattern: manipulation too weak to justify cost, so value collapsed onto kiosk-like functions a screen could do cheaper. The discovery note "10 kg payload" refers to the cargo bay, not the arms.
