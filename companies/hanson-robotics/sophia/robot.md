# Sophia — Hanson Robotics

> Sophia is the world's most famous social android: a human-scale robot with Frubber synthetic skin, 60+ facial expressions, expressive 15-DoF arm/hand units and a wheeled tripod base (with optional autonomous mobile base and experimental legs). She is a media/ambassador and research platform rather than a working service robot — competitively significant for brand power and public perception of humanoids, not for task capability.

## Design & morphology
Human-scale android: height ~167 cm, weight ~20 kg (light due to shell construction); 83 total DoF in full configuration — 36 head/neck, 15 per arm+hand, 3 torso, 14 in the mobile base [S2][S3]. Face: patented Frubber elastomer skin actuated by small servos, 60+ expressions; transparent skull cap deliberately exposes head mechatronics [S1][S2]. Base: standard non-motorized wheeled tripod; options for remote-controlled wheeled base or autonomous self-navigating mobile base [S3]. Experimental bipedal legs (DRC-HUBO collaboration, 2018 CES demo) were a one-off research add-on, not a product [S4].

## Locomotion
Wheeled tripod (passive or motorized); autonomous variant self-navigates indoors at walking pace (speed n/a, not disclosed). Not designed for payload transport or uneven terrain [S3].

## Upper body & manipulation
Two arms, 15 DoF each including articulated five-finger hands; used for gestures, handshakes, holding light objects (payload not rated, effectively <0.5 kg, estimated), and drawing/painting (Sophia's NFT artwork sold for $688k in 2021) [S2][S4]. No tool interface, no force control disclosed.

## Sensing
Cameras in eyes plus a chest RealSense-class depth camera (visible in recent photos as a chest sensor bar); microphones; face tracking and recognition, natural-language interaction [S2][S3].

## Actuation & power
Small electric servos throughout (facial servos exploit Frubber's low actuation force); battery/runtime n/a (not disclosed; event units often tethered/swapped, estimated).

## Compute & software
Hybrid AI stack: scripted performance dialogue + chatbot frameworks + perception (face/emotion recognition), historically OpenCog/SingularityNET components under chief scientist Ben Goertzel; recent versions integrate LLMs for open conversation [S2][S4]. Partially open: Hanson published the "Sophia blockchain/SDK" experiments and Hanson AI SDK to research customers. Teleop/wizard-of-Oz modes used in performances (a core point of the authenticity criticism) [S6].

## Safety & compliance
n/a — no service-robot safety certifications published; operates as supervised exhibit/research hardware.

## Deployment evidence & traction
Small install base: R&D units at universities and labs (Sophia 2020 R&D version distributed via RobotShop), custom units, plus the touring flagship(s) — appearances in 65+ countries, UN/ITU AI for Good fixture including "Sophia and Robert" with RB Labs (July 2026), Brazil event circuit 2024 (see images) [S2][S5]. Milestones: Saudi citizenship (2017), UNDP Innovation Champion (2017), 2021 mass-production announcement ("thousands in 2021") that never visibly materialized (analyst note), sister android Grace in eldercare pilots (Awakening Health) [S4][S5]. Confidence: media traction third-party verified; unit numbers undisclosed and small (estimated: low tens to ~100 heads/units cumulative).

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* Strengths: unrivaled brand recognition, best-in-class facial expressiveness (Frubber remains state of the art), a durable events/appearance revenue niche. Weaknesses: essentially no manipulation or autonomy relevant to service work; credibility damaged among technologists by scripted-AI controversies; production scale never demonstrated. Threat to a new EU semi-humanoid entrant: negligible commercially. Strategic lesson: hyper-realistic faces buy media attention but invite backlash and don't convert into deployable service value — an EU entrant should note the reputational dynamics Sophia created around "overpromising humanoids."
