## Company background
TwoLabs is a two-person, 2026-founded San Francisco startup in Y Combinator's
Spring 2026 cohort building Tobi, a semi-humanoid caregiving robot for nursing
homes, senior living and assisted-living facilities. Against the industry's
warehouse/logistics mainstream, TwoLabs deliberately targets elder care:
feeding, dressing, medication reminders, object retrieval, walking support with
wayfinding, and companionship, with facility-system integration. Brand language
stresses that Tobi "moves quietly, speaks gently" and prioritizes "safety,
reliability, and warmth over flashy specs" [S1][S2].

The YC-standard playbook applies: minimal team, prototype hardware (mid-2026),
a $50k target price, and a modular data-collection/skill-training platform
pitch underneath the care use case [S1][S2].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Relevant as a signal, not a competitor: YC is now funding wheeled care
humanoids, validating elder-care as a semi-humanoid beachhead — a segment
directly relevant in aging European markets (and one a German entrant could
credibly own with certification advantages). At 2 employees and prototype
stage, execution risk is extreme; watch for a seed round or first facility
pilots as the real signal.
