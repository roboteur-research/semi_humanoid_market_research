## Company background
RT Corporation is a Tokyo (Akihabara) robotics SME founded in September 2005 by roboticist Yukiko Nakagawa under the slogan "Life with Robot". It grew out of the hobby/education robotics scene and still runs a significant education business (ROS training platforms, robot kits used widely in Japanese universities), alongside contract AI/robotics development. RT Corporation Forward was merged into the main entity in 2018. [S1][S2]

Its two business pillars relevant here are (a) research dual-arm humanoids — the Sciurus17 upper-body robot (2018, ¥3.3M) that became a de-facto standard Japanese lab platform for dual-arm manipulation and imitation-learning research — and (b) food-factory automation with Foodly, a human-sized dual-arm collaborative robot that picks bulk foodstuffs into bento boxes using deep-learning vision (announced FOOMA 2019, production model early 2021). RT designs, develops and manufactures in-house and sells direct, with a monthly rental option and a "hybrid staffing" model (dispatching pre-trained staff together with robots via a 2020 partnership with Will of Factory/Will of Work). [S1][S3][S4]

In June 2025 RT extended Sciurus17 with the Sciurus Lift (70cm vertical axis) and ROS 2 integration with Preferred Robotics' Kachaka Pro mobile base, turning the static torso into a mobile semi-humanoid research platform — an inexpensive Japanese analogue to Western mobile-manipulation research rigs. [S5]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
RT matters in two niches: it owns the most concrete Japanese case of humanoid-form robots doing paid production work in food factories (Foodly, deployed at bento/side-dish makers such as Hirai and Ichibiki), and it supplies the research market from which Japanese semi-humanoid talent and software emerge (Sciurus17 + ROS/ROS 2 open packages). It is a niche SME, not a scaling threat, but Foodly's "800 meals/hour, works beside humans on existing lines" positioning is a reference point for food-sector semi-humanoid economics. [S3][S4]
