# Songyi (松羿) — Siasun

> Songyi is Siasun's wheeled humanoid: a self-developed dual-arm execution system on the company's mature AMR/wheeled mobile platform, debuted at the World Robot Conference in Beijing (August 2025). Aimed at warehouse peak-season handling, new retail and household service, it is the state-backed incumbent's entry into the semi-humanoid category — light on published specs, heavy on channel credibility, and backed by Siasun's two decades of composite-robot (arm-on-AMR) experience.

## Design & morphology
Humanoid upper body (head + dual arms) on Siasun's wheeled mobile platform. Height, weight and DoF counts not published (n/a, not disclosed). Demo units at WRC 2025 show a white torso robot with two ~6-7-DoF arms and multi-finger/gripper end-effectors performing tabletop manipulation [S1, vendor images; DoF estimated].

## Locomotion
"Mature wheeled mobile platform with excellent maneuvering ability" — Siasun's AMR heritage implies lidar-SLAM differential or omni drive [S1, vendor-claimed; drive type estimated]. Speed/climbing n/a (not disclosed).

## Upper body & manipulation
Self-developed dual-arm system supporting parallel multi-task execution; grasping, transporting, sorting and stacking objects at different heights via intelligent motion planning and high-precision control algorithms [S1][S2, vendor-claimed]. Payload/reach/repeatability for Songyi itself n/a; Siasun's related stationary dual-arm platform claims ±0.05 mm repeatability [S2]. End-effector details n/a.

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* Strengths: unmatched deployment channel (industrial/logistics integration business, state procurement), proven AMR base technology, in-house arms, and a listed company's balance sheet; the composite-robot install base gives it a natural upgrade path to sell semi-humanoids. Weaknesses: spec-free launch suggests early prototype maturity; Siasun's recent track record in fast-moving product categories is conservative, and it faces far more agile startups (Galaxea, Robot Era) on AI software. Threat to an EU entrant: low today, but high structurally in China — and moderate in export markets where Siasun's industrial channels reach; watch for a productized Songyi with real specs in 2026-27.
