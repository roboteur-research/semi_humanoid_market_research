## Company background
Siasun is China's legacy robotics champion: a CAS Shenyang Institute of Automation spin-off (2000) that became the country's largest home-grown robot maker across industrial arms, AGVs/AMRs and automation lines, and the first Chinese robot company to list (ChiNext 300024, 2009). It effectively invented the Chinese 复合机器人 "composite robot" category — a cobot arm on an AMR base — giving it two decades of exactly the ingredients (mobile platforms + arms + integration business) that wheeled humanoids recombine [third-party].

In 2025 Siasun pushed into embodied AI with a full lineup shown at the World Robot Conference (Beijing, 2025-08-08/12, booth A114): the 松羿 (Songyi) wheeled humanoid on its mature AMR platform, the RICO wheeled humanoid (autonomous navigation, dual-arm compliance control), a high-precision stationary dual-arm platform (±0.05 mm repeatability), and the 松行 (Songxing) biped — plus a joint embodied-intelligence launch with AI-compute firm ALVA (阿依瓦) [vendor-claimed, WRC materials]. Go-to-market runs through its established industrial/logistics integration business and state-affiliated channels.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Siasun matters because it owns the deployment channel wheeled humanoids need: thousands of factory/warehouse AMR installations, national service networks, and state procurement access. Its semi-humanoids are conservative (prototype-stage, spec-shy) but sit on proven mobile bases — the classic incumbent play of upgrading composite robots into humanoid-form platforms. For an EU entrant, Siasun is a bellwether: when it productizes Songyi/RICO at scale, wheeled humanoids will be sold as line items inside standard Chinese intralogistics contracts.
