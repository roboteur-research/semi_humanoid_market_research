# Northstar — UMA

> Northstar is the first robot from Paris startup UMA, founded by LeRobot creator and ex-Tesla Optimus AI scientist Rémi Cadène. **Base type verified: the initial platform ("Version 0", unveiled July 2026) is WHEELED** — a lightweight (~40 kg) dual-arm mobile humanoid for warehouse/assembly pilots — with a bipedal version only on the later roadmap, which explains the conflicting "bipedal" mention in one discovery source. Its competitive significance is the software: real-time learning-from-demonstration built on the LeRobot lineage, backed by ~$40M seed money.

## Design & morphology
Humanoid torso with dual arms on a **wheeled mobile base** (V0); lightweight design ~40 kg total — deliberately lighter than Tesla Optimus Gen 3 (57 kg) or Atlas (90 kg) — and engineered for repairability [S2][S3]. Discovery-sweep figures (unverified in accessible press): height ~165 cm, 24 DoF, five-finger hands [S5]. Two product variants planned: dual-arm mobile industrial robot; compact humanoid for hospitals/labs/homes [S4].

## Locomotion
Wheeled base (drive type not disclosed). Company roadmap: transition to bipedal locomotion after wheeled proof-of-concept units validate the software stack with industrial clients (end-2026 "Version Zero" deliveries) [S2 via search reporting]. **Conflict resolution: "wheeled" is correct for the current/initial product; "bipedal" refers to the future roadmap.**

## Upper body & manipulation
Dual arms with five-finger hands (per discovery sweep; 15 kg lift claim likewise sweep-sourced, unconfirmed) [S5]. At the July 2026 demo the robot performed observation-based gesture learning, not heavy manipulation [S3].

## Sensing
n/a (not disclosed). Cameras for demonstration-observation learning implied [S3].

## Actuation & power
n/a. V0 prototype was powered by a thick external cable at unveiling (no onboard battery demonstrated) [S3].

## Compute & software
Core differentiator: real-time learning system — the robot adapts movements based on what it observes (learning from human demonstration), built on the open-source **LeRobot** toolkit lineage that Cadène created at Hugging Face [S3][S4]. Software-first, data-engine strategy analogous to Tesla Autopilot practice.

## Safety & compliance
None published (prototype).

## Deployment evidence & traction
- No deployments yet. ~50 potential customers evaluating use cases (vendor-claimed); pilots in logistics and manufacturing announced for 2026, healthcare mentioned [S1][S2][S4].
- Prototype built in 9 months by a small Paris team; tethered at unveiling; press assessment: hardware "far behind" the AI ambition [S1][S3].
- ~$40M seed (Greycroft, Relentless, Red River West, Factorial, Kima, Unity Growth; angels Xavier Niel, Nico Rosberg, Olivier Pomel; LeCun/Wolf advising) [S1][S2][S4].

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* Strengths: arguably Europe's best embodied-AI founding team (Tesla Optimus + LeRobot), exceptional seed capital, Europe-first positioning that resonates with sovereignty-minded industrial buyers, and a pragmatic wheels-before-legs sequence. Weaknesses: hardware maturity is far behind (tethered V0, no published specs), no deployments, and the two-variant + biped roadmap risks focus dilution for a small team. Threat to a new EU entrant: high in the medium term — UMA will compete for the same French/EU pilot customers, engineers and VC money, and its LeRobot community gravity could make its data/learning stack a de-facto standard. Near term, its immaturity is a window of opportunity.
