## Company background
UMA was founded in Paris in 2025 by Rémi Cadène, the creator and lead of Hugging Face's LeRobot open-source robot-learning library and previously ~3 years at Tesla in the Autopilot group working on AI for driver assistance and Optimus. He left Hugging Face to build "Europe's humanoid robot company", explicitly Europe-first before US/Asia expansion [S1][S2]. Co-founders include ex-Hugging Face engineer Simon Alibert and robot designer Rob Knight. The startup emerged from stealth December 2025 and unveiled its first prototype, **Northstar (Version 0)**, on 7 July 2026 at the Machina Summit in Paris — designed and assembled in Paris in nine months, still tethered by a power cable at demo [S1][S3].

Strategy: software-first physical AI. The learning stack builds on LeRobot lineage, emphasizing real-time learning from demonstration — the robot adapting its movements from what it observes — rather than hand-engineered autonomy [S3]. Hardware is deliberately lightweight (~40 kg vs Optimus Gen 3's 57 kg) and repairable; the go-to-market starts with a **wheeled** dual-arm platform for warehouses/assembly, with a bipedal transition and a compact hospital/lab/home variant on the roadmap [S2][S4]. UMA claims a pipeline of ~50 potential customers evaluating use cases and plans logistics/manufacturing (and possibly healthcare) pilots in 2026 [S1][S2]. Reported seed ~$40M makes it one of Europe's best-funded semi-humanoid starts at day one.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
UMA is the most credible new European challenger in AI-first wheeled humanoids: top-tier embodied-AI pedigree (Tesla + LeRobot), marquee backers, Paris talent pool, and a wheels-first pragmatism identical to the segment's thesis. It is early — prototype tethered, specs unpublished, "far behind on the robotics front" per skeptical press [S1] — but its LeRobot-derived software leverage and funding mean fast iteration. For an EU entrant this is a direct future competitor for the same pilots, talent and capital. Trajectory: scaling up.
