## Company background
Promobot, founded in Perm in 2015, has long claimed to be the largest service-robot manufacturer in Russia and Northern/Eastern Europe. Its flagship V.4 is a wheeled, gesturing, touchscreen-equipped service humanoid sold for promotion, reception, and government service roles; partner materials claim 800+ units working in 40+ countries (vendor figures cite "43 countries") [S1][S2]. Landmark deployments are largely in Russian public administration — e.g., a V.4 working as a "civil servant" issuing certificates at the Perm MFC (multi-function government service center), processing 30,000+ inquiries since 2019 [S5] — plus airports, malls, banks and museums abroad (e.g., Kuwait Oil Corporation) [S1]. Pricing: from ~$25,000 purchase, ~$2,000/day event rental (third-party distributor figures) [S2][S6].

The Robo-C / Robo-C2 line (2019-) was a stationary hyper-realistic android with 600+ facial expressions and customizable appearance, sold as concierge/companion and famously as celebrity doubles. As of 2026 Promobot's own site states "The humanoid project is over. We are no longer accepting any applications" — the android line is discontinued [S3].

Sanctions-era status: Promobot opened a New York sales office pre-2022; after Russia's invasion of Ukraine, Western expansion stalled (no formal sanctions on the company found, but payment/logistics barriers apply). The company reoriented to the Middle East and Asia: 2025 statements position the UAE as its strategic humanoid-robotics hub, and an October 2025 report says Promobot plans a robot factory in China [S4]. The July 2026 US FCC ban on imports of foreign-made humanoid robots further closes the US market [S7]. Recent product work includes a Robo-C-based patient-simulator for Perm State Medical University [S4].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Promobot matters as the highest-volume Russian service-humanoid vendor and a long-running proof that wheeled gesturing receptionists sell in the hundreds of units at ~$25k. But its arms are gesturing-only (no real manipulation), its Western market access is effectively gone, and its android line is dead — trajectory is sideways-to-down outside Russia/MENA. For an EU entrant it is not a serious manipulation competitor; its relevance is as a price/volume benchmark in reception-class robots and a cautionary tale on geopolitical market access.
