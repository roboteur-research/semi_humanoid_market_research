## Company background
MELTIN MMI was a Tokyo "cyborg technology" startup combining biosignal processing with wire-driven (cable-driven) bio-mimetic robotics. Its avatar line — MELTANT-α (concept, March 2018) and MELTANT-β (field-test model, March 2020) — showcased hands with near-human dexterity, speed and grip strength for teleoperated hazardous work, and joined the JAXA/ANA Holdings AVATAR X space-avatar program in September 2018, including ISS crew-task replacement studies with JAXA [S1][S2][S3]. Demos reached the G20 Osaka summit and the Prime Minister's residence [S1].

The avatar business never commercialized: after MELTANT-β field trials, MELTIN concentrated on its medical product MELTz (EMG-driven hand rehabilitation, launched Sep 2022, sold with Sumitomo Pharma). In June 2024 the MELTz business was transferred to FrontAct Co. (100% Sumitomo Pharma subsidiary), and the company itself was dissolved on 28 November 2024 [S4]. This goes beyond the "paused/pivot" framing in earlier notes — MELTIN MMI no longer exists as an operating company.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Now defunct — relevance is historical/technological: MELTANT's wire-driven hands (~0.02 s response, high grip-to-weight) remain a reference point for tendon-driven end-effector design, and the AVATAR X tie-in illustrates the teleop-avatar niche Japanese startups occupied before the current AI-autonomy wave. The team/IP dispersal (partly into Sumitomo Pharma's orbit) leaves no successor product.
