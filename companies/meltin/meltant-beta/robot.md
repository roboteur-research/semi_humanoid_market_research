# MELTANT-β (メルタント・ベータ) — MELTIN MMI

> Defunct teleop avatar prototype: a pedestal-mounted upper-body avatar robot whose signature was wire-driven bio-mimetic hands combining human-like dexterity with high grip strength and ~0.02 s response. MELTANT-α (2018) was the concept; MELTANT-β (31 Mar 2020) was ruggedized (dust/splash-proof) for field trials in construction, plants and CBRN-type hazardous sites, and fed the JAXA/ANA AVATAR X space program. Development ended with MELTIN's medical pivot; the company dissolved 28 Nov 2024.

MELTANT was Japan's most technically distinctive avatar-robot line: instead of motor-in-joint arms, MELTIN routed cable transmissions from remote actuators, yielding light, fast, strong hands able to grip tools, open valves, and manipulate delicate objects under direct human teleoperation with force/tactile feedback [S1][S2]. MELTANT-β's stated commercial thesis was labor-shortage and safety relief in construction and heavy industry — send the avatar into chemically contaminated, hot/cold or radiological environments while the operator works remotely [S1]. Field-test partnerships (incl. an insurance tie-up with Mitsui Sumitomo Marine) and AVATAR X gave visibility, but no commercial product followed; MELTIN redirected resources to the MELTz rehabilitation device, transferred that business to Sumitomo Pharma's FrontAct in June 2024, and dissolved on 28 Nov 2024 [S4]. Contradiction vs discovery notes: the program is not merely "paused" — the company no longer exists.
