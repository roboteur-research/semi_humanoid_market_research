## Company background
isento GmbH is a Nuremberg software/IT consultancy that runs pib ("printable
intelligent bot"), an open-source, fully 3D-printable humanoid robot anyone can
build. The project distributes free print files and assembly guides, sells
component kits (motors, electronics, fasteners) via pib.Shop, and runs an
education program placing pib in schools through a sponsorship/partner model.
pib won a German Design Award 2025 and maintains an active Discord community of
2,100+ robotics/AI enthusiasts [S2][S3]. Founder Jürgen Baier frames humanoids
as "the next major change in society" [S2].

In 2025/26 the project moved up-market with pib.Pro, pitched as "Europe's first
open-source, ready-to-ship humanoid robot platform" for real-world work, learning
and care: stronger hardware, higher payload, precise motors, 3D vision + lidar,
onboard AI, ROS-based community software modules for navigation, manipulation and
interaction. Positioning stresses a "DSGVO-compliant, modular and future-proof
alternative to closed robotics systems" — an explicitly European/data-sovereignty
sales angle. Pilot customers are being recruited via the website; humanoid.guide
lists it at USD 5,999 "ready-to-ship" [S1][S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Strategically relevant for a German client as a domestic, open-source,
GDPR-flagged competitor with strong education/community mindshare in Germany —
i.e. a talent and narrative competitor more than a capability one. Hardware
(servo-driven, 3D-printed polymers, ~4 kg payload claim) is far below industrial
grade; near-term overlap is in research/education and light assistance pilots,
not logistics or manufacturing. Trajectory: scaling from maker kit to commercial
pilots; worth monitoring as the German open-source humanoid ecosystem nucleus.
