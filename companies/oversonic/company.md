## Company background
Oversonic Robotics was founded in 2020 in Besana in Brianza by Fabio Puglia and Paolo Denti with the thesis that a wheeled, certified, "cognitive" humanoid could enter real factories and hospitals years before bipeds. Its single platform, RoBee, is a 40-joint wheeled humanoid built and assembled in Lombardy, marketed as "the only humanoid robot currently certified for the industrial and medical sectors" (vendor claim; in verifiable terms: CE marking under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC, EMC-EMI, ISO 27001:2013 for information security, GDPR compliance, IPX4 — not ISO 13482 or medical-device certification, see robot.md) [S1][S2][S3].

The company's breakout deal came in December 2025: a global supply agreement with STMicroelectronics to introduce custom RoBee units into production and logistics across ST's plant network — the first operational integration of humanoid robots in the semiconductor industry — with the first unit working in ST's advanced packaging & test fab in Kirkop, Malta [S5][S6]. In mid-2026 STMicroelectronics, Fondazione ENEA Tech Biomedical and SpotInvest took equity stakes to fund industrial and international expansion [S7]. Oversonic launched RoBee in the US in January 2026 after CES 2026 demos, opening offices in Cincinnati and Los Angeles, with initial focus on medical and aerospace sectors [S4].

Go-to-market is direct B2B sale/rental of configured robots plus its proprietary cognitive AI platform (multilingual voice interaction, mission execution, ML vision, predictive maintenance). Revenue was 95% Italian in 2025; the company targets a 50/50 domestic/international split in 2026 and reports orders from Germany, France and the USA [S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Oversonic is Europe's most commercially advanced wheeled-humanoid vendor in real industrial settings: paying customers, a marquee semiconductor account (STM) with global rollout potential, healthcare trials, and strategic capital from a major chipmaker. For a new EU entrant it is the closest like-for-like competitor — same region, same wheeled+bimanual formula, same certification-first pitch — and its "certified cognitive humanoid" positioning sets the reference customers will benchmark against. Trajectory: scaling up aggressively (200-300% growth target, US entry, possible IPO).
