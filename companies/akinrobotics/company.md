## Company background
AKINROBOTICS is the robotics arm of Konya-based software house AKINSOFT (founded 1995 by Dr. Özgür Akın). On 4 November 2017 it opened what Turkish and international press called **Türkiye's first humanoid-robot factory** — the company itself markets it as the **"world's first humanoid robot factory"** (a marketing claim; small-batch lines existed elsewhere) — beginning serial production of the **ADA GH5** social humanoid and **Mini ADA** [S3][S4]. The ADA name = "Android Developed by Akınrobotics", also honoring Ada Lovelace [S5]. Robots are wheeled service humanoids with LED/animated faces, gesturing arms and touchscreen chests, produced with claimed full vertical integration (mechanics, electronics and software all in-house, "fully domestic") [S1][S5].

Product line evolution: ADA GH5 (2016-17, mall/fair/hospital/home assistant; in 2019 Daily Sabah coverage the company boasted it was "smarter than Sophia") → ADA GH6 → **Mini ADA** generations 1-3 (123 cm / 45 kg, 15-language AI conversation) → **ADA-7** (current flagship social robot: 0.6 m/s wheeled base with 360° turn, 4-axis arms with grasping hands, LED face, skeleton tracking, face/emotion/age/gender analysis) [S1][S2][S5]. Flagship deployments: **Mini ADA on duty at Istanbul Airport** (passenger info, directions, flight info via barcode reader, TR/EN) [S2]; ADA robots populate the company's own **Istanbul Robot Museum** (marketed as the world's first robot museum) where Mini ADA is resident guide [S5]. Sales/rental of robots is offered via the akinoid.com store; exports are claimed to several countries but no verified export volumes were found (n/a).

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
AKINROBOTICS owns the Turkish service-humanoid niche with genuine (if low-volume) serial production since 2017 and real public-facing deployments (airport, museum, events). Its robots are reception/social class — modest 4-axis arms, basic grasping, no industrial manipulation — so it does not compete in the industrial semi-humanoid segment. Relevance to an EU entrant: a local incumbent/partner for Türkiye go-to-market, and a case study that national-champion branding plus own-venue showcases (museum, airport) can sustain a service-robot business at small scale.
