# G1 — GITAI (T3 compact dossier)

> G1 was GITAI's dual-arm humanoid-torso telepresence robot (~2018-2020) built to substitute astronaut labor inside space stations, teleoperated through an immersive 360° VR link with GITAI's proprietary video compression (2.7K stream squeezed from 800Mbps to ~2.5Mbps, ~60ms latency). In a JAXA Kibo mock-up experiment (Dec 2018) a GITAI robot completed 13 of 18 (72%) astronaut tasks. GITAI later pivoted to single-arm modular systems (S1 ISS 2021, S2 external ISS 2024) and moved its HQ to Los Angeles — G1 is historical, but a notable data point: a semi-humanoid design retired in favor of task-specific arms.

Key facts: dual-arm humanoid torso with head-mounted 360°/stereo cameras; immersive VR teleoperation with data compression 800Mbps→~2.5Mbps at ~60ms latency [S1] (third-party). JAXA Kibo mock-up test 2018-12-25/27: 72% (13/18) task success on a JAXA-provided astronaut task list [S1]. JAXA joint research agreement signed 2019-03 [S2]. Funding round 2019 explicitly aimed at "world's greatest robot working in space" [S3]. Superseded by single-arm S1 (ISS interior demo 2021) and S2 (ISS exterior demo 2024); company now pursues NASA/DARPA-adjacent lunar robotics from the USA [S2]. Detailed G1 specs (DoF, mass, payload) were never published (n/a).
