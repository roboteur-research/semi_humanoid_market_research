## Company background
Tangible Robots is a Bay-Area startup ("Tangible — Building the physical future", tagline "Scaling Robotics") that emerged from stealth in November 2025 with Eggie, a wheeled dual-arm home humanoid. The company keeps an unusually thin public profile — its website is a single landing page — and most public information comes from its demo videos and third-party robot directories. Design philosophy per launch coverage centres on three pillars: dexterity, compliance and whole-body control, choosing a stable wheeled base over legs for domestic efficiency. [S1][S2]

Demonstrations show Eggie cleaning spills, folding laundry, watering plants and handling fragile objects in home settings — the classic household-manipulation demo suite. No funding, team or manufacturing information has been disclosed. [S2]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
One of several US home-robot startups betting on wheeled semi-humanoids (cf. Sunday, Weave/Isaac, 1X consumer line). Low near-term relevance to an EU industrial entrant — consumer/home focus, prototype stage, opaque company — but its compliance/whole-body-control emphasis and ~$32k listed price point illustrate where US home humanoids are heading. Trajectory: too early to call.
