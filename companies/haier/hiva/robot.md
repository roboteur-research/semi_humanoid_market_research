Haier unveiled HIVA at AWE 2025 as a home-assistant humanoid. It is the Robot Era Q5 wheeled platform (dual 7-axis arms with 11-DoF hands, ERA-42 VLA — see Q5 dossier) with Haier branding and smart-home/appliance integration [S1][S2]. Demos to date are largely teleoperated by engineers while the home-task AI is trained [S2]. The deal illustrates Robot Era's B2B2C route into consumer channels; any future retail launch would ride Haier's massive appliance distribution.
