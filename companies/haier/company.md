## Company background (compact)
Haier is a global appliance group, not a robot manufacturer. Its humanoid entry, HIVA (海娃, "Haiwa"), unveiled at AWE 2025, is a jointly developed/customized version of Robot Era's wheeled Q5 humanoid, integrated with Haier's smart-home appliance ecosystem [S1][S2]. The tie is also financial: Haier Capital co-led Robot Era's Series A [S3 — QA-notes resolution]. Haier's role is branding, distribution and smart-home integration; the robot platform, VLA models (ERA-42) and manufacturing come from Robot Era.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Haier/HIVA matters as a channel signal: a top-tier appliance brand putting a wheeled semi-humanoid into its consumer smart-home line, via partnership rather than in-house development. For platform details, specs and assessment see the Robot Era Q5 dossier.
