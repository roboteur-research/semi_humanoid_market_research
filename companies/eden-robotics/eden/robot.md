# Eden I — Eden Robotics

> "Eden I" is the first industrial semi-humanoid from YC Spring-2026 startup Eden Robotics, offered exclusively as hourly robotic labor (from $10/hour, $0 setup) for manufacturing, warehouse and 3PL tasks. No hardware specifications or public imagery of the robot exist yet — it is pre-deployment and included here for completeness of the RaaS semi-humanoid landscape.

Eden claims a vertically integrated stack: in-house hardware, "Theta OS" general-purpose robot operating system, "Interlink" teleoperation and "Fleet-Sync" multi-robot coordination — i.e., human-in-the-loop supervised autonomy of the kind Reflex Robotics and Weave operate [S4, vendor-claimed]. The founders describe the robots as "semi-humanoid" and "filling positions designed for humans", implying a mobile manipulator with human-workstation-compatible geometry; form factor (wheeled base assumed for industrial semi-humanoids, unconfirmed), DoF, payload, sensors and compute are all n/a (not disclosed). Marketing stresses plug-and-play deployment with no on-site engineers, 24/7 operation and fleet hiring for enterprise [S3]. No customers, pilots or third-party sightings of hardware were found as of Aug 2026; treat all capability claims as vendor-claimed and unverified.
