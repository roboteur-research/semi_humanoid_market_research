## Company background
Eden Robotics is a YC Spring 2026 startup (YC batch code "P26"; some YC metadata also labels it Fall 2026 — the Launch YC post went up ~mid-2026) building "autonomous robots for industrial labor", starting with manufacturing, warehouses and 3PL [S2][S3]. The pitch: robots that can fill positions designed for humans for any task "explainable to a new worker in under 5 minutes", sold not as hardware but as usage-based labor — Robots-as-a-Service starting at $10/hour with $0 setup fee, plug-and-play deployment ("unbox it and it just works"), and fleet hiring for enterprises [S3][S4]. The stack is vertically integrated: own hardware, Theta OS, plus human-in-the-loop infrastructure (Interlink teleop, Fleet-Sync coordination) that keeps fleets "safe, supervised and productive" — i.e., a teleop-backed autonomy model similar to Reflex or Weave [S1].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Pre-deployment and tiny (4 people), Eden matters mainly as a signal: hourly-priced ($10/h) teleop-backstopped semi-humanoid labor is becoming the default YC playbook, and Eden is one of the few explicitly using the term "semi-humanoid". No hardware specs, images of the robot, pilots or customers are public yet; it is seeking pilot partners and software integrations. Threat to an EU entrant currently negligible, but note the London legal HQ — it may emerge as a UK/EU competitor. (Curiosity: the website embeds an anti-AI-cloning notice aimed at coding agents.)
