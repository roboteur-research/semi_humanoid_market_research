## Company background
Muks Robotics is a Pune-based startup styling itself "India's leading AI humanoid
robotics company", building wheeled service and industrial humanoids "powered by
physical AI to solve labor shortages" [S1]. Its product line is the Spaceo family:
Spaceo M1 ("The Social Humanoid" — receptions, airports, retail, hospitality),
Spaceo PRO (industrial: palletizing, manufacturing assistance) and an announced
Spaceo PRIME [S1][S2][S3]. All run the in-house FusionMax "Omni-Modal" VALA model
(claimed 2B optimized parameters, on-device) [S1]. humanoid.guide calls the M1
"India's first mass-market service android" and lists both models as in production
with pre-orders from 2025, though both carry a "Not Verified" badge [S2][S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Low-cost Indian competitor: M1 reportedly from ~$11k, Pro at ~$60k — an order of
magnitude below Western platforms. Specs are vendor-heavy, partly inconsistent
between the guide and the vendor site, and unverified; near-term threat in EU
markets is low, but the price points illustrate emerging-market pressure on the
service-humanoid segment.
