## Company background
CasiVision (中科慧远, Zhongke Huiyuan — the "CAS" in the brand reflecting Chinese Academy of Sciences lineage) is a machine-vision company founded around 2016 that became a leading Chinese manufacturer of AOI (automatic optical inspection) equipment for industrial quality control, best known for high-speed cover-glass/panel defect inspection. [S2, third-party]

On 19 August 2025 it unveiled CASIVIBOT, billed as China's first "industrial embodied quality-inspection robot": a dual-arm wheeled platform with a "hand-eye-brain" integrated design — three-camera multispectral vision array, 7-DoF arm(s) with interchangeable intelligent grippers, and the proprietary "HuiBrain" AI platform. Management framed it as a "dimensional upgrade" in which fixed AOI machines and mobile robots collaborate across the product quality lifecycle, moving CasiVision from inspection equipment vendor to embodied-robotics player. [S2, third-party]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
A vertical-specialist entrant: instead of a general-purpose humanoid, CasiVision wraps its proven inspection optics and defect-detection AI in a mobile dual-arm form. For an EU entrant this is the "domain incumbent goes embodied" pattern — strong in China's 3C/panel quality-inspection niche, unlikely to compete outside it near-term. Trajectory: early; first units listed ~$29k (third-party) with production status claimed.
