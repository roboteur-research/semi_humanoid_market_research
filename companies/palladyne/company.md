## Company background
Sarcos was the storied Utah robotics house (humanoid entertainment robots, prosthetics, Raytheon exoskeletons) that went public via SPAC in 2021 promising to commercialize the Guardian XO exoskeleton and Guardian XT teleoperated dual-arm avatar, and bought RE2 Robotics (Sapien dexterous arms, Navy HDMS dual-arm systems) in 2022. Commercialization stalled: facing "ongoing financial challenges", the board elected in November 2023 to exit hardware development and production entirely; in March 2024 the company rebranded as Palladyne AI, retaining defense work under the Sarcos Defense name [S1][S2]. Today it sells robot-agnostic AI software: Palladyne IQ (closed-loop autonomy for existing industrial arms in unstructured environments, IQ 2.0 commercially released) and Palladyne Pilot (UAV autonomy; completed Phase 1 of a US Air Force software contract; partnership with drone maker Red Cat) [S2][S3].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Palladyne matters to this study as (a) the most prominent cautionary tale — a well-funded public company that shelved a mature teleop dual-arm torso product (Guardian XT) plus acquired dexterous-arm IP (RE2 Sapien/HDMS) because unit economics and market timing failed pre-VLA-era, and (b) a possible software supplier: Palladyne IQ targets exactly the "existing arms + autonomy" gap. Its hardware assets/patents (backdrivable hydraulic-electric actuation, SenSuit teleop) remain a latent acquisition target. Direct competitive threat to an EU semi-humanoid entrant: none (hardware discontinued).
