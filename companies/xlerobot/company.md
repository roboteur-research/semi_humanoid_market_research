## Company background
XLeRobot is the most prominent ultra-low-cost open-source dual-arm mobile manipulator: two SO-100/SO-101 LeRobot arms and a three-omni-wheel drive base bolted onto an IKEA RÅSKOG utility cart, buildable from ~$660 in under 4 hours [S1]. Created by Rice University grad student Vector Wang as a hobby-turned-platform, it builds directly on Hugging Face's LeRobot stack (imitation learning, VLA training) and the Lekiwi/Bambot lineage. A developer assembly kit is sold worldwide by Wowrobo ($579; CNY 3,699 in China), with optional stereo cameras (+$30), Raspberry Pi (+$79) or RealSense RGB-D (+$220) [S1].

Timeline: v0.2.0 (first fully capable) June 2025; MuJoCo simulation + VR control July 2025; v0.3.0 with household demos August 2025; browser-based MuJoCo/3DGS control January 2026. Traction: ~5.4k GitHub stars, ~590 forks [S1] (as of 2026-08).

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
The de-facto reference design for hobbyist/edu wheeled dual-arm robots: it commoditizes the semi-humanoid form factor at 1/100th commercial prices and funnels a large community into LeRobot-based imitation learning. For an EU entrant it is not a competitor but an ecosystem signal — talent, datasets and expectations are forming around open low-cost platforms.
