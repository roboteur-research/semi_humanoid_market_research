## Company background
Hexagon AB is the Swedish measurement-technology ("digital reality") giant behind Leica Geosystems, coordinate-measuring machines and manufacturing-intelligence software. In 2024-25 it created a dedicated **Hexagon Robotics** division and at Hexagon LIVE in **June 2025** unveiled **AEON**, an industrial humanoid whose defining trait is wheels-on-legs locomotion and a metrology-grade sensor suite. Development is backed by an NVIDIA partnership (Isaac Sim/Omniverse simulation, Jetson onboard) and by Hexagon's existing factory-customer base [S1][S4].

The strategic angle is unique among humanoid entrants: Hexagon monetizes **measurement and inspection** — AEON doubles as a mobile reality-capture/QC platform producing centimetre-accurate digital twins while also doing manipulation, plugging directly into Hexagon's metrology software estate. Marquee traction came fast: a **BMW Group** test deployment at Plant Leipzig (initial test Dec 2025; second phase April 2026; full pilot from summer 2026) [S2][S3], a Pilatus (aerospace) pilot, and a 2025 pilot with **Schaeffler** that expanded in April 2026 into the largest confirmed European humanoid fleet commitment: **at least 1,000 AEON units across Schaeffler's global production network by 2032**, with Schaeffler also supplying high-precision actuators to AEON — a circular customer/supplier alignment [S4][S5].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Hexagon is the heavyweight incumbent entrant: balance-sheet-funded, pre-wired into thousands of factories through metrology sales channels, and holding the two most bankable European lighthouse accounts (BMW, Schaeffler). Its wheels-on-legs design is BORDERLINE for the strict wheeled-semi-humanoid category — legged articulation with wheel end-effectors (~9 km/h) — but it competes head-on for every European industrial pilot a wheeled entrant would target. Trajectory: aggressive scale-up with committed fleet volume — arguably the most serious European competitor in this study.
