# Eno — Genesis AI

> Eno is Genesis AI's first general-purpose robot (unveiled 2026-06-16): a wheeled base carrying a foldable, height-adjustable "tower" torso with two arms and human-form five-finger hands, run by the GENE robotics foundation model. It is an explicit "opposite bet" against legged humanoids, aimed at manufacturing, logistics and labs, reportedly ~$90k, with production deployments (incl. an LG CNS partnership) targeted for end-2026. Competitively it pairs top-tier foundation-model/simulation credentials with the wheeled semi-humanoid form factor.

## Design & morphology
Wheeled base with a minimalist tower of articulated panels that adjusts height and reach in real time and folds down "to the size of a checked bag" for storage/transport [S2][S3]. Height ~170 cm (working), weight ~120 kg, 40 DoF total [S1, third-party aggregator]. Optional chest-mounted "cognitive interface" screen displays the AI's reasoning [S4]. White finish, aluminum/composite construction [S1].

## Locomotion
Wheeled (drive type not detailed); max speed ~6 km/h (1.7 m/s) [S1]. Indoor industrial environments.

## Upper body & manipulation
Two arms (DoF per arm not broken out) with proprietary dexterous five-finger hands co-developed with Wuji Tech: human-scale, back-drivable, soft-covered articulated fingers; hand DoF reported as 20 total (humanoid.guide) vs 22 DoF (Humanoids Daily) — unresolved [S1][S4]. Manipulation payload ~12 kg [S1]. Demonstrated skills (GENE-driven): cooking tasks, lab pipetting, multi-object grasping, solving a Rubik's Cube [S3, vendor-claimed].

## Sensing
Cameras assumed (not itemized in launch coverage); n/a (not disclosed).

## Actuation & power
Electric servo actuators [S1]. Battery capacity n/a; runtime ~8 h per charge [S1].

## Compute & software
Runs GENE, Genesis AI's robotics-native foundation model: understands objectives, retains memory, adapts to changes, plans multi-step workflows — positioned as a "true physical agent" rather than pre-programmed motion [S3]. Trained heavily on synthetic data from Genesis World 1.0 (physics engine up to 430,000x real-time) [S5]. Cloud-connected; Wi-Fi/Ethernet [S1]. Onboard compute n/a (not disclosed).

## Safety & compliance
"Human-safe" claimed; no standards or certifications cited [S1]. eWeek flagged missing proof points: safety validation, payload verification, full-shift uptime [S6].

## Deployment evidence & traction
- Strategic multi-phase partnership with LG CNS to evaluate, pilot and scale Eno across LG's US manufacturing/logistics operations from 2026 (announced at launch) [S3][S4, vendor-claimed].
- No named paying customers, unit counts or field deployments yet; production targeted end-2026 [S2][S6].
- Company reportedly in talks for a ~$500M round to fund scaling [S7, third-party].

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* Strengths: exceptional funding and talent density, a real foundation-model + simulation moat (GENE + Genesis World), dexterous five-finger hands, and a pragmatic wheeled/foldable design that industrial customers reportedly asked for; the LG CNS channel derisks go-to-market. Weaknesses: hardware is unproven at launch (no certified safety, no verified uptime), spec disclosure is thin, and the company's center of gravity is AI, not manufacturing at scale. Threat to a new EU entrant: high — same form-factor thesis, ~$90k price point, Paris presence and French/EU investors mean direct collision in EU industrial accounts by 2027 if execution holds.
