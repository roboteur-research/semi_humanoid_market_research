## Company background
Shanghai SAGE Intelligent Technology (brand iSAGEBOT; Chinese names 智在无界 / 赛智) was founded in November 2017 as an AI mobile-robot company; its first AMR product series launched in October 2019 [S3][S4]. The company built its base business in intelligent mobile co-robots for manufacturing intralogistics (picking, placing, warehousing) plus niche applications such as library book handling, serving new-energy, biopharma, automotive-parts and semiconductor customers [S1][S4].

From 2025 it moved up into multi-arm wheeled humanoids, deliberately skipping the two-arm mainstream: the SageMan (also branded iSAGE STAR / SageBot) is marketed as the world's first AI-powered four-armed humanoid, with a 52-DoF body, wheeled base and 360° workspace [S2]. In July 2026 the company teased the six-armed MAHAKBOT at China Media Group's intelligent-robot exhibition in Beijing (heterogeneous arm layout, up to ~120 DoF, 300 mm telescoping lift), claiming 100+ letters of intent collected at the China International Technology Fair in Shanghai [S4][S5]. Proprietary stack: SageBrain controller + SageOS real-time OS with external LLM/VLA integration [S1].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
iSAGEBOT is the flag-bearer of the emerging Chinese 超人形 ("super-humanoid") multi-arm category alongside Midea's MIRO U: rather than imitating human morphology, it multiplies arms for throughput ("4× vs dual-arm") and 360° coverage on a wheeled base. If the concept proves out in 3C/automotive assembly, it attacks exactly the value proposition (parallel bimanual work) that dual-arm semi-humanoids sell, with a stronger cycle-time story. Still an early-stage player — pre-A funding scale, robots "in development" per third-party trackers — so execution risk is high; watch the claimed LOI-to-order conversion in 2026-27.
