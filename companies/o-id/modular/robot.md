## Design & morphology
172 cm, 70 kg; humanoid upper body — two anthropomorphic arms shoulder-mounted
either side of a slim white column torso with a sensor slot (no distinct head
in render) — on a wheeled base (base not shown in featured render; guide
describes "stable, wheeled approach") [S1][S4]. 30 DoF total; aluminum alloy +
composites; white/blue/grey finishes. Core design: interchangeable component
modules, swappable in the field [S1][S2].

## Locomotion
Wheeled base with wheel hub motors; max 5 km/h (1.4 m/s); flat-floor
environments [S1].

## Upper body & manipulation
Dual 7-axis-class arms (per-arm DoF not stated) with multi-finger claw-style
grippers (10 fingers total); total payload 16 kg — solid for the class.
Manipulation skill score 3/10 per guide. Modularity extends to arm/end-effector
swaps [S1][S2]. Reach/repeatability n/a.

## Sensing
Sensor aperture in torso column (camera bar); sensor suite otherwise
undisclosed [S1][S4].

## Actuation & power
Electric servo/BLDC actuators with harmonic/planetary reducers in arms; wheel
hub motors in base. Runtime 4 h per charge, but rated "24/7 uptime" — implying
hot-swappable batteries or dock rotation (mechanism not disclosed) [S1].

## Compute & software
Linux/ROS-based OS; Ethernet + WiFi; LLM integration unspecified, SDK
"possible" per guide. Fleet/autonomy software undisclosed [S1].

## Safety & compliance
"Safe for human interaction" (guide attribute); no standards, CE or
certification claims found [S1].

## Deployment evidence & traction
None found — no customers, pilots or unit counts; early production claim is
vendor/guide only [S1][S2].

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* O-ID's thesis — modular, field-serviceable, 24/7-capable
wheeled humanoid with sovereign domestic manufacturing — is strategically the
closest Japanese analogue to a German industrial entrant's likely pitch, and
16 kg total payload at $70k undercuts most Western offerings on paper. But the
company has near-zero public footprint (no team, funding, deployments), the
4 h runtime vs 24/7 claim needs a battery-swap story, and the "Modular" brand
suggests a components business as much as a robot. Threat to an EU entrant:
low now; conceptually instructive, worth a direct evaluation contact.
