## Company background
WIRobotics is a Korean robotics company best known for the WIM lightweight wearable
walking-assist robot, sold commercially and used in dedicated gait-training centers
[S1]. In 2025 it entered the humanoid space with ALLEX, a dual-arm upper-body
humanoid built around ultra-low-friction, low-inertia proprietary actuators and
"sensorless" whole-body force control — a technology transfer from its wearable
(inherently human-contact-safe) actuation heritage [S2][S3]. The company announced a
strategic collaboration with Korean physical-AI startup RLWRLD for foundation-model
development on ALLEX [S2].

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Korea entry with a differentiated compliance/force-control angle rather than
vision-only autonomy. ALLEX is today an upper-body/pedestal system (mobile bases,
incl. wheeled configs, and eventually biped locomotion are described as under
development), so it competes as a manipulation platform for cleaning, home service
and logistics R&D. Priced ~$180k (third-party). Watch for the Samsung-lineage
actuator IP: fingertip force sensing at ~100 gf without force sensors is a strong
manipulation claim.
