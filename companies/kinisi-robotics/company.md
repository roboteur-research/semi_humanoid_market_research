## Company background
Kinisi Robotics is a US/UK startup founded by Bren Pierce building a low-cost wheeled
humanoid ("Physical AI platform") for warehouse logistics. Strategy is explicitly
pragmatic: wheels instead of legs ("twice as fast as a legged humanoid"), onboard
edge intelligence (no cloud dependency), and quick training/minimal setup. The KR1
won the Automate Start-up Challenge in May 2025 and the company reported ~$2M in
funding [S2]. Product branding shifted from "KR1" to "Kinisi 01" on the current
website; the vendor treats them as the same platform generation ("KR1, our Physical
AI platform") [S3].

In 2026 Kinisi announced it is joining Bear Robotics (the Korean/US service-robot
maker backed by LG), which would fold the KR1 into Bear's fleet and give the platform
a manufacturing/distribution parent [S1][S3]. This makes Kinisi one of the first
Western wheeled-humanoid startups to exit via acquisition.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
Directly relevant competitor archetype: dual-arm torso on omnidirectional wheeled
base, aggressively priced (~$75k reported), targeting warehouse pick/place, totes and
palletizing — the same segment an EU semi-humanoid entrant would contest. The Bear
Robotics acquisition signals consolidation and gives the product a scaled channel.
UK/US footprint; notable founder with prior robotics-commercialization experience.
