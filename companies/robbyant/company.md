## Company background
Robbyant (officially Shanghai Ant Lingbo Technology Co., Ltd., 蚂蚁灵波科技) is the humanoid-robotics and embodied-AI subsidiary of Ant Group, the Chinese fintech giant affiliated with Alibaba. Established around 2024, it is a strategically significant entrant because it pairs a well-capitalised tech conglomerate with in-house foundation-model capability — its stated positioning is "Building One Brain for All Robots", a full-stack embodied-intelligence play covering vision models, spatial perception and video-action models, with integration of Ant's Bailing (百灵) LLM. [S1][S2]

Its first hardware product, the wheeled humanoid R1, was unveiled on 11 September 2025 at Ant Group's Inclusion·Bund Conference (外滩大会) in Shanghai, where it cooked four dishes end-to-end in a live "robot chef" demo; it was also exhibited at IFA Berlin in September 2025, signalling export ambitions. The R1 is offered from ~USD 55k and is reportedly in pilot testing at Chinese museums, drugstores (pharmacy sorting) and public venues, targeting service-sector deployments (retail, hospitality, museums, healthcare support). A taller beige variant ("R1-S", ~175 cm) has been reported. [S1][S2]

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
High strategic relevance despite being an early entrant: Ant Group's capital, AI stack and distribution (Alipay ecosystem, smart-retail footprint) could let Robbyant scale a service-oriented wheeled humanoid quickly in China's commercial-services market. Trajectory: scaling up; watch for RaaS-style bundling with Ant's retail/payments channels.
