# Robbyant R1 — Ant Lingbo Technology (蚂蚁灵波)

> Wheeled service humanoid from Ant Group's robotics subsidiary: a ~1.6 m, 34-DoF dual-arm robot on a circular wheeled pedestal base, aimed at hospitality, retail, museum-guide, pharmacy, light food-prep and healthcare-support roles. Unveiled 11 September 2025 at the Inclusion·Bund Conference in Shanghai with a live "robot chef" demo. Competitively significant less for its specs than for its backer — Ant Group brings deep capital, in-house embodied foundation models plus the Bailing LLM, and a huge commercial-services distribution network.

## Design & morphology
Humanoid torso with two arms and a sensor head on a single-column pedestal over a circular wheeled base (puck-style, ~fully enclosed). Height ~160 cm, weight ~110 kg, 34 DoF overall [S1, third-party]. One report of a beige "R1-S" variant at 175 cm suggests at least one taller derivative model [S3, third-party, unconfirmed]. Torso lift/waist articulation n/a (not disclosed).

## Locomotion
Wheeled base; drive topology not disclosed (enclosed skirt hides wheels — likely diff-drive or omni). Max speed ~1.5 m/s (5.4 km/h) [S1]. Terrain/climbing limits and brakes n/a (not disclosed).

## Upper body & manipulation
Dual arms, per-arm DoF not disclosed (visually ~7 DoF each); combined payload ~6 kg [S1]. End-effectors in verified imagery are two-finger grippers; hand DoF n/a (not disclosed). Demonstrated fine tasks: identifying ingredients and kitchen tools, cooking four dishes end-to-end (selection → cooking → cleaning), and sorting pharmaceuticals in a drugstore [S2][S3]. Tool changer / media at flange n/a (not disclosed).

## Sensing
Head described only as a "multimodal perception camera" module (glossy black visor head) [S1]. Wrist/hand sensors, base lidar/ultrasonics, force/tactile sensing all n/a (not disclosed).

## Actuation & power
48 V DC geared motors with encoders [S1, third-party]. Battery capacity n/a; runtime ~4–6 h claimed [S3, third-party]. Hot-swap/charging dock n/a (not disclosed).

## Compute & software
Onboard compute n/a (not disclosed). Connectivity: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth [S1]. Software: Lingbo's proprietary embodied-AI foundation models (vision, spatial perception, video-action) with Ant Group's Bailing (百灵) LLM for interaction and task reasoning [S1][S2]. Company slogan: "Building One Brain for All Robots" — a full-stack embodied-intelligence platform play. SDK/API openness n/a (not disclosed). No fleet-management product disclosed.

## Safety & compliance
n/a (not disclosed). No ISO 13482/CE claims found.

## Deployment evidence & traction
Live kitchen demo (four dishes) at the Sept 2025 Bund Conference launch [S2, third-party]. Reported pilot testing in real Chinese venues: streets, museums, drugstores (pharmacy sorting), health consultations [S2][S3, third-party]. IFA Berlin 2025 appearance signals export ambitions [S3]. No named paying customers or unit counts found.

## Assessment (analyst view)
*Analyst opinion.* Strengths: Ant Group's capital, in-house foundation models (Bailing LLM + embodied models), and unmatched distribution into Chinese retail/hospitality/payments ecosystems; aggressive ~$55k price for a dual-arm service humanoid. Weaknesses: very sparse published specs, no disclosed compute/safety story, unproven manipulation (2/10 manipulation score on third-party listing), and demos are choreographed showcases. Threat to a new EU entrant: moderate today, potentially high within 2–3 years if Ant scales via its services network — but its service/consumer positioning overlaps less with industrial semi-humanoid use-cases, and EU market entry would face data-governance headwinds for an Ant-affiliated platform.
