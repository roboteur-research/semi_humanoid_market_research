## Company background
NimbRo is the robot-competition powerhouse of Sven Behnke's lab at the University of Bonn. For the semi-humanoid category its key systems were the self-built domestic service robots Dynamaid (2009) and Cosero (2010): lightweight wheeled humanoids with omnidirectional bases made of Dynamixel servo modules, 2-DoF movable trunk (with torso lift), two 7-DoF anthropomorphic arms with 2-DoF grippers, and a movable sensor head [S1]. Cosero — roughly twice as strong in the arms as Dynamaid — dominated RoboCup@Home in 2011-2014 (world titles 2011, 2012, 2013 plus repeated German Open wins), demonstrating cooking (omelet making), fridge access, table carrying and multimodal human interaction [S1][S2].

The lab's later trajectory matters as context: team NimbRo won the $5M ANA Avatar XPRIZE grand prize (Long Beach, Nov 2022) with an immersive telepresence robot, and its @Home team returned to winning the RoboCup@Home world championship in 2024 (Eindhoven) and German Opens 2024/2025 — now on a purchased PAL Robotics TIAGo++ platform rather than self-built hardware [S2][S3]. Cosero and Dynamaid are retired museum-grade research artifacts.

## Relevance to the semi-humanoid market
NimbRo demonstrates (a) German strength in autonomy software for domestic semi-humanoids — its competition record is the best in Europe — and (b) the shift from self-built hardware to commercial platforms (TIAGo++), which signals where academic demand for purchasable semi-humanoid platforms exists. For a German entrant: a top talent source (Behnke alumni), a potential software/benchmark partner, and indirect validation that a commercial research platform market exists (currently served by PAL Robotics).
