# Cosero (+ Dynamaid) — NimbRo, University of Bonn (T3 compact)

> Cosero ("Cognitive Service Robot", fall 2010) was NimbRo's self-built wheeled domestic semi-humanoid that dominated RoboCup@Home 2011-2014, cooking omelets, fetching juice from fridges and carrying tables. Predecessor Dynamaid (2009) established the lightweight Dynamixel-based design. Both retired; the lab now competes on a PAL TIAGo++ and won the ANA Avatar XPRIZE (2022). Relevance today: proof of German autonomy-software excellence, not a product.

Cosero: anthropomorphic upper body on an omnidirectional base with four individually steerable wheel-pair axes (8 wheels driven by Dynamixel EX-106 servos, axes steered by RX-64s; 12 base DoF), 2-DoF movable trunk (incl. vertical lift for varying working heights), two 7-DoF arms (~twice Dynamaid's strength, i.e. low single-kg payload class) with 2-DoF grippers, and a 2-DoF sensor head [S1][S3]. Sensors: four laser range finders, time-of-flight camera, two color cameras, directional microphone; consumer-notebook compute [S1]. Dynamaid (2009): same architecture, 20 kg base payload, 0.5 m/s, 1 kg arm payload, Sick S300 + Mesa SR4000 + FireWire cameras, Lenovo X200 subnotebook; won the RoboCup 2009 innovation award and placed 3rd [S1]. Cosero's record: RoboCup@Home world champion 2011, 2012, 2013 and multiple German Open titles 2011-2014, with signature demos (omelet cooking, fridge access, pouring, table carrying in pairs with humans) [S1][S2]. The line was retired as the lab moved to Momaro/Centauro, the XPRIZE-winning NimbRo Avatar, and (since 2023) a purchased PAL TIAGo++ for @Home — winning the 2024 world championship with it [S2].
