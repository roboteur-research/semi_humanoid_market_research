## Chinese terminology
- 轮式人形机器人 "wheeled humanoid robot" — DOMINANT term (UBTech, CASBOT, Topstar, Robot Era). One analysis: wheeled >80% of deployed "humanoids" (https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1977103110511034595). Trend since WRC 2024 (https://m.mp.oeeee.com/a/BAAFRD000020240822989334.html).
- 半人形机器人 "semi-humanoid robot" — moderately common; popularized by Pudu D7.
- 轮式双臂机器人 "wheeled dual-arm robot" — industrial/research framing (Fourier GRW, Rokae Helios).
- 轮臂式(具身)机器人 "wheel-arm (embodied) robot" — emerging (Quectel, Zerith, KNEWBOTS).
- 双臂移动操作机器人 "dual-arm mobile manipulation robot" — academic.
- 复合机器人 "composite robot" — legacy arm-on-AMR term.
- 具身智能/具身作业机器人 "embodied intelligence/operation robot" — umbrella buzzword 2024-26.
- 人形具身服务机器人 — Keenon's coinage.
- 类人型机器人 "human-like robot" — older term (Canbot).
- 超人形机器人 "super-humanoid" — new, multi-arm forms (Midea MIRO U, iSageBot).
- 全尺寸通用轮式人形机器人 "full-size general-purpose wheeled humanoid" — launch phrasing (Cruzr S2).
