## 2. WHY WHEELS vs LEGS

### 2.1 Case for wheels
- COST: Galbot founder Wang He: wheeled chassis ~10x cheaper than legs, more stable; bipedal "unnecessary... would increase costs". https://chozan.co/galbot-g1/ [third-party]. Melonee Wise: wheeled mobile manipulation "could probably get into the $10,000 range". Clearpath/OTTO: better performance/dollar; legged can need 12 motors → breakdowns. https://clearpathrobotics.com/blog/2025/04/do-robots-really-need-legs/
- STABILITY: low CG, no fall risk; bipeds burn power balancing, collapse on power loss; wheeled STOP rather than fall → faster certification. https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321462/20260724/uk-humanoid-startup-raises-152m-bosch-schaeffler-back-wheels-over-legs.htm
- SAFETY PHYSICS: Rodney Brooks (2025-09-26): falling legs carry high kinetic energy; predicts "humanoid robots will get wheels for feet"; humanoids matching human manual skill in decades = "pure fantasy". https://rodneybrooks.com/why-todays-humanoids-wont-learn-dexterity/
- BATTERY: legged electric = poor efficiency (Joule heating at low speed/high torque). Bipeds <1,000 Wh typical: Optimus V2 ~2.3 kWh ~2h; Unitree H1 864 Wh <4h static. Wheeled >1,500 Wh, >6h: Galbot G1 8h lab; A2-W 2 kWh 5h hot-swap. Pattern: bipedal 2-4h vs wheeled 5-16h [estimated synthesis]. https://manlybattery.com/humanoid-robot-battery-program-guide-2026-outlook-and-beyond/
- ENGINEERING FOCUS: wheels free budget for manipulation (Galbot); 1X EVE "bypassed the still-monumental challenge of stable, efficient bipedal locomotion". Speed: wheeled ~3x faster than comparable bipeds [unverified methodology].

### 2.2 Industry voices
- Bernt Børnich (1X): wheeled EVE first for real-world data, then NEO. 
- Vivian Chu (Diligent): wheeled Moxi = "minimum viable humanoid".
- Ali Raja (ABB MD): "I don't see... real practical applications where humanoids are bringing in a lot of value". Michael Cicco (FANUC America): wheeled arms solve factory tasks more efficiently. https://hardware.slashdot.org/story/25/05/27/2231236/robot-industry-split-over-that-humanoid-look
- Kang Ki-hyuk (Neubility VP): "essence... is not copying the human form"; Korea shifting to wheeled semi-humanoids (Neubility Billy, T-Robotics TR Works, I.L ILBOT). https://en.sedaily.com/technology/2026/03/08/wheeled-semi-humanoid-robots-gain-traction-over-bipedal (2026-03-08)
- Framing: middle category "marries the stability, speed, and pricing of an AMR with the dexterous bimanual manipulation of a humanoid torso". https://standardbots.com/blog/humanoid-robot

### 2.3 Counter-case (legs)
- Agility (2024-09-25): stairs/curbs/cable covers; legs = precise positioning system; concedes wheels optimal for heavy loads on flat floors. https://www.agilityrobotics.com/content/why-humanoid-robots-need-legs
- Melonee Wise "little teapot": static base unstable when reaching far with load; biped counterbalances.
- TAM appeal: bipeds expand into households (1X NEO); Figure fully committed. Counter-to-Brooks (Scott Walter): Roomba does 80% and still sells.

### 2.4 Use cases favoring semi-humanoids
Retail/pharmacy (Galbot); flexible mfg/logistics (AgiBot A2-W/G2, Galbot factory pilots); hospitality/F&B (ADAM, XMAN); security (EVE/Everon); healthcare/eldercare (Moxi, MiPA, Mirokai); DATA COLLECTION for embodied AI (AgiBot World 2026 on wheeled G2; π0.5 on wheeled mobile manipulators; EVE fleet data → NEO).

- ISO 10218-1/-2:2025 published early 2025: adds functional safety, cybersecurity, collab requirements; TS 15066 folded in. https://www.therobotreport.com/iso-10218-industrial-robot-safety-standard-receives-major-overhaul/
- IEEE Humanoid Study Group (Prather/ASTM) "Pathway Study" 09/2025: gaps = taxonomy, stability/fall response, HRI; 18-36 months to ratified standards. ISO/CD 25785-1 (dynamically stable mobile robots) at CD stage, pub 2026-27.
- US: ANSI/RIA R15.08-1-2020 (industrial mobile robots + mounted manipulators) fits wheeled semi-humanoids; UL 3300 added to OSHA NRTL list 2025-12-31.
- EU: Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 applies 2027-01-20 (no grace); AI/self-evolving review, cybersecurity, digital docs, HRI; dual regulation with AI Act. https://www.tuv.com/world/en/new-machinery-regulation-eu-2023-1230.html
- China: MIIT humanoid+embodied-AI standards committee (11/2025, 65 members, deputy chairs Wang Xingxing/Unitree, Peng Zhihui/AgiBot); first national humanoid standard system 03/2026 (six pillars); GB/T 39785-2021, 40013-2021, 41527-2022, 45502-2025; Shanghai governance guidelines WAIC 07/2024.
